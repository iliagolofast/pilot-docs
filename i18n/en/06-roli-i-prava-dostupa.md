---
title: "Roles and access rights"
---

PILOT has a hierarchical structure of roles and access rights. The role hierarchy separates the rights of different users.

For example, a company employs drivers, logistics specialists, managers, and a director. They all need access to information about vehicles, objects, and routes, but not to all of it at once.

A driver only needs to see their own vehicle and route. A director needs to see all vehicles, assign drivers, and configure the system for the company.

Everyone sees only what their job requires. Drivers don't get unnecessary data, and the director has everything at hand.

This is why PILOT has several types of users with different access rights.

## Roles in the user account:

1.

### Administrator

This is a user with full access to all features of the user account.

2.

### User

This is a regular user of the system. They work through the user portal, but their rights are limited. The administrator decides which actions a user can and cannot perform.

## Roles in the admin panel:

1.

### Main administrator

These are usually technical support employees who have unrestricted access to all data and settings. They can perform any action in the system, including managing roles and rights.

### 2\. Administrator

This is a PILOT partner who configures and integrates the monitoring system for various companies and fleets. Their rights are limited, but they can see all the fleets they work with, all contracts, vehicles, and all the necessary information.

### 3\. User

A partner in the administrator role can create additional users in the admin panel, for example for their employees, and assign them specific access rights within their own limits.

### Master status

In addition to roles, the system has a special user attribute: master. It is assigned when logging in through the admin panel. A user with master status has unrestricted rights in the system.
