## How to start working with PILOT

1. Open your web browser.
2. Type the platform address in the address bar: [https://pilot-gps.com/](https://pilot-gps.com/)
3. Press Enter.
4. You will see the sign-in page with fields for your login and password.
5. Enter the login and password you received from your contract administrator.
6. Choose the interface language.
7. Click “Sign in”.
