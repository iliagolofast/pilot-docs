---
title: "How to view history"
---

History is information about an object’s past movements and states, including its routes, stops and sensor readings over a chosen period.

A track is a line on the map that shows the object’s path. A track is made of points marking where the object was at different times.

### To view an object’s movement history

1. Open the “History” section in the top bar.
2. Choose an object from the list.
3. Set the time range by clicking the calendar icon.
4. Click “Search”.
5. In the search results, tick the object’s track records you want to view.

<div class="demo" data-demo="history"></div>

Once you select track records, the screen shows:

1. The list of selected tracks.
2. The player, a tool for playing back tracks and controlling the playback.
3. The “Events” window with significant events or changes related to the object.
4. Routes on the map.
5. A chart with the object’s sensor readings over the selected period.
