---
title: "How to build a report"
---

A report is the information collected about monitored objects over a chosen period.

### To view a report

1. In the main menu, open “Reports” and select an object by ticking it in the object list.
2. Choose the time range for the report by clicking the calendar icon.
3. Choose the report type.
4. Set how the report is split: by day, by week or not split into intervals.
5. Choose the grouping: by date, to see how values change over time, or by object, to compare objects with each other.
6. Choose the sensor groups the report will use.
7. Click “Build report”.

<div class="demo" data-demo="report"></div>

After you click “Build report”, the report appears in the chosen format. You can save it as CSV, Excel or PDF, or print it.
