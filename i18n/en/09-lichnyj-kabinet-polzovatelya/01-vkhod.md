## If you forgot your password

If you already have a PILOT account and forgot your password, follow these steps:

1\. In the login window, click the “Forgot your password?” button

![Logging in ](images/drex_vkhod__screen_2.png)

2\. In the window that opens, enter your username and click the “Send password” button. If the data you entered matches the data in the database, you will receive an e-mail at the address specified during registration

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_2.png)

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_3.png)

3\. Follow the link in the e-mail you received

4\. Create and enter a new password

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_4.png)

5\. Click the “Set password” button

6\. After you change the password, you will be logged in to the user account automatically

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>If you used the password recovery function by accident, delete the e-mail you received and use your previous login details.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

## Logging in to the user account with two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is an additional level of account protection.

In addition to the username and password, a one-time code is used to log in. This reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if third parties learn the password.

PILOT offers two 2FA methods:

-   TOTP authentication: through an authenticator app
    
-   E-mail authorization: with confirmation by e-mail
    

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>The contract administrator must enable the use of two-factor authentication.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

### TOTP two-factor authentication

This is an additional level of protection for your account. In addition to your regular password, you will use a temporary code to log in.

How to enable TOTP two-factor authentication:

1\. Before your first login

install a two-factor authentication app for iOS or Android

[Google Authenticator for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.authenticator2&hl=ru)             [Google Authenticator for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/google-authenticator/id388497605)

[Microsoft Authenticator for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azure.authenticator)         [Microsoft Authenticator for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/microsoft-authenticator/id983156458)

[Twilio Authy for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.authy.authy)                             [Twilio Authy for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/twilio-authy/id494168017)

2\. Find the “Two-factor authentication” section in the [“Privacy”](nastrojki-dogovora-2/privatnost.html#Двухфакторная_аутентификация) tab

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_5.png)

 3. Click the “Connect” button next to “TOTP authorization”

4\. Scan the QR code with the installed app

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_6.png)

5\. Enter the code from the app

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_7.png)

### How to log in with TOTP:

1\. Open a browser and go to [https://pilot-gps.com/](https://pilot-gps.com/)

2\. Enter your credentials: username and password

3\. Click the “Log in” button

4\. In the window that appears, enter the six-digit code from the app. To do this, open the authenticator app on your smartphone, find the six-digit code displayed, and enter it in the required field on your computer screen.

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_8.png)

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>The code refreshes every 30 seconds. If you didn't manage to enter the code before it refreshed, just wait for a new code to appear and enter it.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

5\. Click the “Log in” button

### E-mail two-factor authentication (Email 2FA)

This is an additional level of protection for your account. After you enter your username and password, the system additionally asks for a code that is sent to your personal e-mail. You can log in only after entering this code.

How to enable Email 2FA

1\. Find the “Two-factor authentication” section in the [“Privacy”](nastrojki-dogovora-2/privatnost.html#Двухфакторная_аутентификация) tab

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_5.png)

2\. Click “Connect” next to “E-mail authorization”

3\. Select a confirmed e-mail from the list

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_9.png)

4\. Click “Send code”. An e-mail with a confirmation code will be sent to the selected address![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_10.png)

5\. Enter the code from the e-mail and confirm the connection

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_11.png)

### How to log in with Email 2FA

1\. Open a browser and go to [https://pilot-gps.com/](https://pilot-gps.com/)

2\. Enter your credentials: username and password

3\. Click the “Log in” button

4\. Enter the one-time code sent to your e-mail

![](images/drex_vkhod__custom_12.png)

5\. Click “Log in”
