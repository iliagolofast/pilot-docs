title: "Navigation menu"
---

The navigation menu helps you find the tools you need and move between PILOT sections.

The menu has two levels: the main menu in the top panel and the menu of the selected section on the left side of the screen.

![Navigation menu](images/drex_navigatsionnoe_menyu_screen.png)

### Main menu

The main menu contains the main sections of the system: “Main”, “History”, “Reports”, and others.

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>The available sections depend on the modules connected to your account.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

You can change the order of sections in the top panel by dragging them with the mouse. This way you can arrange the sections to suit you: place the sections you use often at the beginning of the menu for quick access.

### Section menu

The menu on the left contains the tabs and tools of the selected section. Its contents change when you move between sections of the main menu.

<table><tbody><tr><td><div><img alt="2. " src="images/drex_navigatsionnoe_menyu_control_2.png" data-full-src="drex_navigatsionnoe_menyu_control_2.png" data-full-width="204" data-full-height="136"></div></td></tr><tr><td><div><a href="#top"></a></div></td></tr></tbody></table>

For example, after you select “Main”, the left side shows the tabs for working with objects and other features of this section.

The menu can be displayed in two views:

-   Full: icons and tab names are visible
    
-   Compact: only icons remain, freeing up more space for the map, tables, and other information
    

![Navigation menu](images/drex_navigatsionnoe_menyu_screen_2.png)

To switch between modes, click the arrow ![](images/drex_navigatsionnoe_menyu_custom.png) at the bottom of the menu.

The left arrow collapses the menu to icons, and the right arrow expands it fully, showing all names.

### How to work with the navigation menu:

1\. Select the section you need in the main menu in the top panel

2.Open the tab you need in the menu on the left

3\. On the page that opens, view the information or perform the necessary actions.
