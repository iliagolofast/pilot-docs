The right side of the top panel shows the name of the current user and the balance of the user account.

![](images/drex_imya_polzovatelya_i_balans_custom.png)

## User name

The name shows which account you are logged in to PILOT with.

 ![](images/drex_imya_polzovatelya_i_balans_custom_2.png)

## Balance

Below the user name, the current amount on the balance and the currency designation are shown. For example, 0,00 ₽ means a zero balance in rubles.

The balance helps you keep track of the funds available to pay for the service.

If you need to clarify how to top up or how charges are made, contact the specialist who services your contract.
