---
title: "Notifications"
---

In PILOT you can set up notifications about events that happen to an object: speeding, downtime, refueling, violations, entering or leaving a geofence, and other types of notifications.

To work with notifications you need:

1\. The “Notifications” module connected in your contract

2\. Notification parameters configured in advance

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>To connect the <a href="../../moduli-1/uvedomleniya/index.html"><span>“Notifications”</span></a> module, contact the contract administrator.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

To view the list of received notifications, click the notifications icon ![](images/drex_uvedomleniya_1_custom.png) in the top panel of the interface.

![](images/drex_uvedomleniya_1_custom_2.png)

You can find detailed instructions on setting up notifications in the article about the [“Notifications”](../../moduli-1/uvedomleniya/index.html) module.
