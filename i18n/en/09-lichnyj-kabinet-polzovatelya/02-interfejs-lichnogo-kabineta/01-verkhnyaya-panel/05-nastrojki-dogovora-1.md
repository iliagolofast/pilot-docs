title: "Contract settings"
---

When you click the icon ![](images/drex_nastrojki_dogovora_1_custom.png), the contract settings open, where you can configure various system parameters yourself:

![](images/drex_nastrojki_dogovora_1_custom_2.png)

— privacy and data access

— the look of interface elements and the system of measurement

— display of addresses and events on the map

— access to reports

— additional geographic data in KML/KMZ format

— object tags

— management of user accounts

— scheduled report mailing

— access tokens

![](images/drex_nastrojki_dogovora_1_custom_3.png)

You will find a detailed description of all available settings and how to change them in the [“Contract settings”](../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/index.html) section.
