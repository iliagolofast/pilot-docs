The AI assistant helps you explore PILOT's capabilities, find instructions, and get recommendations on configuring the system. The assistant opens in a separate window inside the user account.

Ask your question in your own words in the language you prefer. If you need to clarify something, continue the conversation in the same chat.

In the interface the assistant is called AI Support. In the documentation it may also be called the AI assistant or the AI bot; it is the same assistant.

## How to enable the AI assistant

For the assistant to work, AI must be enabled for your account. Technical support performs the initial setup.

After it is enabled:

1\. In the top panel of PILOT, open the additional actions menu and select “Extensions”

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_2.png)

2\. Find AI Support in the list

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_3.png)

3\. Turn on the switch to the left of the name. A green switch means the extension is enabled

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_4.png)

4\. Click the AI Support icon ![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_5.png) in the top panel to open the assistant

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_6.png)

## What the AI assistant helps with

-   Working with PILOT: setting up devices and objects, reports and templates, geofences, routes, events, notifications, users, and access rights
    
-   API and integrations: API methods, authorization, request parameters, response handling, and code examples
    
-   Pilot Extension: extension structure, required files and components, examples, and local testing
    

The assistant can suggest links to the official documentation. If the information is not enough, it will help you prepare a request to technical support.

## How to ask a question

1\. Go to the AI assistant

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_6.png)

2\. In the field at the bottom of the window, describe your question or task

3\. Click the button ![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_7.png) to send the message

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_8.png)

4\. Read the answer. Follow the links to the documentation if you want to see detailed instructions

For example, you can ask:

-   How do I set up daily report sending?
    
-   Why is the object online but no new points appear?
    
-   Show an example API request for getting the list of objects
    
-   Which files are needed to create an extension?
    

To help the assistant understand the task better, say which section you are working in, what you want to do, and at which step the difficulty arose.

If you need to refine an answer, ask a follow-up question in the same chat. For a different task, click “New chat” on the left side of the window.

![AI assistant](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_screen.png)

## Chat history

The “Recent chats” list on the left shows your conversations. Click a chat name to return to the messages and continue the discussion.

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_9.png)

### How to rename a chat

To change the name of a chat you have already worked with:

1\. Click ![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_10.png)next to the chat

2\. Select “Rename”

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_11.png)

3\. Enter the new name

**![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_12.png)**

4\. Click “Save”

### How to delete a chat

Click ![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_10.png) next to the conversation name and select “Delete”.

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_11.png)

## Rating an answer

Below the assistant's answer there are positive and negative rating buttons ![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_13.png). Use them to mark whether the answer was helpful.

![AI assistant](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_screen_2.png)

If you want to explain what should be clarified or improved:

1\. Click “Comment” below the answer

![AI assistant](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_screen_3.png)

2\. Enter your remark in the “Comment on the answer” window

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_14.png)

3\. Click “Save”

## Display settings

The arrow at the top of the left panel collapses or expands the chat list.

![AI assistant](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_screen_4.png)

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_15.png)

The theme button in the upper right corner of the chat switches between light and dark themes.

![AI assistant](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_screen_5.png)

![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_16.png)

The cross ![](images/drex_ai_pomoshchnik_custom_17.png) in the upper right corner of the window closes the AI assistant.
