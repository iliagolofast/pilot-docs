---
title: "Additional settings menu"
---

The additional settings menu is in the right part of the top panel. Click its icon ![](images/drex_menyu_dopolnitelnykh_nastroek_custom.png) to choose the interface language and theme, view news, contact support, or log out of the account.

![](images/drex_menyu_dopolnitelnykh_nastroek_custom_2.png)

### Interface theme

Choose the display mode that is comfortable for your eyes: the light theme suits daytime work, and the dark theme reduces eye strain in the evening or at night.

![](images/drex_menyu_dopolnitelnykh_nastroek_custom_3.png)

### Interface language

Choose the language that suits you.

![](images/drex_menyu_dopolnitelnykh_nastroek_custom_4.png)

### News

Click “News” to open a window with the latest publications about the platform and the company. Here you can learn about PILOT updates and new features.

![](images/drex_menyu_dopolnitelnykh_nastroek_custom_5.png)

### Technical support

If you have a question or need help, select “Tech support”.

Describe what you want to do and at which step the difficulty arose. If an error appears, attach a screenshot, which will help specialists understand the situation faster.

### Extensions

Select “Extensions” to open the list of PILOT's additional tools and enable the ones you need. For example, here you can enable AI Support if it is connected for your account.

![](images/drex_menyu_dopolnitelnykh_nastroek_custom_6.png)

![](images/drex_menyu_dopolnitelnykh_nastroek_custom_7.png)

### Logging out

Click “Log out” to end your session under the current account. This is especially useful if you work on a shared computer.
