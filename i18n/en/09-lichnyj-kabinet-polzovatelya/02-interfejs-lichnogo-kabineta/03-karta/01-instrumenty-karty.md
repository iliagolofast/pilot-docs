All map tools are gathered in one place: a special panel in the lower right corner of the screen.

![Map tools](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_screen.png)

## Zooming

There are several ways to change the map scale:

1\. Buttons on the map toolbar ![](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_custom.png) and ![](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_custom_2.png)

In the upper left part of the map there are buttons for quick zooming in and out. When you click the buttons, the map zooms in or out, but the point that was in the center stays in place.

2\. Mouse scroll wheel

Turn the mouse wheel: away from you to enlarge the map, toward you to reduce it. The cursor points to the area you want to examine in more detail.

3\. Mouse double click

Double-click the left mouse button at any point on the map, and it zooms in immediately.

The current coordinates are always visible in the lower right corner and are updated as you move the mouse cursor over the map.

## Printing the map

![](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_custom_3.png)

This function prints the current view of the map. When you click the print button, three print orientation options are offered:

1\. Landscape: the map is printed in horizontal orientation. This orientation is convenient for displaying extended areas or objects arranged horizontally.

2\. Portrait: the map is printed in vertical orientation. This format suits areas that extend vertically.

3\. Auto: the system automatically chooses the best print orientation depending on the current map view and its proportions.

## Measuring area

This tool helps you calculate the area of a section you select on the map.

How to use it:

1\. On the map, mark the corners of the shape you want to measure one after another

2\. When you finish drawing the shape, the program calculates and shows its area automatically

![](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_custom_4.png)

## Measuring the distance between points

This tool helps you easily calculate distances between any points on the map.

 How to use it:

-   Click on the map to mark the first point
    
    2\. Click again to mark the second point
    
    3\. A line showing the distance appears between the points
    
    If you need to measure several segments, keep clicking on the map to add new points. The length of each segment is shown next to it.

## Measuring distance along roads

With this tool you can determine the distance along roads between selected points on the map.

How to use it:

1\. Click the first point on the map

2\. Mark the intermediate points of the route one after another

3\. Finish building the route with a second, final click

4\. The system automatically calculates the path along existing roads. The total length and duration of the route are shown on the screen.

![](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_custom_8.png)

## Distance to the nearest vehicle

 This tool helps you quickly find the nearest vehicle and find out the distance to it from a selected point on the map.

How to use it:

1\. Click any point on the map for which you want to find the nearest vehicle

2\. The system automatically determines the nearest vehicle and displays:

— the vehicle number or object name

— the distance from the selected point to the vehicle

— the time it will take the vehicle to reach the point

![](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_custom_9.png)

## Geocoder

The geocoder lets you find a point on the map by a specified address.

How to use it:

1\. Enter an address or coordinates in the geocoder field

2\. A hint with a refined address appears below the input field

3\. Click the hint with the exact address in the drop-down list

4\. View the point you need on the map

![](images/drex_instrumenty_karty_custom_10.png)
