---
title: "Map types"
---

In professional cartography, a special term, “base map”, is used when choosing maps. For simplicity, we will use the more familiar term “map type”.

The map type is the base layer of the cartographic image used in the monitoring system to display spatial data.

PILOT offers several map types for displaying space in the user interface. The choice of a specific map type depends on your needs and preferences. Some maps are better suited for navigation, others for studying terrain or finding infrastructure objects.

To change the map, click the map selection button ![](images/drex_tipy_kart_custom.png).

![](images/drex_tipy_kart_custom_2.png)

In the menu that opens, select the map type you need. It becomes the main one and will be loaded by default when you open the system.
