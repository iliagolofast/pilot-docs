title: "Map layers and additional functions"
---

In the upper right part of the map there are additional functions that help display more contextual information on the map: connect custom layers, view streets, assess road conditions, and see GPS jamming zones.

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom.png)

In this article:

• [Object layers](sloi-i-dopolnitelnye-funktsii-karty.html#Слои_объектов): display of additional geographic data from KML/KMZ files

• [Street view](sloi-i-dopolnitelnye-funktsii-karty.html#Просмотр_улиц): go to panoramic images of the area

• [Traffic view](sloi-i-dopolnitelnye-funktsii-karty.html#Просмотр_пробок): display of road congestion on the map

• [GPS jamming](sloi-i-dopolnitelnye-funktsii-karty.html#Глушение_GPS-сигнала): display of zones with different levels of GPS interference

## Object layers

The “Object layers” function turns the display of additional geographic data from uploaded KML/KMZ files on the main map on and off.

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom_2.png)

How to use the function:

1\. Find the “Object layers” panel in the upper right corner of the map

2\. Expand the list of available layers and select the checkboxes next to the layers you want to see

You can enable several layers at the same time or turn off the additional data completely, leaving only the main map.

To learn how to upload and work with KML/KMZ files, see the [“KML settings”](../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/nastrojki-kml.html) section.

## Street view

The street view function lets you see panoramic photos of the area.

Three viewing options are available:

-   GSV: panoramic images from Google with high image quality and wide territory coverage
    
-   YR: street panoramas from “Yandex”. They have good coverage in Russia and some neighboring countries
    
-   OSC: panoramas created by a community of users. They may contain more recent images in some areas
    

To enable street view:

1\. Select the checkbox for this option in the upper right part of the map

2\. Drag the yellow figure on the map to the place you need

3\. Choose the viewing option you want: GSV, YR, or OSC

4\. After you choose one of the options, the corresponding panorama service opens, where you can view interactive panoramas of the selected area.

![Map layers and additional functions](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_screen.png)

## Traffic view

The traffic view function shows current road congestion using color indication:

-   Green: free-flowing traffic
    
-   Yellow: moderate congestion
    
-   Red: heavy traffic jams
    

To turn on traffic display, select the checkbox for this option in the upper right part of the map.

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom_3.png)

## GPS jamming

The “GPS jamming” function shows zones where GPS signal interference was recorded on the selected date. This data helps you assess areas where object coordinates may be determined less accurately because of an unstable GPS signal.

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom_4.png)

The function is available on the map as a separate checkbox in the upper right part of the map. After you enable it, colored cells with the interference level are displayed on the map.

![Map layers and additional functions](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_screen_2.png)

A legend is displayed in the lower left corner:

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom_5.png) green: low interference level, 0–2%

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom_6.png) yellow: medium interference level, 2–10%

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom_7.png) red: high interference level, more than 10%

You can also select the date for which to display data. To do this, use the “Date” field in the layer panel.

![](images/drex_sloi_i_dopolnitelnye_funktsii_karty_custom_8.png)
