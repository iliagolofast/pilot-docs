## Setting up e-mail

In PILOT, e-mail is used to receive reports and notifications.

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_2.png)

### How to add an e-mail address:

1\. In the “Personal settings” tab, find the “Users' e-mail addresses” section

2\. Click the “Add Email” button ![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_3.png)

3\. Enter a valid e-mail address

4\. Click the “Save” button

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>To receive reports and notifications, you must confirm your e-mail address. The system will send you information only after confirmation.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

E-mail statuses:

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_4.png) — e-mail confirmed

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_5.png) — e-mail not confirmed

### How to confirm your e-mail address:

1\. Click the “Send confirmation e-mail” button ![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_6.png)

2\. Open your mailbox

3\. Find the e-mail from the PILOT system

4\. Follow the link in the e-mail

5\. The e-mail address will be confirmed automatically

The “Update email list” button ![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_7.png) updates the statuses of the added e-mail addresses.

## Changing the password

### How to change the password in your account:

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_8.png)

1\. In the “Privacy” tab, find the “Change password” section

2\. Come up with a new strong password

3\. Enter the new password

4\. Repeat the new password in the next field

5\. Click the “Save password” button

If you select the “Send by e-mail” checkbox, an e-mail with the new password will be sent to the e-mail address specified in your account.

## IP filtering protection

IP filtering is a technology that gives access to the account only to allowed IP addresses.

### How to enable IP address filtering:

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_9.png)

1\. In the “Privacy” tab, find the “IP address filtering” section

2\. Select the “IP address filtering” checkbox

3\. Enter the IP addresses from which you allow logging in to your account. For example, the addresses of your work and home computers.

4\. Click “Save”

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div><span>When you enable the function, your current IP address is added automatically so that you don't lose access.</span></div></td></tr></tbody></table>

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>&nbsp;You will be able to log in to the account only from the specified IP addresses.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

If you want to turn this function off, just clear the checkbox and save the changes.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is an additional level of account protection.

In addition to the username and password, a one-time code is used to log in. This reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if third parties learn the password.

PILOT offers two 2FA methods:

-   TOTP authentication: through an authenticator app
    
-   E-mail authorization: with confirmation by e-mail
    

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_10.png)

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>&nbsp;The contract administrator must enable the use of two-factor authentication.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

### TOTP two-factor authentication

This is an additional level of protection for your account. In addition to your regular password, you will use a temporary code to log in.

How to enable TOTP two-factor authentication:

1\. Before your first login

install a two-factor authentication app for iOS or Android

[Google Authenticator for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.authenticator2&hl=ru)             [Google Authenticator for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/google-authenticator/id388497605)

[Microsoft Authenticator for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azure.authenticator)         [Microsoft Authenticator for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/microsoft-authenticator/id983156458)

[Twilio Authy for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.authy.authy)                             [Twilio Authy for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/twilio-authy/id494168017)

2\. In the “Privacy” tab, find the “Two-factor authentication” section

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_10.png)

 3. Click the “Connect” button next to “TOTP authorization”

4\. Scan the QR code with the installed app

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_11.png)

5\. Enter the code from the app

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_12.png)

### How to log in with TOTP:

1\. Open a browser and go to [https://pilot-gps.com/](https://pilot-gps.com/)

2\. Enter your credentials: username and password

3\. Click the “Log in” button

4\. In the window that appears, enter the six-digit code from the app. To do this, open the authenticator app on your smartphone, find the six-digit code displayed, and enter it in the required field on your computer screen.

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_13.png)

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>The code refreshes every 30 seconds. If you didn't manage to enter the code before it refreshed, just wait for a new code to appear and enter it.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

5\. Click the “Log in” button

### E-mail two-factor authentication (Email 2FA)

This is an additional level of protection for your account. After you enter your username and password, the system additionally asks for a code that is sent to your personal e-mail. You can log in only after entering this code.

How to enable Email 2FA:

1\. In the “Privacy” tab, find the “Two-factor authentication” section

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_10.png)

2\. Click “Connect” next to “E-mail authorization”

3\. Select a confirmed e-mail from the list

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_14.png)

4\. Click “Send code”. An e-mail with a confirmation code will be sent to the selected address![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_15.png)

5\. Enter the code from the e-mail and confirm the connection

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_16.png)

### How to log in with Email 2FA

1\. Open a browser and go to [https://pilot-gps.com/](https://pilot-gps.com/)

2\. Enter your credentials: username and password

3\. Click the “Log in” button

4\. Enter the one-time code sent to your e-mail

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_17.png)

5\. Click “Log in”

### How to turn off 2FA

Go to the “Two-factor authentication” section and click “Disable” next to the active method.

![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_18.png)

## Reset cache

Sometimes the system may not show all current information. This happens because the browser stores old data.

To make everything work correctly again:

1\. In the upper right corner of the “Privacy” tab, find the “Reset cache” button ![](images/drex_privatnost_custom_20.png)

2\. Click it, and the system clears the old data and loads current data

After that, everything will be displayed correctly again.
