---
title: "Climate chambers"
---

The “Climate chambers” tab configures the user's access to the data of the [“Climate”](../../../moduli-1/klimat/index.html) module. You can grant access to the whole mode or only to individual objects and sites.

The module has two modes:

![](images/drex_klimaticheskie_kamery_custom.png)

-   NORMAL (Standard): the standard mode for working with climate sensors. It shows connected objects and sensors, their current readings, update time, and charts
    

![](images/drex_klimaticheskie_kamery_custom_2.png)

-   VELAVU: a mode for equipment, sites, and premises loaded from the Velavu platform. In this mode you can view the structure of sites, buildings, and floors, as well as the location of devices and RFID tags on the map or plan.
    

![](images/drex_klimaticheskie_kamery_custom_3.png)

### To grant access:

1\. Open the user's card

2\. Go to the “Climate chambers” tab

3\. Select the checkbox next to the mode you need

-   NORMAL: access to the module's standard data
    
-   VELAVU: access to the Velavu integration data
    

![](images/drex_klimaticheskie_kamery_custom_4.png)

4\. To grant access only to individual data, expand the mode you need and select the checkboxes next to the corresponding elements:

-   in NORMAL mode: groups or objects with climate sensors
    
-   in VELAVU mode: sites, buildings, or individual floors
    

![](images/drex_klimaticheskie_kamery_custom_5.png)

5\. Click “Save”
