The “Vehicles” tab configures the user's access to monitored objects. The user will see in PILOT only the objects selected here.

![](images/drex_transport_1_custom.png)

### To grant access:

1\. Open the user's card

2\. Go to the “Vehicles” tab

3\. Expand the folder you need

4\. Select the checkboxes next to the objects that should be available to the user. To select all objects in a folder, select the checkbox next to its name

5\. Click “Save”.

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>New objects are not assigned to users automatically. After creating or importing an object, open the cards of the users you need and grant them access manually. This also applies to objects received from a mapping contract.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

To close a user's access to an object, clear the corresponding checkbox and save the changes.
