title: "Drivers"
---

The “Drivers” tab is available if the corresponding [module](../../../moduli-1/voditeli-3/index.html) is connected to the account. Here you configure the user's access to driver data. The user will be able to see and use only the selected records.

![](images/drex_voditeli_1_custom.png)

### To grant access:

1\. Open the user's card

2\. Go to the “Drivers” tab

3\. Expand the group you need

4\. Select the checkboxes next to the drivers available to the user. To select the whole group, select the checkbox next to its name

5\. Click “Save”

New drivers are not assigned to the user automatically. After adding a driver, grant access to them in the “Drivers” tab if necessary.
