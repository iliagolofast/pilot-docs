---
title: "Object tags"
---

The “Object tags” tab configures the user's access to tags used to classify and quickly search for objects. The user will be able to see and use only the selected tags.

![](images/drex_tegi_obekta_custom.png)

### To grant access:

1\. Open the user's card

2\. Go to the “Object tags” tab

3\. Select the checkboxes next to the tags you need

4\. Click “Save”

To close access to a tag, clear the corresponding checkbox and save the changes.
