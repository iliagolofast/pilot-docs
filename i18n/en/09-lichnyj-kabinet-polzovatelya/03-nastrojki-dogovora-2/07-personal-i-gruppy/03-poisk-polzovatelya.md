---
title: "Searching for a user"
---

In the “Staff and groups” tab you can find a user by login, account name, or e-mail address.

![](images/drex_poisk_polzovatelya_custom.png)

1\. Enter part of the value in the search field in the upper right corner

2\. The list updates automatically and shows only matching records

![Searching for a user](images/drex_poisk_polzovatelya_screen.png)

You can type your query in uppercase or lowercase letters; case does not affect the search results.

To show all users again, clear the field with the icon ![](images/drex_poisk_polzovatelya_custom_2.png).

## Account owner

The account owner is highlighted in green in the user list, so they can be told apart from the other users of the contract.

![](images/drex_poisk_polzovatelya_custom_3.png)

The owner cannot be deleted through the PILOT interface. If you need to assign another owner, contact the administrator or technical support.
