---
title: "Assigning reports"
---

For each user you can choose the reports that will be available to them in PILOT.

1\. Open the “Staff and groups” tab

2\. Find the user you need

![](images/drex_naznachenie_otchyetov_custom.png)

3\. Click the report list icon ![](images/drex_naznachenie_otchyetov_custom_2.png) in their row

4\. In the “User rights to reports” window, select the checkboxes next to the reports you need

![](images/drex_naznachenie_otchyetov_custom_3.png)

5\. Click “Save”

To find a report quickly, enter part of its name in the search field ![](images/drex_naznachenie_otchyetov_custom_4.png).

![](images/drex_naznachenie_otchyetov_custom_5.png)

The “Select all” button assigns all available reports, and “Clear all” removes all assignments.

![Assigning reports](images/drex_naznachenie_otchyetov_screen.png)

If you clear the checkbox for a report and save the changes, it stops being displayed for the user.
