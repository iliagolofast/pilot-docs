In the “Staff and groups” tab you can create employee accounts and configure their access to PILOT. For each user you can define the available objects, reports, modules, and actions in the system.

![](images/drex_personal_i_gruppy_custom.png)

This section describes:

— [creating and editing users](sozdanie-polzovatelya-1.html)

— [configuring the user card](kartochka-polzovatelya/index.html)

— [assigning access rights](kartochka-polzovatelya/prava.html)

— [creating and applying rights templates](kartochka-polzovatelya/prava.html#Шаблоны_прав)

— [searching for users](poisk-polzovatelya.html)

— [viewing login history](istoriya-vkhodov.html)

— [assigning available reports](naznachenie-otchyetov.html)

— [setting up IP filtering](ip-filtratsiya-dlya-polzovatelya.html)

— [blocking and unblocking accounts](blokirovka-i-razblokirovka-polzovatelya.html)

The available settings depend on the connected modules and the rights of the current user.

 An administrator can create users and manage their access.
