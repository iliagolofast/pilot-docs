---
title: "Display settings template"
---

A template lets you transfer display settings from one account to another. This is convenient when you create accounts with similar parameters or configure several partner accounts: you can export the required parameters to a file and then apply them again.

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>Creating and uploading templates is available only to users with master rights.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

The buttons for exporting and uploading display settings templates are available on any tab of “Contract settings”.

![Display settings template](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_screen.png)

A template can include settings from the [“Personalization”](personalizatsiya.html) and [“Map and geocoding”](karta-i-geokoding.html) tabs, including:

— the list of available reports

— the system of units

— general display parameters

— the address format

— display of events on the map in history

— the contents of pop-up hints

## Exporting a template

1\. Open “Contract settings” and go to any tab, for example “Staff and groups”

2\. Click the “Download settings” icon ![](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_custom.png)

![Display settings template](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_screen_2.png)

3\. Select the checkboxes for the parameters to include in the template

![](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_custom_2.png)

4\. Click “Export”

The template file is saved to your computer. You can use it to transfer the selected settings to another account.

## Uploading a template

Before uploading, make sure the account you want to apply the settings to is open.

1\. Open “Contract settings” and go to any tab, for example “Staff and groups”

2\. Click the “Upload template” icon ![](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_custom_3.png)

![](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_custom_4.png)

3\. Click “Browse” and select the previously saved file

![Display settings template](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_screen_3.png)

4\. Click “Upload”

![](images/drex_shablon_nastroek_otobrazheniya_custom_5.png)

5\. When the upload is complete, refresh the page

The parameters included in the template are applied to the selected account.
