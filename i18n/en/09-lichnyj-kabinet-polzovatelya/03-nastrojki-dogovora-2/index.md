To open the contract settings, click the icon ![](images/drex_nastrojki_dogovora_2_custom.png) on the top panel.

-   [KML settings](nastrojki-kml.html)

-   [Staff and groups](personal-i-gruppy/index.html)
    
-   [Report scheduler](planirovshchik-otchyetov.html)
