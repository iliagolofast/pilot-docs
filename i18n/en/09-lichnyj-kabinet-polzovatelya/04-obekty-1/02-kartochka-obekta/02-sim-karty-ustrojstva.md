The “SIM cards” tab (SIM cards) stores information about the SIM cards installed in the main and additional devices of the object. The availability of the tab depends on the user's role and rights. Only a user with master rights can add and change this information.

![](images/drex_sim_karty_ustrojstva_custom.png)

The data is grouped by hardware:

-   Main device — the main terminal of the object
    
-   Additional device — other hardware added to the object card
    

For each SIM card, fill in the following fields:

-   Slot — the number of the connector or position of the SIM card in the device. If the hardware supports one SIM card, enter 1. For devices with several SIM cards, select the number of the corresponding slot.
    
-   MSISDN — the phone number assigned to the SIM card by the carrier. The number length may differ depending on the country and carrier, so enter it in the format provided by the carrier
    
-   ICCID — the unique serial number of the SIM card. It usually has 19 or 20 digits and is printed on the SIM card itself, its packaging, or in the carrier's account
    
-   SIM provider — the carrier that serves the SIM card
    

## How to add a SIM card

1\. Open the “SIM cards” tab

2\. Find the main or additional device in which the SIM card is installed

![](images/drex_sim_karty_ustrojstva_custom_2.png)

3\. Fill in the information about the first SIM card

4\. If the device supports several SIM cards, click the plus icon next to its name

5\. For each added row, select the slot number and enter the data of the corresponding SIM card

![](images/drex_sim_karty_ustrojstva_custom_3.png)

6\. Click “Save”

If an additional device is connected to the object, the information about its SIM cards is filled in separately in the “Additional device” block.

![](images/drex_sim_karty_ustrojstva_custom.png)

To delete an added row, click the trash can icon ![](images/drex_sim_karty_ustrojstva_custom_4.png). The “Reset” button cancels changes that have not been saved yet.
