On the “Info” tab, fill in the vehicle details:

![](images/drex_informatsiya_custom.png)

On the “Info” tab, fill in the vehicle details:                                                

-   Year of manufacture of the vehicle
    
-   VIN
    
-   Engine number
    
-   Engine displacement in liters. You can select the value with the arrows in the field or enter it manually
    
-   Engine power in horsepower
    
-   Total number of seats and number of passenger seats in the vehicle
    
-   Select how to specify the validity period of your vehicle registration certificate:
    
    “Valid until” — enter the exact expiration date of the document
    
    “Issue date” — enter the date the document was issued
    
-   Series and number of the registration certificate and its validity period
    
-   Insurance policy number and its validity period
    
    To specify the validity period of the registration certificate or the insurance policy, click the calendar icon ![](images/drex_informatsiya_custom_2.png) and select a date.
    

After you fill in the parameters on this tab, do not forget to click “Save” to apply your changes.

### How to set up a reminder for document deadlines:

-   Click the icon ![](images/drex_informatsiya_custom_3.png) to set up a reminder about the expiration of the insurance or the registration certificate.
    

![](images/drex_informatsiya_custom_4.png)

2\. In the window that opens, enter a name for the reminder

3\. In the “Date” field, enter the end of the document's validity period

4\. Select the repeat interval — how often to send reminders

5\. Specify how many days before the document expires the reminder should be sent

6\. Specify how you want to receive the reminder

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>To receive reminders by e-mail, confirm your e-mail in <a href="../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/privatnost.html#Электронная_почта"><span>“Privacy”</span></a>.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

7\. Write the reminder text:

-   Enter the reminder subject — what exactly the notification should be about
    
-   Write a title for your reminder — what appears at the top of the message
    
-   Create the reminder text with automatic data substitution
    

To do this, use variables:    

%current\_time% — current time 

%reminder\_name% — reminder name

%current\_moto% — current mileage

%current\_mil% — current mileage in miles

%agent\_id% — account number

%vehicle\_name% — vehicle name

%driver\_name% — driver name

These variables are automatically replaced with real data when the message is sent.

For example: The insurance of %vehicle\_name% expires on %current\_time% 

-   To keep receiving notifications after the specified period ends, select the “Repeat the procedure when maintenance is completed” option
    

8\. Click “Save” — the reminder is created.
