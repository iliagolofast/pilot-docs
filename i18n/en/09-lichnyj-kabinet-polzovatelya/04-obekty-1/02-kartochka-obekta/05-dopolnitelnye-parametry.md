---
title: "Additional parameters"
---

On this tab you can fill in additional information about the object. Which parameter fields are available is determined by the administrator of your contract.  The administrator manages the fields in the account of the [admin panel](../../../panel-administratora/dogovor/redaktor-polej-transportnogo-sredstva.html).

After the administrator configures the additional fields, you can see and fill them in on this tab. If you later need to display more information about the object, the administrator will add the corresponding fields.

This is an example of the tab with additional fields in the user interface:

![](images/drex_dopolnitelnye_parametry_custom.png)

After you fill in the parameters on this tab, do not forget to click “Save” to apply your changes.
