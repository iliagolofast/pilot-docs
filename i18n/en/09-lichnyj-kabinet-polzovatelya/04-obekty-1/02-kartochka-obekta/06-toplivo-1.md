title: "Fuel"
---

On this tab you can specify parameters that help you manage fuel consumption.

![](images/drex_toplivo_1_custom.png)

-   Summer and winter fuel consumption — specify fuel consumption for winter and summer separately to understand which changes in consumption are caused by seasonal factors and not only by how the vehicle is used. 
    
-   Period start — use the calendar ![](images/drex_toplivo_1_custom_2.png)to select the start dates of the summer and winter periods for calculating fuel consumption by season.
    
-   Fuel consumption per engine hour — this parameter tracks fuel consumption not only by distance traveled but also by engine running time. For example, it is specified for equipment that often works in one place: construction and agricultural machinery or generators.
    
-   Idle fuel consumption
    
-   Fuel type: diesel, AI-80, AI-92, AI-95, AI-98, gas
    
-   Fuel tank capacity — specify the parameter for tracking fuel consumption and level
    
-   Fuel cost
    

After you fill in the parameters on this tab, do not forget to click “Save” to apply your changes.
