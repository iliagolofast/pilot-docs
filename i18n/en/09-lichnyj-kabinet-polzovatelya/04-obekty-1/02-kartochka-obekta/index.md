---
title: "Object card"
---

The object card stores information about the vehicle and the settings of the hardware associated with it. Here you can change the main characteristics of the object, configure the device and its connection, add SIM cards, add fuel parameters, and other data.

To open the object card, double-click the object in the list, or open the object's context menu and select “Edit”.

![](images/drex_kartochka_obekta_custom.png)

The card has several tabs:

-   [“Main”](osnovnye-nastrojki-obekta.html) — vehicle information, its image, the device, and connection parameters
    
-   [“SIM cards”](sim-karty-ustrojstva.html) — slot numbers, MSISDN, ICCID, and carriers of the SIM cards of the main and additional devices
    
-   [“Info”](informatsiya.html) — additional information about the object
    
-   [“Settings”](nastrojki-1.html) — operating and display parameters of the object
    
-   [“Additional parameters”](dopolnitelnye-parametry.html) — additional characteristics and settings
    
-   [“Fuel”](toplivo-1.html) — parameters related to fuel accounting
    
-   [“Files”](fajly.html) — documents and other files of the object
    

![](images/drex_kartochka_obekta_custom_2.png)
