---
title: "Login summary"
---

The “Login summary” report contains summarized information about users' logins for the selected period. It lets you find out the number of logins of each user, the time of the last login, and the total and average session duration.

![](images/drex_svodka_po_vkhodu_v_sistemu_custom.png)

### What the report displays

-   Name — the user's display name
    
-   Email — the user's e-mail address
    
-   Last login — the date and time of the user's last login in the specified period
    
-   No logins — the number of registered logins of the user

-   Total duration — the total duration of all of the user's sessions
    
-   Avg Duration — the average duration of one session
