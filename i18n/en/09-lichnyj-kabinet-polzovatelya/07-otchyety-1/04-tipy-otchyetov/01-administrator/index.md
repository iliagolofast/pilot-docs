---
title: "Administrator"
---

This section contains reports related to user management and monitoring of users' actions in the system. They help you track account changes, logins, password resets, blocks, and other user actions.

With the reports you can:

— view the history of administrative actions

— monitor the creation, modification, disabling, and blocking of accounts

— track successful and failed login attempts

— analyze the number and duration of user sessions

— find out which user, under which login or with which role, performed an action
