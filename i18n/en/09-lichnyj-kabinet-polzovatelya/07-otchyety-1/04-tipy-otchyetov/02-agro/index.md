title: "Agro"
---

This section contains reports related to the [“Agro”](../../../moduli-1/agro.html) Module.

With these Reports you can:

— monitor the progress of field work

— analyze the duration and distance of completed work

— calculate the area treated based on the working width of the equipment

— track fuel and working fluid consumption

— compare results across different fields and Geofences

— assess the efficiency of agricultural machinery use
