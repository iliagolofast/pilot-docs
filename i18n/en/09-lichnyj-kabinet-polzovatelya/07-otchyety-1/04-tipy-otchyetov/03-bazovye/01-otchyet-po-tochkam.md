---
title: "Points Report"
---

The Points Report contains information about the location and status of vehicles at each recorded route point. Use it to trace vehicle movement and find out its speed, heading, coordinates, and status at a specific moment.

Before generating the report, you need to select:

— a period of no more than one day

— no more than three vehicles

A separate data block is generated for each selected vehicle. Each row of the report corresponds to one point received from the GPS tracker.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_tochkam_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   № — sequence number of the point since the start of the selected period
    
-   GPS date/time — date and time the point was recorded by the GPS tracker
    
-   Driver — name of the Driver assigned to the vehicle
    
-   Speed — vehicle speed at the moment the point was recorded
    
-   Heading — direction of vehicle movement in degrees
    
-   Distance — distance traveled during the interval between points
    
-   Travel time — duration of movement during the corresponding interval
    
-   Latitude — geographic latitude of the point
    
-   Longitude — geographic longitude of the point
    
-   Place — address or description of the vehicle location
    
-   Status — vehicle state at the moment the point was recorded, for example “Stop”
    
-   Geofence — name of the Geofence the vehicle was in
    
-   Received date/time — date and time the point was received by the system from the GPS tracker
