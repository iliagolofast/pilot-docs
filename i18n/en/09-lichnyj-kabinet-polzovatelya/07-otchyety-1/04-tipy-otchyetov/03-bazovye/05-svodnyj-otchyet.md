---
title: "Summary Report"
---

The Summary Report contains key information about the operation of the selected vehicles over the specified period. Use it to check mileage and travel time, refuels and fuel drains, speeding, and parking.

![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchyet_custom.png)

Before generating the report, select the period and the vehicles, and check the boxes for the data you want to include:

-   Refuels
    
-   Drains
    
-   Speed
    
-   Parking
    
-   Distance
    

You can select all parameters or only the ones you need. A separate data block is generated for each selected vehicle.

![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchyet_custom_2.png)

### What the report shows

The beginning of the block shows the Object — the name of the vehicle the figures are calculated for.

### Distance

If the “Distance” box is checked, the following is shown:

-   Mileage — distance traveled by the vehicle over the selected period
    
-   Travel time — total duration of movement
    
-   Max. speed — maximum recorded speed
    
-   Average speed — average travel speed
    

### Refuels

If the “Refuels” box is checked, recorded increases in fuel level are shown:

-   date and time of the refuel
    
-   volume of fuel added, in liters
    

### Drains

If the “Drains” box is checked, recorded decreases in fuel level are shown:

-   date and time of the drain
    
-   volume of fuel drained, in liters
    

Refuel and drain data is shown when a fuel level sensor is present and correctly configured.

### Speed

If the “Speed” box is checked, the “Speeding” section shows cases of exceeding the set speed:

-   date and time the speeding began
    
-   recorded speed
    
-   duration of the speeding in seconds
    

### Parking

If the “Parking” box is checked, the following is shown:

-   date and time the parking began
    
-   parking duration
