---
title: "Vehicle Operation"
---

The “Vehicle Operation” report contains summary indicators of vehicle operation for the selected period. Use it to assess mileage and travel time and to identify trips outside working hours, speed limit violations, travel outside Geofences, and idling.

Data can be split by day. Each row corresponds to one vehicle for the specified time interval. The “Total” row shows the summary figures for all vehicles.

![](images/drex_rabota_transporta_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   № — row sequence number
    
-   Vehicle — name or number of the monitored Object
    
-   Total distance (km) — total mileage for the reporting period
    
-   Travel time (hh:mm) — total duration of movement
    
-   Distance traveled outside working hours (km) — distance traveled outside the set working hours
    
-   Number of speed limit violations — number of recorded speeding cases
    
-   Distance outside Geofences (km) — distance traveled outside monitored Geofences
    
-   Time outside Geofences — time the vehicle spent outside Geofences
    
-   Fuel consumption (norm) (l) — calculated fuel consumption according to the set norm
    
-   Idling during working hours — total duration of idling within the set working hours
