---
title: "Detailed Trips Report"
---

The Detailed Report shows how a vehicle moved over the selected period. It lets you view the time and route of Trips, the vehicle location, Driver data, and travel speed.

![](images/drex_podrobnyj_otchyet_po_poezdkam_custom.png)

The report can be generated for only one vehicle at a time. The selected period must not exceed three days.

### What the report shows

-   Object — the vehicle the report was generated for
    
-   Date and time — date and time of the Event
    
-   Location — the address where the vehicle was at the given moment
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle during the Trip
    
-   Travel speed — vehicle speed at the given route point
    
-   Max. speed — maximum speed on the corresponding segment
    
-   Status — Trip type, for example, Trip start or Parking end
    

For each Trip, summary information is also provided:

-   Trip number
    
-   duration
    
-   average speed
    
-   maximum speed
    
-   distance traveled
