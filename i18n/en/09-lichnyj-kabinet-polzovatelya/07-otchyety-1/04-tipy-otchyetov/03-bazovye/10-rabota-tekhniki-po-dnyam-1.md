---
title: "Equipment Operation by Day"
---

The “Equipment Operation by Day” report is designed to monitor the daily operation of monitored Objects. Use it to assess the duration of movement and Parking, working time on the line, ignition-on time, daily Mileage, and average speed.

![](images/drex_rabota_tekhniki_po_dnyam_1_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Object — name or number of the monitored Object;
    
-   Time moving — total duration of the Object’s movement during the day. The minimum speed at which an Object is considered to be moving is set in its [settings](../../../obekty-1/kartochka-obekta/index.html#Настройки_объекта)
    
-   Parking time — total time during which the Object was not moving; set in the Object card on the [“Settings”](../../../obekty-1/kartochka-obekta/index.html#Настройки_объекта) tab
    
-   Working time on the line — the sum of moving time and stop time during working hours
    
-   Stop time during working hours— total duration of stops included in working time. The minimum duration of such a stop is determined by the “Min. parking time” parameter in the Object [settings](../../../obekty-1/kartochka-obekta/index.html#Настройки_объекта)
    
-   Ignition on — total time the ignition was on
    
-   Time off duty — the part of the day not included in working time on the line. Calculated as the difference between 24 hours and working time;
    
-   Distance — the Object’s Mileage for the day
    
-   Avg. speed — the Object’s average speed for the day
