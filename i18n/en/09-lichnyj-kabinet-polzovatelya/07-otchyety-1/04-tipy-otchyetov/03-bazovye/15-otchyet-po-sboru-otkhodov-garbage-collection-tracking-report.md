---
title: "Waste Collection Report (Garbage Collection Tracking Report)"
---

The **Garbage Collection Tracking Report** contains information about garbage truck visits to waste collection points over the specified period. Use it to monitor the completion of assigned routes, check vehicle arrival and departure times, and identify the collection points actually visited.

Data is grouped by vehicle and date. For each vehicle, the list of collection points visited during the corresponding day is shown.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_sboru_otkhodov__garbage_collection_tracking_report__custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Vehicle ID — name, registration, or fleet number of the vehicle

• Vehicle Type — type of vehicle

• Driver Name — the Driver assigned to the vehicle

• Assigned Route — the route assigned to the vehicle

• Notes — additional notes

• Collection Point — name or full address of the waste collection point

• Location — the actual location of the visited point; shown as a link together with geographic coordinates

• Arrival — date and time the vehicle arrived at the collection point

• Departure — date and time the vehicle left the collection point

• Comment — additional information about the visit to the collection point

The links in the “Location” column take you to the corresponding point on the Map. Comparing the Collection Point with the actual location helps you check whether the required point was actually visited.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_sboru_otkhodov__garbage_collection_tracking_report__custom_2.png)

The Report helps you:

— monitor compliance with waste collection routes

— check the sequence in which points were visited

— determine how long the vehicle spent at each point

— identify missed points or deviations from the assigned route

— analyze vehicle and Driver performance by day
