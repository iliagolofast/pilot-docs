---
title: "Iman Group Refuel Report"
---

The Report contains information about changes in fuel level for the selected vehicles over the specified period. In addition to refuels, the report also shows fuel drains.

Use it to find out when and where the fuel level changed, determine the volume of a refuel or drain, and compare the Sensor readings at the beginning and end of the Event.

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_zapravkakh_iman_group_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Object — name or number of the vehicle
    
-   Date — date and time the fuel Event was recorded
    
-   Fuel start — fuel level at the beginning of the Event
    
-   Refuel — volume of the fuel level change. For a refuel, this is the amount of fuel added; for a drain, the amount drained
    
-   Fuel end — fuel level after the Event ended
    
-   Type — kind of fuel Event:
    
-   Refuel — the fuel level increased
    
-   Drain — the fuel level decreased
    
-   Geofence — the Geofence where the Event occurred
    
-   Address — address or description of the refuel or drain location
    

The data is generated from fuel level sensor readings. For Events to be displayed correctly, the Sensor must be installed and configured for the corresponding vehicle.
