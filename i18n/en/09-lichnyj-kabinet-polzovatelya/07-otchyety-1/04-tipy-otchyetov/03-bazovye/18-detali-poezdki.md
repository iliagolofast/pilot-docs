---
title: "Trip Details"
---

The “Trip Details” report contains detailed information about the movement and state of the selected Object over the specified period. Use it to trace the route by individual GPS points, find out the Object’s speed, heading, coordinates, and location, and check the readings of connected Sensors.

Before generating the report, you need to select a period of no more than three days and no more than three monitored Objects.

A separate data block is generated for each selected Object. Each row corresponds to one point recorded by the GPS tracker.

# ![](images/drex_detali_poezdki_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   № — sequence number of the point
    
-   GPS date/time — date and time the point was recorded by the GPS tracker
    
-   Speed — the Object’s speed at the moment the point was recorded
    
-   Heading — direction of the Object’s movement in degrees
    
-   Distance — distance traveled during the interval between points
    
-   Travel time — duration of movement during the corresponding interval
    
-   Latitude — geographic latitude of the point
    
-   Longitude — geographic longitude of the point
    
-   Place — address or description of the Object’s location at the moment the point was recorded
    
-   Status — state of the Object, for example “Stop” or “Moving”
    
-   Geofence — name of the Geofence the Object was in
    
-   Received date/time — date and time the data was received by the system from the GPS tracker
    
-   Temperature — temperature Sensor reading
    
-   Humidity sensor — humidity Sensor reading
    
-   Battery — battery charge level
    
-   Door — door state, for example, open or closed
    

The columns with temperature, humidity, charge level, and door state are filled in when the corresponding Sensors are present and correctly configured.
