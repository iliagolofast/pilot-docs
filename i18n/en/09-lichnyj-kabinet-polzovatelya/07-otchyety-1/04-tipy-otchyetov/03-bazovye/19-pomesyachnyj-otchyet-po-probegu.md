---
title: "Monthly Mileage Report"
---

The Monthly Mileage Report contains information about the distance traveled by the selected vehicles in each month of the reporting period. Use it to compare Mileage by month and find out the total Mileage of each vehicle over the entire selected period.

Before generating the report, select the vehicles and a period that may cover one or more months. The system automatically splits the data by calendar month and creates a separate column for each month.

![](images/drex_pomesyachnyj_otchyet_po_probegu__custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Vehicle — name or number of the monitored Object
    
-   Month — the vehicle’s Mileage for the corresponding month
    
-   Total — the vehicle’s total Mileage for the entire selected period.
