---
title: "Mileage Report"
---

The Mileage Report contains information about the Trips of the selected vehicles over the specified period. Use it to find out the time and place each Trip started and ended, its duration, the distance traveled, and the average and maximum speed.

The “Total” row shows the total duration, total distance, and maximum speed for the corresponding Object.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Object — name or number of the monitored Object
    
-   Begin — date and time the Trip started
    
-   End — date and time the Trip ended
    
-   Start — address or description of the Trip start location
    
-   Stop — address or description of the Trip end location
    

The addresses in the “Start” and “Stop” columns are shown as links that take you to the corresponding location.

-   Duration — Trip duration, calculated as the difference between the start and end times
    
-   Distance — the vehicle’s Mileage for the Trip
    
-   Avg. speed — average travel speed during the Trip
    
-   Max. speed — maximum speed recorded during the Trip
    

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu_custom_2.png)

### Fuel data

Before generating the report, you can check the “Fuel” box.

![Mileage Report](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu_screen.png)

In this case, fuel level and consumption information for each Trip is added to the table:

-   Fuel level at start — fuel Sensor reading at the start of the Trip
    
-   Fuel level at end — fuel Sensor reading at the end of the Trip
    
-   Fuel consumption — amount of fuel used during the Trip, in liters
    
-   Fuel consumption — average fuel consumption during the Trip, calculated in liters per 100 km
    

The figures are generated when a fuel level or fuel consumption sensor is present and correctly configured. If there is no Sensor or no data was received from it, the corresponding columns may show zero values.
