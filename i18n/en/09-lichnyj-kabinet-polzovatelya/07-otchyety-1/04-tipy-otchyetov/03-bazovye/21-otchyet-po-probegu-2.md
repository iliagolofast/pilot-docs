---
title: "Mileage Report No. 2"
---

Mileage Report No. 2 contains information about the Trips of the selected vehicle over the specified period. Use it to find out the time and place each Trip started and ended, the assigned Driver, odometer readings, duration, distance, and travel speed.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu___2_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Begin — date, time, and place the Trip started
    
-   End — date, time, and place the Trip ended
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle during the Trip
    
-   Odometer at start, km — odometer reading at the start of the Trip
    
-   Odometer at end, km — odometer reading after the Trip ended
    
-   Next maintenance, km — remaining Mileage until the next maintenance
    
-   Duration — Trip duration
    
-   Distance, km — distance traveled during the Trip
    
-   Avg. speed, km/h — average travel speed during the Trip
    
-   Max. speed, km/h — maximum speed recorded during the Trip
