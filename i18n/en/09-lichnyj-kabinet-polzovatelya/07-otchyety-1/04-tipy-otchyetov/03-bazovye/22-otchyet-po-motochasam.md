---
title: "Engine Hours Report"
---

An engine hour is a unit for measuring the operating time of an engine or other equipment. In the simplest case, one engine hour corresponds to one hour of engine operation. In some systems, the calculation may also take engine speed and load into account. Engine hours help determine the actual operating time of machinery, monitor its use, and plan maintenance by operating time rather than by Mileage alone.

The Engine Hours Report contains information about the operating time of the selected Objects over the specified period. Operating periods are determined from the readings of the Sensor configured for calculating engine hours.

The “Total” row shows the total operating time and total fuel consumption for the corresponding Object.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_motochasam_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Sensor — the Sensor whose state is used to calculate operating time, for example, an ignition Sensor;
    
-   Start — date and time the Sensor switched to the active state and engine hour counting began;
    
-   Stop — date and time the Sensor switched to the inactive state and counting ended;
    
-   Duration — operating time during the corresponding interval;
    
-   Fuel — amount of fuel used during operation.
    

For engine hours to be calculated correctly, you need to configure the corresponding Sensor for the Object. Fuel data is shown when a fuel level or fuel consumption sensor is present and correctly configured.
