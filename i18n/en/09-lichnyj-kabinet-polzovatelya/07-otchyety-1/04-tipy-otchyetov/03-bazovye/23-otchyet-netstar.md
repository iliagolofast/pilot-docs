---
title: "Netstar Report"
---

The Netstar Report is an electronic trip sheet (e-Trip Sheet) with detailed information about the vehicle’s operation over the selected period. The report shows, in sequence, the movement and Parking intervals, speed indicators, Mileage, violations, and fuel consumption.

![](images/drex_otchyet_netstar_custom.png)

Before generating the report, you need to select the period and the vehicle. In the “Report parameters” window you can specify:

-   Crew Member 1–4 — crew members
    
-   Controller — the responsible controller
    

![](images/drex_otchyet_netstar_custom_2.png)

The information you enter is used to generate the electronic trip sheet.

### General information

The top of the report shows:

-   Trip Sheet Number — the electronic trip sheet number generated by the system
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle
    
-   Crew Members — the crew members specified before generating the report
    
-   Vehicle — name or number of the monitored Object
    
-   Period — the dates the trip sheet was generated for
    

![](images/drex_otchyet_netstar_custom_3.png)

### What the report shows

Each row corresponds to a separate movement or Parking interval.

-   Status — state of the vehicle: moving or a numbered Parking
    
-   Start — date and time the interval began
    
-   End — date and time the interval ended
    
-   Duration — duration of the movement or Parking
    
-   Distance — distance traveled during the movement interval
    
-   Max. — maximum speed during the interval
    
-   Average — average speed during the interval
    
-   Driver ID — Driver identifier
    
-   Speeding — information about recorded speeding
    
-   Harsh Braking — information about harsh braking
    
-   Harsh Acceleration — information about harsh acceleration
    
-   Over Idle — duration of exceeding the allowed idling time
    
-   Started at — start time of the recorded action or Event
    
-   Ended at — end time of the recorded action or Event
    
-   Work — type of activity during Parking, for example Rest/Sleep/Eat/Other
    
-   Work — working time category, indicated by the codes NT/OT/DT
    

If a particular Event was not recorded, a dash is shown in the corresponding column.

### Summary indicators

The bottom of the report shows summary information:

-   Total distance — total Mileage
    
-   Driving time — total duration of movement
    
-   Parking time — total duration of Parking
    
-   Number of Stops — number of Parking stops
    
-   Average speed — average travel speed
    
-   Max. speed — maximum recorded speed
    
-   Fuel consumption (by norm) — calculated fuel consumption according to the set norm
    
-   Fuel consumption (by sensor) — fuel consumption according to Sensor readings
    
-   Average by norma — average normative consumption per 100 km
    
-   Average by sensor — average consumption per 100 km according to Sensor readings
