---
title: "No Movement"
---

The “No Movement” report is designed to find vehicles that did not move for a set amount of time or traveled less than a set distance over the selected period.

Use it to determine which vehicles have been stationary for a long time, when data was last received from them, and where they are.

Before generating the report, you can set the following parameters:

1 — Vehicle not moving time (hours) — the minimum number of hours the vehicle has not moved. The report includes Objects whose time without movement is equal to or greater than the specified value

2 — Vehicle has traveled less than (km) — the maximum distance traveled by the vehicle over the selected period. The report includes Objects whose Mileage is less than the specified value

![No Movement](images/drex_net_dvizheniya_screen.png)

For example, if you enter 2 in the first field and 5 in the second, the report shows the vehicles that meet these conditions: those that have not moved for at least two hours and have traveled less than five kilometers.

### What the report shows

-   Group — the group or folder the vehicle belongs to
    
-   Registration — registration or fleet number of the vehicle
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle
    
-   Last movement date — date and time when the vehicle was last moving
    
-   Last update time — date and time the last data was received from the Object
    
-   Status — current state of the Object and Parking duration
    
-   Location — the vehicle’s last known location
    
-   Difference — interval between the date of the last movement and the time of the last data update
