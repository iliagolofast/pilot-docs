---
title: "Raw Speed Report"
---

The Raw Speed Report contains raw speed and coordinate data for the selected Object over the specified period. Use it to view the sequence of recorded measurements, find out the vehicle’s speed, and determine its location at each moment.

![](images/drex_otchyet_ob_iskhodnoj_skorosti_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Date — date the measurement was recorded
    
-   Time — exact time the measurement was recorded
    
-   Speed — vehicle speed at the moment the data was received
    
-   lat — geographic latitude of the Object’s location
    
-   Lon — geographic longitude of the Object’s location
