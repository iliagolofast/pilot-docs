---
title: "Refrigerator Report"
---

This report shows information about the operation of the Sensors installed on the refrigerator unit, as well as its location and speed over the selected period.

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_refrizheratore_custom.png)

When generating the report, you can set the minimum recording interval (in seconds).

The minimum recording interval (in seconds) is a parameter that determines how often the system records the state of the refrigerator unit and adds new rows to the report.

For example, if you set the interval to 900 seconds (15 minutes), the temperature, door state, ignition, and other parameters are shown in the report at 15-minute steps over the selected period.

![Refrigerator Report](images/drex_otchyet_o_refrizheratore_screen.png)

To track the parameters of specific Sensors and get their readings in the report, you need to [configure](../../../datchiki-1/dobavlenie-datchika.html) them on the Object. For example, if temperature and humidity Sensors are installed on the refrigerator unit, their readings are shown in the report.

### What the report shows

-   Gps Date & Time — date and time within the reporting period
    
-   Geographic latitude and longitude coordinates to determine the Object’s location
    
-   Place — place where the movement began
    
-   Speed — speed of the Object
    
-   Ignition sensor — state of the ignition Sensor
    
-   AC unit status — Sensor showing whether the refrigerator unit is on or off
    
-   Back door, Left door, Right door — door states (rear, left, right)
