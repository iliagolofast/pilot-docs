---
title: "Loading and Unloading Report"
---

The Report contains information about loading and unloading operations of the selected vehicles over the specified period. Use it to find out when and where the cargo weight changed, determine the operation type, and compare the weight Sensor readings before and after the operation.

An increase in the recorded weight is treated as loading, and a decrease as unloading. Each row of the report corresponds to one detected operation.

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_pogruzkakh_i_razgruzkakh_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Vehicle number — name, registration, or fleet number of the vehicle
    
-   Date — date and exact time the operation was recorded
    
-   Weight at start (kg) — weight Sensor reading before the operation began
    
-   Weight difference (kg) — amount of weight change during loading or unloading
    
-   Weight at end (kg) — weight Sensor reading after the operation ended
    
-   Operation type — kind of recorded change:
    
-   Load — the vehicle weight increased
    
-   Unload — the vehicle weight decreased
    
-   Address — address or description of the place where the operation took place
    

The address is shown as a link that takes you to the corresponding location.

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_pogruzkakh_i_razgruzkakh_custom_2.png)

### Summary indicators

The bottom of the report shows:

-   Total weight load — total weight of cargo loaded
    
-   Total weight unload — total weight of cargo unloaded
    
-   Max weight load — maximum weight of a single loading
    
-   Min weight load — minimum weight of a single loading
