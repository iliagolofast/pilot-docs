---
title: "Rotation Sensors Report"
---

The Rotation Sensors Report contains graphical information about the vehicle’s speed and the operation of rotating mechanisms over the selected period. Use it to compare the Object’s movement with the intensity of connected equipment, for example, loading and unloading mechanisms.

A separate chart is generated for each selected Object. The horizontal axis shows the date and time, and the vertical axes show travel speed and the rotation speed of the mechanisms.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_datchikam_vrashcheniya_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Speed, km/h — travel speed of the Object
    
-   Loading, rpm — rotation speed of the loading-related mechanism
    
-   Unloading, rpm — rotation speed of the unloading-related mechanism
    

Each indicator is shown in a separate color on the chart. This lets you determine periods of movement, when the mechanisms start and stop working, and compare their activity over time.

Rotation data is shown when the corresponding Sensors are present and correctly configured on the Object.
