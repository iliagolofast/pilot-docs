title: "Points"
---

The “Points” report contains information about the telematics points received by the system from the GPS tracker of the selected Object. Use it to check the recording and receipt time of each point, the coordinates, speed, heading, ignition state, and other transmitted parameters.

Before generating the report, you need to select:

— one vehicle

— a period of no more than one day

![](images/drex_tochki__custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   № — sequence number of the point
    
-   Gps Date — date the point was recorded by the GPS tracker
    
-   Gps Time — time the point was recorded by the GPS tracker
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle at the time the point was recorded
    
-   Speed — the Object’s speed determined from GPS data
    
-   Latitude — geographic latitude of the Object’s location
    
-   Longitude — geographic longitude of the Object’s location
    
-   GPRS Received Date — date the point was received by the system
    
-   GPRS Received Time — time the point was received by the system
    
-   Ignition — ignition state at the time the point was recorded
    
-   Heading — direction of the Object’s movement indicated by a compass point, for example N, SE, or W
    
-   Acceleration/Deceleration — recorded value of the Object’s acceleration or deceleration
    

![](images/drex_tochki__custom_2.png)

### Downloading the report

Next to the “Generate report” button there is a download button. Use it to download the report in XLSX format for viewing and further processing in a spreadsheet program.
