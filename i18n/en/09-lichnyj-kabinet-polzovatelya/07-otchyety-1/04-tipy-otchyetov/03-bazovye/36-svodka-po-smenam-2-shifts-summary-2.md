This is a summary report for analyzing vehicle operation by shift. It shows how Mileage and working time are distributed between the first shift, the second shift, and time outside shifts.

![](images/drex_svodka_po_smenam__2__shifts_summary__2__custom.png)

The report is built based on the working time settings in the Object card.

For example, two shifts are set in the Object’s working time:

-   Shift 1 — from 06:00 to 17:00
    
-   Shift 2 — from 18:00 to 05:00
    
-   Friday — day off
    

![Shifts Summary #2 (Shifts Summary #2)](images/drex_svodka_po_smenam__2__shifts_summary__2__screen.png)

The system uses this schedule to split data into time intervals:

-   movement from 06:00 to 17:00 falls into Shift 1
    
-   movement from 18:00 to 05:00 falls into the second shift
    
-   movement between shifts and on days off falls outside shifts
    

### What the report shows

-   1st Shift (km) — Mileage in the first shift
    
-   2nd Shift (km) — Mileage in the second shift
    
-   After Duty (km) — Mileage outside shifts
    
-   Total (km) — total Mileage
    
-   1st Shift Hours — driving time in the first shift
    
-   2nd Shift Hours — driving time in the second shift
    
-   After Duty Hours — driving time outside shifts
    
-   Ignition On (hh:mm) — total ignition-on time
    
-   Running (hh:mm) — driving time
    
-   Idling (hh:mm) — idling time
    
-   Sensor (hh:mm) — operating time of the selected Sensor
    
-   Trips — number of Trips
    
-   Average speed — average speed of Trips
    
-   Avg Km / Trip — average Mileage per Trip
    
-   Avg Time (hh:mm) / Trip — average Trip duration
    
-   Fuel Consumption — fuel consumption
