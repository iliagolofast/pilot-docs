---
title: "Report for Sky Favorit"
---

The Report for Sky Favorit contains information about vehicle movement and Parking over the selected period. Use it to find out when and where a vehicle was moving or parked, assess the duration and distance of Trips, and check the operation of the door, air conditioner, and heater.

![](images/drex_otchet_dlya_sky_favorit_custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Status — state of the vehicle: moving or a numbered Parking

• Begin — date and time the movement or Parking began

• End — date and time the movement or Parking ended

• Dur. — duration of the corresponding interval

• Distance (km) — distance traveled during the movement interval

• Start — address where the movement or Parking began

• Stop — address where the movement or Parking ended

• Coordinates — latitude and longitude of the start and end points of the interval

• Door open (count) — number of recorded door openings

• Air conditioner (Duration) — air conditioner operating time

• Heater (Dur) — heater operating time

The door, air conditioner, and heater indicators are shown when the corresponding Sensors are present and correctly configured.

![](images/drex_otchet_dlya_sky_favorit_custom_2.png)

### Summary indicators

The bottom of each Object’s block shows:

• Total mileage — total distance traveled by the vehicle

• Driving time — total duration of movement

• Parking time — total duration of Parking

• Average speed — average travel speed

• Max. speed — maximum recorded speed

• Fuel consumption (by norm) — calculated consumption according to the set norm

• Fuel consumption (by sensor) — consumption according to the fuel Sensor readings

• Average l/100 km (by norm) — normative fuel consumption per 100 km

• Average l/100 km (by sensor) — actual consumption per 100 km based on Sensor data

Actual fuel consumption data is generated when a fuel Sensor is present and correctly configured.
