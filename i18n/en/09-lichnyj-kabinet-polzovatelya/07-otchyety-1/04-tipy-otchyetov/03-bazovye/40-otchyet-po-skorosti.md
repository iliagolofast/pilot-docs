---
title: "Speed Report"
---

The Speed Report contains charts of speed changes for the selected vehicles over the specified period. Use it to trace speed dynamics, determine periods of movement and stops, and identify sudden changes or high speed values.

A separate chart is generated for each selected Object and time interval.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_skorosti_custom.png)

### What the chart shows

-   Horizontal axis — date and time the data was recorded
    
-   Vertical axis — vehicle speed in km/h
    
-   Blue line — change in the Object’s speed over time
    
-   Zero value — no movement at the corresponding moment
    
-   Gaps in the chart may mean there is no data for that period
    

When you hover over the line, a pop-up window appears with the date, time, and the Object’s speed at the selected point on the chart.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_skorosti_custom_2.png)
