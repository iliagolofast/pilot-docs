---
title: "Equipment Summary Report"
---

The Equipment Summary Report contains daily indicators of the operation of machinery and the equipment installed on it over the selected period. Use it to assess the duration of operation, idling, operation under load, and downtime, and to check the number of recorded actions, fuel consumption, and distance traveled.

![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchyet_po_oborudovaniyu_custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Object — name of the monitored Object

• Vehicle number — registration, fleet, or other vehicle number specified in the system

• Date — the date the figures are calculated for

• Operating time (ignition sensor) — operating duration determined from the state of the ignition Sensor

• Idling — duration of equipment operation without load

• Under load — duration of equipment operation under load

• Downtime — duration of the period during which the machinery or equipment was not operating

• Number of actions — number of recorded work operations or equipment activations

• Fuel consumption — amount of fuel used during the corresponding day

• Distance — distance traveled by the vehicle during the day

The duration of the modes is shown in the “hours:minutes:seconds” format. The content and accuracy of the data depend on the presence and correct configuration of the ignition, load, equipment operation, and fuel consumption Sensors. If the required data was not received, some fields may be empty or contain zero or incorrect values.
