---
title: "Total Mileage Report"
---

The Total Mileage Report contains information about the distance traveled by the selected vehicles over the specified period. Use it to compare the daily Mileage of Objects based on GPS and CAN data, and to find the total Mileage for the entire reporting period.

Data is grouped by date: each row corresponds to one day, and a separate column is generated for each selected vehicle. The top of the report shows the list of selected Objects and the analyzed period.

![](images/drex_summarnyj_otchyet_po_probegu_custom.png)

### What the report shows

• № — row sequence number

• Date — the date the Mileage is calculated for

• Total — total Mileage of all selected vehicles for the corresponding date

• Vehicle — name, registration, or fleet number of the monitored Object

• GPS — Mileage calculated from GPS coordinates

• CAN — Mileage calculated from CAN bus data

The bottom total row shows the total Mileage based on GPS and CAN data for the entire selected period — both for all Objects together and for each vehicle separately.

CAN data availability depends on the hardware and Object settings. If no data is received from the CAN bus, the corresponding value may be missing or equal to zero.
