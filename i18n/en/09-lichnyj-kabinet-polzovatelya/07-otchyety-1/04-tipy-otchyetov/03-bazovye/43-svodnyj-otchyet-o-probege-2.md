---
title: "Summary Mileage Report No. 2"
---

Summary Mileage Report No. 2 contains information about the distance traveled by the selected vehicles on the specified date. Use it to compare the Mileage of monitored Objects based on GPS and CAN data, and to find the total Mileage of all selected vehicles.

![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchyet_o_probege__2_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Object — name, registration, or fleet number of the vehicle
    
-   Date — the date the Mileage is calculated for
    
-   GPS — Mileage calculated from GPS coordinates
    
-   CAN — Mileage calculated from CAN bus data.
    

The bottom “Total” row shows the total Mileage of all selected vehicles for the specified date — separately for GPS and CAN data

CAN data availability depends on the installed hardware and Object settings. If no data is received from the CAN bus, the corresponding value may be missing or equal to zero.
