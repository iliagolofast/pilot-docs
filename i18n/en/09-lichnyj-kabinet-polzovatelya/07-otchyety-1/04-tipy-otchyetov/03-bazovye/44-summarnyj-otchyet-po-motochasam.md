---
title: "Total Engine Hours Report"
---

The Total Engine Hours Report contains information about engine operating time and the distance traveled by the selected vehicles over the specified period. An engine hour is a unit for measuring engine operating time.

The Report helps you monitor vehicle use and plan maintenance based on engine operating time, not just the distance traveled.

![](images/drex_summarnyj_otchyet_po_motochasam_custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Object — name, registration, or fleet number of the vehicle

• Period — date and time the period began and ended for which the data is calculated

• Engine hours — engine operating time over the specified period

• Distance — distance traveled by the vehicle over the specified period

The bottom “Total” row shows the total engine operating time and the total distance traveled by all selected vehicles over the specified period.
