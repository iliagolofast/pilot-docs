---
title: "Trip Report"
---

The Trip Report contains information about the Trips of the selected vehicle over the specified period. Use it to determine the time and place each Trip started and ended, check odometer readings, and find the distance traveled.

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_poezdke_custom.png)

What the report shows

• Type — the data source used to calculate Trip indicators, for example CAN

• Object — name, registration, or fleet number of the vehicle

• Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle during the Trip

• Start time — date and time the Trip started

• Start Odo — odometer reading at the start of the Trip

• Start address — address recorded at the Trip start location

• End time — date and time the Trip ended

• End Odo — odometer reading at the end of the Trip

• End address — address recorded at the Trip end location

• Total Odo — distance traveled during the Trip, calculated as the difference between the final and initial odometer readings

The bottom “Total” row shows the total distance traveled by the vehicle over the selected period.
