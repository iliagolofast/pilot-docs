---
title: "Trips and Parking Report (Working Time) No. 2"
---

The “Trips and Parking (Working Time) No. 2” report contains summary information about the movement of the selected vehicles according to the work schedule. Use it to determine when a vehicle was used, who drove it, where it traveled from and to, and to find out the movement duration, Mileage, and speed indicators.

# ![](images/drex_otchet_o_poezdkakh_i_parkovkakh__rabochee_vremya___2_custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Vehicle — name, registration, or fleet number of the monitored Object

• Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle during the movement

• Day — the day of the week on which the Trips were made

• Start — date and time the first movement began in the counted interval

• End — date and time the last movement ended in the counted interval

• Duration — total movement duration in the report row

• Distance — total distance traveled by the vehicle

• From — address where the movement began

• To — address where the movement ended

The addresses in the “From” and “To” columns are shown as links that take you to the corresponding locations.

![](images/drex_otchet_o_poezdkakh_i_parkovkakh__rabochee_vremya___2_custom_2.png)

### Totals by date

Below each daily block, the indicators calculated from all rows for the corresponding date are shown:

• Total mileage — total distance traveled by the vehicle during the day

• Moving time — total movement duration for the day

• Average speed — the vehicle’s average speed for the day

• Max. speed — the highest speed recorded during the day

### Totals for the entire period

The bottom of the report shows indicators for all data included in the selected reporting period:

• Total mileage for the period — total distance traveled by the vehicle

• Moving time for the period — total movement duration

• Average speed for the period — average travel speed over the entire period

• Max. speed for the period — the highest speed recorded over the entire period

![](images/drex_otchet_o_poezdkakh_i_parkovkakh__rabochee_vremya___2_custom_3.png)

### Working time parameters

Before generating the report, you can select which data to include:

• Working time only — Trips made within the Object’s work schedule are counted

![](images/drex_otchet_o_poezdkakh_i_parkovkakh__rabochee_vremya___2_custom_4.png)

• Non-working time only — Trips made outside the Object’s work schedule are counted

![](images/drex_otchet_o_poezdkakh_i_parkovkakh__rabochee_vremya___2_custom_5.png)

Working and non-working time is determined by the [vehicle’s work schedule settings](../../../moduli-1/kontrol-vremeni.html#Рабочее_время_объекта).

**Working time only**

When you enable the “Working time only” option, the report shows vehicle use during the hours set by the work schedule. This mode lets you assess:

• the number and duration of working departures

• Mileage during working time

• the start and end points of movement

• average and maximum speed

• the assigned Driver’s work

![](images/drex_otchet_o_poezdkakh_i_parkovkakh__rabochee_vremya___2_custom_6.png)

**Non-working time only**

When you enable the “Non-working time only” option, the report shows Trips made outside the set work schedule. This mode lets you identify:

• movement before the start or after the end of a shift

• Trips on weekends and non-working days

• vehicle use outside permitted hours

• Mileage and speed indicators during non-working hours

If a Trip crosses the boundary of the working interval, the report counts the part of the movement that corresponds to the selected mode.

![](images/drex_otchet_o_poezdkakh_i_parkovkakh__rabochee_vremya___2_custom_7.png)
