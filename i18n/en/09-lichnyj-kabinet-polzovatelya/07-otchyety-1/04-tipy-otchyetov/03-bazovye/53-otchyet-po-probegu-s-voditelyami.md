---
title: "Mileage Report (with Drivers)"
---

The “Mileage (with Drivers)” report contains information about vehicle movement and Parking, with the assigned Drivers indicated. It lets you determine who drove the vehicle, when and where Trips and Parking took place, how far the vehicle traveled, and which operating indicators were calculated.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu__s_voditelyami__custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Status — state of the Object: “Movement” or “Parking”; Parking may be assigned a sequence number

• Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle during the Event

• Begin — date and time the movement or Parking began

• End — date and time the movement or Parking ended

• Duration — duration of the corresponding Trip or Parking

• Distance — distance traveled by the vehicle during the Trip; no value is shown for Parking

• Start — address where the Trip began or the Parking location

• Stop — address where the Trip ended; for Parking the value may not be shown

• Coordinates — geographic coordinates of the start and end points of the Trip or of the Parking location

• Max. speed — the highest speed recorded during the Trip

• Trip type — Trip category determined by the system or the Object settings

The addresses in the “Start” and “Stop” columns are shown as links that take you to the corresponding locations.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu__s_voditelyami__custom_2.png)

### Summary indicators

Below the table, the following indicators are shown for each Object and date:

• Trips — total number of Trips

• Movements — number of recorded movement periods

• Parking — number of recorded Parking stops

• Total mileage — total distance traveled by the vehicle

• Moving time — total movement duration

• Parking dur. — total Parking duration

• Average speed — average travel speed for the corresponding period

• Max. speed — the highest recorded speed

• Fuel consumption (by norm) — calculated fuel consumption based on the set norm

• Fuel consumption (by sensor) — fuel consumption calculated from Sensor readings

• Average l/100 km (by norm) — calculated average fuel consumption per 100 km

• Average l/100 km (by sensor) — average fuel consumption per 100 km based on Sensor data

### Filter by Drivers

If you enable the “On Driver” option, an additional “Drivers” panel opens to the right of the Object list.

![Mileage Report (with Drivers)](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu__s_voditelyami__screen.png)

In this panel you can select one Driver, several Drivers, or a group of Drivers. The report will show only Events related to the selected Drivers.

![Mileage Report (with Drivers)](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu__s_voditelyami__screen_2.png)
