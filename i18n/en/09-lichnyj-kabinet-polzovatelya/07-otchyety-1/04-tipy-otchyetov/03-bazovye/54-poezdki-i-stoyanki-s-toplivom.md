---
title: "Trips and Parking with Fuel"
---

The “Trips and Parking with Fuel” report contains information about the Trips and Parking of the selected vehicles over the specified period. Use it to determine the time and place each Event started and ended, its duration, Mileage, and travel speed, and to analyze refuels, fuel drains, and fuel consumption.

### What the report shows

• Object — name, registration, or fleet number of the vehicle

• Folder — the folder where the Object is located in the system

• Status — state of the Object: “Movement” or “Parking”

• Start — date and time the Trip or Parking began

• Start address — address where the movement began or the Parking location

• Stop — date and time the Trip or Parking ended

• End address — address where the movement ended; for Parking the value may be missing

• Duration — duration of the corresponding Trip or Parking

• Mileage (km) — distance traveled by the Object during the Trip; a zero value is shown for Parking

• Refueled (l) — volume of fuel added during the corresponding Event

• Drained (l) — volume of fuel recognized by the system as a drain

• Fuel consumption by sensor (l) — volume of fuel used, calculated from fuel level sensor data

• Fuel consumption/100 km (l) — actual fuel consumption per 100 km, calculated from Sensor data

• Norm, l/100 km — the normative fuel consumption per 100 km set for the vehicle

• Max. speed (km/h) — the highest speed recorded during the Trip

• Average speed (km/h) — the vehicle’s average speed during the Trip

### Trips and Parking filter

At the top of the generated report there are the “Parking” and “Trips” options. They let you change which rows are shown:

• “Parking” and “Trips” enabled — the full sequence of Events is shown

![Trips and Parking with Fuel](images/drex_poezdki_i_stoyanki_s_toplivom_screen.png)

• only “Trips” enabled — only movement periods are shown

![](images/drex_poezdki_i_stoyanki_s_toplivom_custom_2.png)

• only “Parking” enabled — only periods when the Object was parked are shown

![](images/drex_poezdki_i_stoyanki_s_toplivom_custom_3.png)

To show or hide a particular Event type, check or uncheck the corresponding box.
