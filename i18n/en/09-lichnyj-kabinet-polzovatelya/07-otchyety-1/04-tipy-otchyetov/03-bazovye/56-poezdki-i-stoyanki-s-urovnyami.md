---
title: "Trips and Parking with Levels"
---

The “Trips and Parking with Levels” report contains information about the movement and Parking of the selected monitored Objects over the specified period. Data is grouped by Object and date and shown as summary rows that you can expand to view the detailed sequence of Events.

The summary row shows the time and place the counted period started and ended, its total duration, and the total distance. To view the Trips and Parking it includes, click the ![](images/drex_poezdki_i_stoyanki_s_urovnyami_custom.png) button to the left of the Object name.

![](images/drex_poezdki_i_stoyanki_s_urovnyami_custom_2.png)

### What the report shows

• Object — name, registration, or fleet number of the monitored Object

• Type — Event type: “Movement” or “Parking”

• Begin — date and time the Trip or Parking began

• Start — address where the movement began or the Parking location

• End — date and time the Trip or Parking ended

• Stop — address where the movement ended; for Parking it usually matches the place where it began

• Duration (h) — duration of the corresponding Trip or Parking

• Distance (km) — distance traveled by the Object during the Trip; a zero value is shown for Parking
