---
title: "Waybills"
---

The “Waybills” report is designed to generate vehicle waybills according to the established standard forms. Some fields are filled in automatically based on the selected Object, the assigned Driver, the reporting period, and the data specified in the system.

The Report lets you prepare a document for recording the operation of the vehicle and Driver, check the generated information, save the waybill to a file, or print it.

![](images/drex_putevye_listy_custom.png)

### Selecting the vehicle type

Before generating the report, you need to select the vehicle type.

![](images/drex_putevye_listy_custom_2.png)

The waybill form depends on the selected type:

• Passenger car — a passenger car waybill is generated using standard interindustry form No. 3, approved by Resolution of the State Statistics Committee of Russia of 28.11.1997 No. 78

![](images/drex_putevye_listy_custom_3.png)

• Truck — a truck waybill is generated using standard interindustry form No. 4-P, approved by Resolution of the State Statistics Committee of Russia of 28.11.1997 No. 78

![](images/drex_putevye_listy_custom_4.png)

### What the waybill shows

The content of the document depends on the selected form. The waybill may include:

• organization details

• date, series, and number of the waybill

• make and state registration number of the vehicle

• fleet and personnel numbers

• Driver details

• driver’s license number and Driver class

• license details

• actual departure and return times

• data on the Driver’s and the vehicle’s work

• information on movement and fuel consumption

• the Driver’s assignment

• fields for marks, signatures, and technical inspection results

The presence and completion of individual fields depends on the selected form and on what information has been entered in the organization, vehicle, and Driver cards.

### Saving and printing

You can preview the generated waybill in the report. The following actions are available at the bottom of the window:

• Download in Excel format — saves the waybill as a spreadsheet

• Download in PDF format — saves the document in PDF format

• Print — opens the waybill for printing
