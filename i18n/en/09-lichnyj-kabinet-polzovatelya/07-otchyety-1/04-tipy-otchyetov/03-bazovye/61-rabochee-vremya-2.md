---
title: "Working Time No. 2"
---

The “Working Time No. 2” report contains information about the Mileage and working time of the selected vehicle over the specified period. Use it to compare odometer readings with the calculated Mileage, determine the number of Trips, and split working time into categories: regular, overtime, holiday, and Sunday.

# ![](images/drex_rabochee_vremya__2_custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Date — the day the indicators are calculated for

• Start odometer — odometer reading at the start of the first Trip on the corresponding date

• End odometer — odometer reading at the end of the last Trip on the corresponding date

• Total mileage, km — total distance traveled by the vehicle during the day

• Number of Trips — number of Trips recorded for the corresponding date

• Regular/working time — duration of work within the set work schedule

• Overtime hours — duration of work beyond the set norm

• Holiday working hours — duration of work on public holidays

• Sunday working hours — duration of work on Sundays

• Total mileage and hours — graphical comparison of Mileage with the working time categories. The colored sections of the bar show the distribution of the indicator, and the calculated

Mileage value is shown below it

![](images/drex_rabochee_vremya__2_custom_2.png)

### Summary indicators

The bottom “Total” row shows the summary indicators for the entire reporting period:

• total vehicle Mileage

• total number of Trips

• total regular working time

• total number of overtime hours

• total working time on public holidays

• total working time on Sundays

• the total value in the “Total mileage and hours” column
