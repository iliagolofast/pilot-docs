---
title: "Basic"
---

This section contains the main Reports for analyzing the operation of vehicles and other monitored Objects. They help you monitor Mileage, Trips and Parking, working time, engine hours, fuel consumption, equipment utilization, fleet status, and the performance of specialized tasks.

With these Reports you can:

— analyze Mileage based on GPS, CAN bus, and odometer data

— view Trips and Parking, their duration, addresses, and coordinates

— monitor movement time, engine operating time, and ignition-on time

— compare equipment operation during working and non-working time

— track fuel consumption, refuels, and fuel drains

— analyze travel speed, equipment utilization, and axle load

— monitor Driver performance and the completion of assigned routes

— view routes, Parking, and Events on charts and Maps

— generate waybills and monitor document validity periods

— assess the composition and changes of the fleet over the selected period

The specific set of indicators, filters, and additional parameters depends on the selected Report, the Object’s hardware, and the system settings.
