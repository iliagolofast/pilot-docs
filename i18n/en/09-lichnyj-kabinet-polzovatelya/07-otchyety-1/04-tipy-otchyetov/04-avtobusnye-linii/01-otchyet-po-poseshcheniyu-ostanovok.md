---
title: "Stop Visits Report"
---

The Stop Visits Report is designed to monitor visits to bus stops by vehicles assigned to routes. Use it to determine which stops a vehicle visited, and to find out its actual arrival and departure times.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_poseshcheniyu_ostanovok_custom.png)

Before generating the report, you need to select the vehicles and the bus line for which you want to check stop visits.

### What the report shows

Each row of the report corresponds to one recorded visit to a stop by a vehicle.

The report shows:

-   Order — the position of the stop in the sequence of the route
    
-   Route number — name or number of the route on which the stop was visited
    
-   Stop — name of the visited stop
    
-   Address — address of the stop
    
-   Date — date of the stop visit
    
-   Arrival time — time the vehicle arrived at the stop
    
-   Departure time — time the vehicle left the stop
    
-   Vehicle — number of the monitored Object that visited the stop
    

If an [RFID tag is linked](../../../moduli-1/rejsy-2/kak-dobavit-ostanovki.html#Как_привязать_RFID-метку_к_остановке) to the stop, PILOT can determine a visit from the data of the RFID reader installed on the vehicle. The recorded visit is shown in the report in the same way as a visit determined by the Object’s coordinates.
