---
title: "Stop Visit Control"
---

The “Stop Visit Control” report helps you monitor how vehicles actually follow the set routes. Use it to check which stops a vehicle visited, when it arrived and departed, and how long it stayed at the stop.

![](images/drex_kontrol_poseshcheniya_ostanovok_custom.png)

When generating the report, select the stops whose visits you want to check. If several stops are selected, the report shows all recorded visits to these stops by the selected vehicles during the specified period.

### What the report shows

Each row is one recorded visit to a stop by a vehicle.

The report shows:

-   Stop sequence number — the position of the stop in the sequence of the route
    
-   Route — name or number of the route the vehicle was following
    
-   Stop name — name of the visited stop
    
-   Address — location of the stop
    
-   Date — day of the stop visit
    
-   Arrival time — time the vehicle arrived at the stop
    
-   Departure time — time the vehicle left the stop
    
-   Parking — length of time the vehicle was at the stop
    
-   Vehicle number — vehicle number
