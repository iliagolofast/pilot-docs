---
title: "Loop Trips Report"
---

The Loop Trips Report is designed to analyze vehicle operation on routes. It shows how long the vehicle operated, as well as the number of completed full and incomplete loop trips.

The system determines whether a loop trip is complete based on the visits to the stops assigned to the Trip and the set direction of movement. If the vehicle completed the route according to the specified conditions, the loop trip is counted as full. If the route conditions were not fully met, the loop trip is counted as incomplete.

Each row of the report contains information about the operation of one vehicle on the route on the specified date.

### What the report shows

-   Trip number — number of the Trip assigned to the monitored Object
    
-   Vehicle number — vehicle number
    
-   Date — date the Trips were performed
    
-   Route arrival time — time the vehicle entered the route
    
-   Departure time — time the vehicle left the route
    
-   Time on route — total duration of the vehicle’s operation on the route, calculated as the difference between the arrival time and the departure time
    
-   Number of Trips — number of full loop trips completed on the route
    
-   Number of incomplete Trips — number of loop trips during which not all anchor stops were visited
