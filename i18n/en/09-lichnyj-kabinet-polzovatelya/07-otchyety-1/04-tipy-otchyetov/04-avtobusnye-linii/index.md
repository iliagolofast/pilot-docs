---
title: "Bus Lines"
---

This section contains reports related to the [“Trips”](../../../moduli-1/rejsy-2/index.html) Module. They help you monitor vehicle movement along bus lines, stop visits, the completion of loop trips, and schedule adherence.

With these Reports you can:

— check which stops a vehicle visited

— monitor the sequence and timing of stop visits

— analyze the number of full and incomplete loop trips

— check compliance with the set schedule

— get summary information on vehicle operation on routes
