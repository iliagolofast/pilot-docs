title: "Licenses Report"
---

A configured Rent Car Module is required.

The Module must have additional fields configured for the report, which are entered in the Admin panel.

**The Report contains:**

-   **Object or User group**
    
-   **License expiration date**
    
-   **Days remaining until expiration**
    
-   **Contract**
    
-   **MM** – Maintenance Management.
    
-   **FML** – Fleet Management Logistics.
    

![](images/drex_otchet_o_litsenziyakh_custom_2.png)

If needed, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the ![](images/drex_otchet_o_litsenziyakh_custom_3.png) icon; a menu opens where you can turn individual fields off or on.

![](images/drex_otchet_o_litsenziyakh_custom_4.png)
