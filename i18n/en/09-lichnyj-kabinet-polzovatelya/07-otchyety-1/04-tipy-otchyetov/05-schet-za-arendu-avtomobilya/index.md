The Report is designed to show information about Object rental, with the Contract, amount, and rental type indicated.

If needed, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the ![](images/drex_schet_za_arendu_avtomobilya_custom_2.png) icon; a menu opens where you can turn individual fields off or on.
