---
title: "Daily Trip Report"
---

This report shows how each vehicle and Driver worked during the day.

It lets you see:

— when the first Trip started and the last one ended

— how long the vehicle was moving, parked, and idling

— the Mileage and speed for the day

— how many Trips were made

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_ezhednevnoj_poezdke_custom.png)

### What the report shows

The report is built by day: one table row = one working day of one vehicle

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_ezhednevnoj_poezdke_custom_2.png)

-   Date — date the first Trip of the day started
    
-   Object — vehicle
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle on that day (if no Driver is assigned, no driver is shown)
    
-   First Trip start time — date and time the vehicle first started moving during the day
    
-   Hours between the start of the first Trip and the end of the last (hh:mm:ss) — the vehicle’s total “working time” for the day: from the first Trip to the last, including movement and stops
    
-   Driving time % — the share of working time spent moving
    
-   Travel time (hh:mm:ss) — total movement time for the day
    
-   Parking time (hh:mm:ss) — total time of stops and Parking between Trips
    
-   Duration (hh:mm:ss) — total duration of the vehicle’s operation for the day (movement + Parking)
    
-   Idling time (hh:mm:ss) — time when the vehicle was on but not doing work (for example, the engine was idling)
    
-   Distance (km) — the vehicle’s Mileage for the day.
    
-   Average speed (km/h) — average speed for the day
    
    (distance divided by moving time).
    
-   Max. speed (km/h) — maximum recorded speed for the day
    
-   Number of Trips — how many separate Trips were made during the day
    

At the bottom of the table there is a “Total” row, which automatically calculates:

— total working time

— total Mileage

— average indicators across all rows for the selected date
