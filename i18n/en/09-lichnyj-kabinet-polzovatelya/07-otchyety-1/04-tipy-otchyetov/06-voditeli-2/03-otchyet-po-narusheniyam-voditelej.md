---
title: "Driver Violations Report"
---

This report shows the violations Drivers committed inside the selected Geofence during the specified period.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_narusheniyam_voditelej_custom.png)

Before generating the report, specify:

1\. The Geofence in which to check for violations

2\. The Driver the report is generated for

![Driver Violations Report](images/drex_otchyet_po_narusheniyam_voditelej_screen.png)

1 — Select a Geofence

2 — Select a Driver

### What the report shows

-   Object — the vehicle (monitored Object) in which the violation occurred
    
-   Group — the group or unit the Object belongs to
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle at the time of the violation
    
-   Zone — the Geofence in which the violation was recorded
    
-   Date — exact date and time of the violation
    
-   Violation — name of the violation
