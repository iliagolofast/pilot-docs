title: "Driver List"
---

This report shows summary information about all Drivers registered in the system.

### What the report shows

-   Group — the group the Driver belongs to
    
-   Driver — the Driver’s name or full name
    
-   ID Number — the Driver’s identification number in the system
    
-   Tag ID — tag identifier
    
-   Contact number — the Driver’s phone number
    
-   Registration — the Object/vehicle the Driver is assigned to
    
-   Date — date the Driver was registered in the system
    
-   License Expiry Date — when the driver’s license expires.
