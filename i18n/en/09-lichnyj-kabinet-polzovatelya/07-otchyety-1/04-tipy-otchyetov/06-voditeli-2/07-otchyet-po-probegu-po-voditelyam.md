---
title: "Mileage Report (by Drivers)"
---

The Report contains information about the Trips of the selected vehicles over the specified period, with data broken down by Driver. Use it to determine who was driving the vehicle during each Trip, check its duration and distance, and analyze speed indicators.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu__po_voditelyam__custom.png)

### What the report shows

• Period — date and time the period began and ended for which the report was generated

• Object — name, registration, or fleet number of the vehicle

• Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle during the Trip

• Begin — date and time the Trip started

• End — date and time the Trip ended

• Duration — Trip duration

• Distance — distance traveled by the vehicle during the Trip

• Avg. speed — the vehicle’s average speed during the Trip

• Max. speed — the highest speed recorded during the Trip

At the bottom of each Object’s block, the vehicle’s total Mileage for the selected period is shown.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_probegu__po_voditelyam__custom_2.png)

### Filter by Drivers

Before generating the report, you can enable the “On Driver” option. After you enable it, an additional “Drivers” panel opens to the right of the Object list.

In this panel you can select one Driver, several Drivers, or a group of Drivers.  After the report is generated, it will include only the Trips related to the selected Drivers.
