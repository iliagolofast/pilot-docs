title: "Drivers Report"
---

This report shows which Drivers were assigned to vehicles during the selected period.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_voditelyam_custom.png)

### What the report shows

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_voditelyam_custom_2.png)

-   Object – the vehicle or monitored Object
    
-   Date – the time period the report is generated for
    
-   Driver – name of the Driver assigned to the Object
    
-   Begin – date and time the Driver was assigned to the Object
    
-   End – date and time the Driver was unassigned
    
-   Duration – the time the Driver spent behind the wheel during the selected period
