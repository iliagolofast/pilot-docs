---
title: "Business Summary Report"
---

This report shows information about Drivers’ Trips, splitting them into business and personal. It helps you analyze how much time and how many kilometers Drivers spent on work and on personal purposes.

![](images/drex_svodnyj_biznes_otchyet_custom.png)

Before generating the report, select the Driver the report is generated for.

![Business Summary Report](images/drex_svodnyj_biznes_otchyet_screen.png)

### What the report shows

![](images/drex_svodnyj_biznes_otchyet_custom_2.png)

-   Group – Driver group in the system
    
-   Object – the vehicle or monitored Object
    
-   Driver – who drove the vehicle during the Trip
    
-   Total Trips – number of all Trips during the selected period
    
-   Business Trips – number of Trips marked as business
    
-   Private Trips – number of Trips marked as personal
    
-   Total distance – total Mileage for all Trips
    
-   Business Trip distance – Mileage of business Trips only
    
-   Private Trip distance – Mileage of personal Trips only
    
-   Total duration – total time of all Trips
    
-   Business Trip duration – time spent on business Trips
    
-   Personal Trip duration – time spent on personal Trips
