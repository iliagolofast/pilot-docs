---
title: "Drivers"
---

This section contains reports related to the [“Drivers”](../../../moduli-1/voditeli-3/index.html) Module. They help you monitor Driver performance, driver’s license data, identifiers, and Trips, and analyze vehicle use.

With these Reports you can:

— see who drove a vehicle and when

— track the assignment of Drivers to vehicles;

— monitor the validity of driver’s licenses

— analyze Trips, Mileage, and violations in Geofences

— get summary information on all Drivers in the fleet
