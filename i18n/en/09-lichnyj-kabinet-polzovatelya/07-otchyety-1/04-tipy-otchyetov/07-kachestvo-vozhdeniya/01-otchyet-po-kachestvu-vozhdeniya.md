title: "Driving Quality Report"
---

The Driving Quality Report helps you understand how carefully and safely Drivers drive.

The system records Events (harsh acceleration, braking, speeding, and others), converts them into penalty points, and generates a summary table.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_kachestvu_vozhdeniya_custom.png)

### What the report shows

Each row is the data for one vehicle on the selected date and the assigned Driver.

The report shows:

-   Object — the vehicle for which violations were recorded
    
-   Group — name of the group the vehicle belongs to
    
-   Date — the day the indicators are calculated for
    
-   Driver — the Driver assigned to the vehicle at the time of the violation
    
-   Distance — the section of the route (in km) on which Eco Driving Events were recorded
    
-   Duration — total time of violations
    

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_kachestvu_vozhdeniya_custom_2.png)

### Types of violations

For each Event type (acceleration, braking, cornering, speed, seat belt), the following is shown:

-   number of violations / penalty points
    
-   Total penalty — total number of points for all violations
    
-   Penalty / 100 km — average number of penalty points per 100 km of Mileage
    

The lower the value, the calmer and safer the driving style.

At the bottom of the report there is a “Total” row — these are the summary indicators for all rows of the table.

When generating the report, you can choose which data to use for the analysis:

-   By Objects  — to see which vehicles have more violations
    
-   By groups — to compare indicators between vehicle groups
    
-   By Drivers — to identify the most careful Drivers and those who break the rules most often
    

If you do not select Objects, the report is built for all vehicles driven by the selected Drivers

If you do not select Drivers, the report includes all Drivers, as well as periods when no Driver was assigned to the vehicle

![Driving Quality Report](images/drex_otchyet_po_kachestvu_vozhdeniya_screen.png)

When generating the report, you can also take into account the [eco weights settings](../../../moduli-1/ehkodrajving-kachestvo-vozhdeniya/ehko-vesa.html) ![](images/drex_otchyet_po_kachestvu_vozhdeniya_custom_3.png). These settings let you define which violations are considered more serious and which are less significant.

The penalty points and the overall score of Drivers and vehicles depend directly on the selected weights.
