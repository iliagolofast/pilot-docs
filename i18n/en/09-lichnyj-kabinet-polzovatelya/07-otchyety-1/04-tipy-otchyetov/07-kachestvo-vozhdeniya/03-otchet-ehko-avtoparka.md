---
title: "Fleet ECO Report"
---

The Report is designed to show data on the current and previous Eco Driving rating.

**The Report contains:**

-   **Vehicle -** the monitored Object in the system;
    
-   **Excessive idling -**  the Object’s inactive time exceeds the set values;
    
-   **Speeding -** violation of the set speed limits on roads**;**
    
-   **Harsh Braking -** values of a driving style that involves smooth acceleration and braking to reduce fuel consumption and reduce emissions of harmful substances into the atmosphere.
    
-   **Harsh Acceleration -** values of a driving style that involves smooth acceleration and braking to reduce fuel consumption and reduce emissions of harmful substances into the atmosphere.
    
-   **Distance -** Mileage of the monitored Object during the specified Trip in km;
    
-   **Duration -** violation time for the selected period.    
    
-   **Current rating -** an indicator that reflects achievements over a certain period based on recent driving activity.
    
-   **Previous rating -** an indicator that reflects achievements over a certain period based on recent driving activity.
