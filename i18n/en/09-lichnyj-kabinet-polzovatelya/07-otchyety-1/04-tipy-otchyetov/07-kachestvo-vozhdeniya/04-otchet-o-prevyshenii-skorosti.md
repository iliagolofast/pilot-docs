---
title: "Speeding Report"
---

The Speeding Report is a report that shows when and where the driver of a vehicle exceeded the set speed limit.

The Speeding Report can be obtained using special devices that connect to the vehicle’s on-board computer and record data on speed, acceleration, braking, cornering, and other parameters.

![](images/drex_otchet_o_prevyshenii_skorosti_custom.png)

If needed, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the ![](images/drex_otchet_o_prevyshenii_skorosti_custom_2.png) icon,

a menu opens where you can turn individual fields off or on.

![](images/drex_otchet_o_prevyshenii_skorosti_custom_3.png)
