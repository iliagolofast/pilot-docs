**RAG Summary Report**

This report type provides more detailed information on driving quality.

When you select a specific violation, the violation type and its location on the map are shown.

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which driving quality was recorded;
    
-   **Driver** - the driver assigned to the Object;
    
-   **Month** - the month in which the violation occurred;
    
-   **Distance** - the distance over which the violation occurred;
    
-   **Duration** - the duration of the violation;
    
-   **Distance** - the distance over which the violation occurred;
    
-   **Duration** - the duration of the violation;
    
-   **Number of harsh accelerations** - the number of harsh accelerations;
    
-   **Number of harsh accelerations per 100 km** - the number of harsh accelerations per 100 km;
    
-   **Number of harsh braking events** - the number of harsh braking events;
    
-   **Number of harsh braking events per 100 km** - the number of harsh braking events per 100 km;
    
-   **Number of harsh turns** - the number of harsh turns;
    
-   **Number of harsh turns per 100 km** - the number of harsh turns per 100 km;
    
-   **Maximum speeding, km/h** - the maximum speed limit excess;
    
-   **Speeding duration** - the time during which the speed limit was exceeded;
    
-   **RAG score** - calculated from the sum of harsh turns, braking events and accelerations.

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_summarnyj_otchyet_po_rag_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
