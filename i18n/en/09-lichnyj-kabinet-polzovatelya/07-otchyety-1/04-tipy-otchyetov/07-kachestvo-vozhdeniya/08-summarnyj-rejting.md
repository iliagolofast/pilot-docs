---
title: "Summary Rating"
---

This report displays a numeric or ordinal driving rating indicator for a driver.

**The report contains:**

-   **Driver** - the driver's name in the system;
    
-   **Group** - the name of the group in the system**;**
    
-   **Rank -** the driver's place in the driving rating;
    
-   **Mileage -** the mileage value in km on the specified date;
    
-   **Score -** the score;
    
-   **Group -** the group.
    

![](images/drex_summarnyj_rejting_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_summarnyj_rejting_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
