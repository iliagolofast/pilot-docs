---
title: "Vehicle Evaluation Card No. 2"
---

This report displays the driving rating as a percentage.

**The report contains:**

-   **Driver -** the driver assigned to the Object;
    
-   **No. of trips -** the number of trips;
    
-   **Distance -** the mileage value in km on the specified date;
    
-   **Number of speeding violations -** the number of speeding violations on the specified date;
    
-   **Speeding time -** the time the driver exceeded the speed limit on the specified date;
    
-   **Speeding percentage -** the speed limit excess by the driver, as a percentage;
    
-   **Max. speed -** the maximum speed recorded during the trip;
    
-   **Avg. speed -** the average speed recorded during the trip;
    
-   **Excess idling -**
    
    # **the value of the idling duration excess;**
    
-   **Harsh braking -** the number of harsh braking events;
    
-   **Harsh acceleration -** the number of harsh accelerations;
    
-   **Rating -** a score based on the sum of harsh turns, braking events and accelerations, as a percentage;
    
-   **Group rating -** a score based on the sum of harsh turns, braking events and accelerations, as a percentage;
