---
title: "Vehicle Evaluation Card No. 3"
---

Evaluation Card No. 3 displays data by month.

**The report contains:**

-   **Driver -** the driver assigned to the Object;
    
-   **Calendar month** - a score based on the sum of harsh turns, braking events and accelerations, as a percentage.
