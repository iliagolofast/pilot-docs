**Two-State Sensors Report**

A two-state sensor is a type of sensor that can be in one of two states: active or passive. Such sensors are commonly used to monitor liquid level, pressure, temperature and other parameters. They can be mechanical, optical, magnetic, capacitive or inductive.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Date and time** - the date and time of the event;
    
-   **Event** - the sensor state;
    
-   **Driver** - the driver assigned to the Object.
    
-   **Geofences** - the location of the event.
    
-   **Location** - the address where the sensor was triggered.
