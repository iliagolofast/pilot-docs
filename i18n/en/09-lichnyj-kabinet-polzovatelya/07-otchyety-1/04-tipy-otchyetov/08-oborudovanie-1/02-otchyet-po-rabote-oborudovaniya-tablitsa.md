---
title: "Hardware Operation Report (Table)"
---

The hardware operation report displays data on the operation of hardware.

For the report to work correctly, all the necessary sensors of the connected hardware must be configured.

**The report contains:**

-   **Date** - the time period for which the report table is built.
    
-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the report table is built.
    
-   **Hardware** - the name of the hardware.
    
-   **Start** - the date and time the hardware started operating.
    
-   **End** - the date and time the hardware stopped operating.
    
-   **Duration** - the hardware operating time, the difference between the end and the start.
    
-   **Distance** - the distance traveled by the Object during the period.
    
-   **Start location** - where the hardware started operating.
    
-   **Stop location** - where the hardware stopped operating.
