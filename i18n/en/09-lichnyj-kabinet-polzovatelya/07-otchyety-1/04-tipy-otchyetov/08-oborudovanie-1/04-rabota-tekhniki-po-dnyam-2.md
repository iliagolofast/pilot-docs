# **This report is used to track and record the daily operation of various equipment. It includes the operating time in motion, parking time, resource consumption, and distance data for the reporting period.**

**The report contains:**

-   **Object -** the monitored object for which the report table is built;
    
-   **Time in motion -** the time the equipment started operating;
    
-   **Parking time -** the time the equipment stopped operating;
    
-   **Working time on route -** the equipment operating time;
    
-   **Stop time during working hours -** the equipment stop time;
    
-   **Ignition on -** the sensor state;
    
-   **Off-duty time -** equal to the difference between 24 hours and the working time on route;
    
-   **Distance -** the distance traveled by the Object during the period.
    
-   **Avg. speed -** the equipment speed during the reporting period.
