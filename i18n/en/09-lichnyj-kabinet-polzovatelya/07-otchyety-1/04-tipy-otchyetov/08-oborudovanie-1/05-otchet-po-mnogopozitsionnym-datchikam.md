**Multi-State Sensors Report**

This report type presents information as a table showing changes in multi-state sensor readings and speed over a given period of time.

**The report contains:**

-   **Time** - the time of the event;
    
-   **Sensor** - the sensor name;
    
-   **Message** - the sensor state;
    
-   **Speed** - the speed of the Object.
