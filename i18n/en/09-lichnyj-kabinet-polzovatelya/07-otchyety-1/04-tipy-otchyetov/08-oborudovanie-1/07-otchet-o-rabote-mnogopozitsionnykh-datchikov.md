---
title: "Multi-State Sensors Operation Report"
---

The report displays changes in sensor operation as a table, including the mileage for the selected period.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object -** the vehicle registered in the system;
    
-   **Start -** the date and time operation started**;**
    
-   **End -** the date and time operation ended**;**
    
-   **Sensor** - the sensor name in the system;
    
-   **Value** \- the sensor operating state;
    
-   **Mileage** - the distance traveled by the Object, in km, during the specified period.
    

![](images/drex_otchet_o_rabote_mnogopozitsionnykh_datchikov_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_o_rabote_mnogopozitsionnykh_datchikov_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
