# **This report is used to process fuel dispensing data for fuel tankers and fuel depots.**

**The report contains:**

-   **Object -** the vehicle registered in the system;
    
-   **Operation** - the operation type;
    
-   **Start -** the date and time operation started**;**
    
-   **End -** the date and time operation ended**;**
    
-   **Start vol. (L)-** the initial fuel volume;
    
-   **End vol. (L) -** the final fuel volume;
    
-   **Refuel (L) -** the amount of fuel refueled;
    
-   **Dispensed (L)-** the amount of fuel dispensed;
    
-   **Fuel drain (L) -** the amount of fuel drained;
    
-   **Address** **-** the address.
