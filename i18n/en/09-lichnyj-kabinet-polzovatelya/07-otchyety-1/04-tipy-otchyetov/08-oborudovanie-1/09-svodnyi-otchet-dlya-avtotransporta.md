title: "Summary Report for Motor Vehicles"
---

This report displays data on

# **mileage, fuel consumption and speed.**

**The report contains:**

-   **Group -** the Object's group in the system;
    
-   **Object -** the vehicle registered in the system;
    
-   **Date -** the date and time operation started**;**
    
-   **Mileage (km) -** the distance traveled by the Object, in km, during the specified period;
    
-   **Mileage while speeding (km) -** the mileage driven while exceeding the speed limit;
    
-   **Engine-off time -** the engine operating time;
    
-   **Initial volume (L) -** the initial fuel volume;     
    
-   **Final volume (L)  -** the final fuel volume;
    
-   **Actual consumption (L) -** the amount of fuel the vehicle consumed over a given distance, measured in liters per 100 kilometers**.**
    
-   **Refuel volume (L) -** the amount of fuel the vehicle's fuel tank holds or the amount that was added to the tank during refueling;
    
-   **Fuel drain volume (L) -** the amount of fuel that was removed from the system, from the vehicle's fuel tank;
    
-   **Actual consumption per 100 (km) (L) -** an indicator showing how many liters of fuel the vehicle uses to travel 100 kilometers**;**
    
-   **Engine hours -** the unit of measurement for engine operating time;
    
-   **Actual consumption per engine hour (L)** - the amount of fuel the engine consumes in one hour of operation.
