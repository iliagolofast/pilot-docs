---
title: "Fuel Summary Report (Sensor and CAN)"
---

This report helps you control fuel consumption: you can view and compare fuel data received from the sensor and from the CAN bus, and easily spot possible fuel drains and discrepancies in readings.

![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchet_po_toplivu__datchik_i_can__custom.png)

The report is presented as a table in which, for each Object, you can see:

-   Mileage — how many kilometers the vehicle traveled during the selected period
    
-   Fuel level — how much fuel was in the tank at the beginning and at the end of the day
    
-   Refuels — how much fuel was added (by the sensor and by the CAN bus)
    
-   Fuel consumption — how much fuel was consumed during the trip (by the sensor and by the CAN bus)
    
-   Average consumption — how much fuel is used on average per 100 km (by the sensor and by the CAN bus)
    
-   Drained fuel — if any, how much fuel was drained
    

All information is broken down by day so you can track trends and notice any deviations.
