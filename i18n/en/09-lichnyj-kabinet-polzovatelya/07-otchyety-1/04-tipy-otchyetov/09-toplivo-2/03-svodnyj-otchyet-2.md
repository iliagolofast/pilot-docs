**Summary Report No. 2**

Summary Report No. 2 displays information on distance traveled, refuels, fuel drains, fuel consumption and engine hours for a given period.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the data is displayed.
    
-   **Mileage** - the distance traveled over the given period of time.
    
-   **Fuel level at start, L** - the amount of fuel at the beginning of the period.
    
-   **Fuel level at end, L** - the amount of fuel at the end of the period.
    
-   **Refuels** - the amount of fuel refueled during the given period.
    
-   **Fuel drains** - the amount of fuel drained during the given period.
    
-   **Fuel consumption** - the amount of fuel consumed during the given period.
    
-   **Consumption, L/100 km** - the average fuel consumption per 100 km during the given period.
    
-   **Fuel consumption per norm** - fuel consumption based on the fuel consumption norm set for the Object.
    
-   **Norm** - the calculated fuel consumption per 100 km based on the fuel consumption norm set for the Object.
    
-   **Engine hours** - the number of engine hours accumulated during the given period of time.
