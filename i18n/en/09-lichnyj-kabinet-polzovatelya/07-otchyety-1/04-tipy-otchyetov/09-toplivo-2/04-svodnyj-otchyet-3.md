**Summary Report No. 3**

Summary Report No. 3 displays information on refuels, fuel drains, speeding and idling for a given period.

**The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle number** - the name of the monitored object for which the information is displayed.
    
-   **Refuels** - the amount of fuel refueled, in liters.
    
-   **Fuel drains** - the amount of fuel drained, in liters.
    
-   **Speeding** - the date and time when speeding was recorded and the maximum speed recorded at that moment.
    
-   **Idling over 1 hour** - the duration, date and time of the recorded idling.
    
-   **Idling addresses** - the idling location.
