**Summary Report No. 4**

  This report displays information on refuels and distance traveled.

  **The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Distance (km)** - the distance traveled by the Object during the reporting period;
    
-   **Refuel** - the amount of fuel refueled, in liters, according to the sensor;
    
-   **Average fuel consumption** - the average fuel consumption per 100 km for the day;
    
-   **Idling duration -** the Object's idling duration during the selected period.
    
-   **Parking duration** - the Object's parking duration during refueling.

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchyet__4_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
