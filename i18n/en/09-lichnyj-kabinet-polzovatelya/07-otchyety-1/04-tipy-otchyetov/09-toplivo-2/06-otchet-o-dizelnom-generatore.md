---
title: "Diesel Generator Report"
---

The report displays generator operation, showing the operating period, engine hours, fuel consumption, refuels, fuel drains and hourly fuel consumption.

**The report contains:**

-   **Date -** the report start date;
    
-   **Period duration -** the duration of the period**;**
    
-    **Vehicle number** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Engine hours -** the unit of measurement for engine operating time;
    
-   **Fuel consumption by sensor -** an indicator reflecting the amount of fuel consumed by the vehicle, based on data received from the fuel level sensor**;**
    
-   **Refuels -** the amount of fuel refueled, in liters, according to the sensor;
    
-   **Fuel drains -** the amount of fuel drained, in liters, according to the sensor;
    
-   **Fuel consumption per hour -** an indicator showing how much fuel the engine consumes in one hour of operation.
