**Fuel Drains**

This report type displays information about fuel drains.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object on which a fuel drain was recorded.
    
-   **Date** - the date and time of the fuel drain.
    
-   **Fuel level at start (L)** - the fuel level at the start of the drain.
    
-   **Drained (L)** - the amount of fuel drained.
    
-   **Address** - the address where the fuel drain took place.
