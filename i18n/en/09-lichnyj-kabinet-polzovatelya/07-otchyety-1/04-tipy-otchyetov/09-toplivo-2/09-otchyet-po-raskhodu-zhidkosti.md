**Liquid Consumption Report**

The liquid consumption report presents information as a diagram showing the amount of liquid consumed at a given moment in time.

When you hover over a bar, additional consumption information pops up.

It is intended for pulse sensors only.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the diagram is built.
    
-   **Date** - the time period for which the diagram is built.
    
-   **Mileage, km** - the monitored object's mileage on the specified date.
    
-   **Total consumption, L** - the amount of fuel consumed on the specified date.
    
-   **Consumption, L/100 km (sensor/norm)** \- the amount of fuel consumed per 100 km of mileage according to the sensor readings (calculated as (Consumption \* 100 km)/Mileage) / the amount of fuel consumed per 100 km of mileage according to the set norm (the norm is set on the Fuel tab).
    
-   **Idle consumption** - the amount of fuel consumed while the monitored object has no speed.
    
-   **Consumption per engine hour, L/h** - the amount of fuel consumed per engine hour, calculated as Total consumption/Engine hours.
