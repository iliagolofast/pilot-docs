**Liquid Consumption Report (Table)**

The liquid consumption report (table) displays information on the liquid consumed during a given period of time.

This report type is intended for pulse sensors only.

**The report contains:**

-   **Date** - the time period for which the report is built
    
-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the corresponding data is shown in the report.
    
-   **Sensor** - the sensor name.
    
-   **Start** - the date and time the liquid flow started.
    
-   **Stop** - the date and time the liquid flow ended.
    
-   **Duration** - the duration of the liquid flow.
    
-   **Fuel** - the amount of fuel consumed.
