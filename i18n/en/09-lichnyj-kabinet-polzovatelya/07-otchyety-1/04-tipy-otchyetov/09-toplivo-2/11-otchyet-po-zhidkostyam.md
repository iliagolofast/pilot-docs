**Liquids Report**

This report type presents information as a chart showing changes in speed and liquid levels.

Refuels and fuel drains are marked with distinctive icons; click them to see additional information.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the chart is built.
    
-   **Date** - the time period for which the chart is built.
    
-   **Balance at start, L** - the amount of fuel in the tank at the beginning of the specified date.
    
-   **Balance at end, L** - the amount of fuel in the tank at the end of the specified date.
    
-   **Refueled, L** - the amount of fuel refueled on the specified date.
