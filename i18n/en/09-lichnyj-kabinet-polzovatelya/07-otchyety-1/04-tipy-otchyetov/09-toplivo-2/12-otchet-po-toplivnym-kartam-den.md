---
title: "Fuel Cards Report (Day)"
---

The fuel cards report displays data as a table with the number of the fuel card linked to the Object, the refueling date, the gas station number, the type and amount of fuel, as well as the price (with discount), the amount (with discount) and the card balance.

When this report type is selected, the breakdown and time refinement parameters are unavailable for configuration.

**The report contains:**

-   **Passenger car/Card** - the Object and the number of the card used for refueling.
    
-   **Date** - the date and time of refueling.
    
-   **Gas station** - the gas station where the refueling took place.
    
-   **Fuel** - the name of the fuel that was refueled.
    
-   **Operation** - the operation performed using the fuel card.
    
-   **Quantity** - the amount of fuel refueled.
    
-   **Refuel by sensor** - the amount of fuel refueled according to the fuel sensor readings (if a fuel sensor is installed
    
-   **Price/Price with discount** - the price of fuel per liter/the price of fuel per liter with the discount applied (if any).
    
-   **Amount/Amount with discount** - the total price of the refueling/with the discount applied (if any).
