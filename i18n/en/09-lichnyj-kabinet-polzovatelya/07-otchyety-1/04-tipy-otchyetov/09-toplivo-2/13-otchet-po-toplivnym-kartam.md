---
title: "Fuel Cards Report"
---

The fuel cards report displays data as a table with the number of the fuel card linked to the Object, the refueling date, the gas station number, the type and amount of fuel, as well as the price (with discount), the amount (with discount) and the card balance.

When this report type is selected, the breakdown and time refinement parameters are unavailable for configuration.

**The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle number/Card number** - the Object and the number of the card used for refueling.
    
-   **Date** - the date and time of refueling.
    
-   **Location** -  the place where the Object was refueled.
    
-   **Gas station** - the gas station where the refueling took place.
    
-   **Match** - the status shown when the refueled fuel is displayed correctly by the sensor and the fuel volume matches its transaction.
    
-   **Fuel** - the name of the fuel that was refueled.
    
-   **Operation** - the operation performed using the fuel card.
    
-   **Quantity** - the amount of fuel refueled.
    
-   **Refuel by sensor** - the amount of fuel refueled according to the fuel sensor readings (if a fuel sensor is installed).
    
    Report color code legend (by what percentage one number exceeds the other).
    

less than or equal to 3% \- green,

less than or equal to 5% \- blue.

less than or equal to 7% \- orange,

more than 7% \- red.

-   **Price** - the price of fuel per liter/the price of fuel per liter.
    
-   **Percentage** - the percentage of discrepancy between the amount of fuel refueled at the gas station and the amount of fuel according to the sensor.
    
-   **Amount/Amount with discount** - the total price of the refueling/with the discount applied (if any).
    
-   **Total value of refuels from the sensor****.**
    

**Additional notes on the report.**

This report has an important column, “Match”. In it you can see whether the gas station coordinates and the Object's refueling time match or not. If the Object was indeed refueled at the gas station, the report shows matching coordinates and the corresponding status. If the coordinates do not match, you can correct them by clicking Transactions, selecting the required transaction, and then selecting the “Change” or “Add gas station” option under the map. Even if the report has already shown a mismatch, you can go back to the previous period, change the location and refresh the report.
