---
title: "Pumps and Nozzles Summary Report"
---

A fuel pump report for refueling accounting.

**The report contains:**

-   **Pump -** the number of pumps
    
-   **Nozzle -** the number of nozzles**.**
    
-   **Fuel -** the fuel type.
    
-   **Total volumes -** the total volumes in liters at the start.
    
-   **Total volumes -** the total volumes in liters at the end.
    
-   **Total volumes difference -** the difference in total volumes in liters.
    
-   **Total dispensed volume -** the total dispensed volume in liters.
    
-   **Total amounts at start -**  the start of the total amounts.
    
-   **Total amounts at end -** the end of the total amounts.
    
-   **Total amount difference -** the difference in total amounts**.**
    
-   **Total dispensed amount** - the total amount.
