---
title: "Fuel Cards Summary Report"
---

This report displays information on the state of fuel consumption over a period of time.

**The report contains:**

-    **No.** \- the sequence number of the monitored object in the report.
    
-   **Vehicle** - the monitored object for which the reconciliation information is provided.
    
-   **Model** - the model of the monitored object.
    
-   **Vehicle number** - the license plate number of the monitored object.
    
-   **Distance** - the monitored object's mileage values at the start and end of the period for which the report is built.
    
-   Period - the mileage for the period for which the report is built.
    
-   Since the beginning of the year - the mileage since the beginning of the year.
    
-    **Engine hours** \- the number of engine hours of the monitored object.
    
-   Period - the number of engine hours for the period for which the report is built.
    
-   Since the beginning of the year - the number of engine hours since the beginning of the year.
    
-    **Consumption norm, L** - the fuel consumption norm of the monitored object (the norm is set on the Fuel tab).
    
-   Summer - the fuel consumption norm in summer.
    
-   Winter - the fuel consumption norm in winter.
    
-    **Fuel card** - the fuel cards of the monitored objects.
    
-   **Consumption (L)** \- the consumption of fuel of various grades.
    
-    **Fuel supplier** \- the fuel card suppliers.
    
-   **Distance** - the monitored object's mileage values at the start and end of the period for which the report is built.
