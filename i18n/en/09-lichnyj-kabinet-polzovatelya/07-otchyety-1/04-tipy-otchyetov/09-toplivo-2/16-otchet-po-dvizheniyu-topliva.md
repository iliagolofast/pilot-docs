**Fuel Movement Report**

  This report is used to account for fuel at the start of the day and at the end of the day.

  **The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Start** \- the time and date the vehicle started moving.
    
-   **Fuel at start of refueling (L)** - the fuel level at the start of the day;
    
-   **Refuel (L)** - the refuel in liters;
    
-   **Fuel after refueling (L)** - the fuel level at the end of the day.
    
-   **Stop** \- the time and date the vehicle stopped moving.
    
-   **Address** \- the address where the refueling took place.
