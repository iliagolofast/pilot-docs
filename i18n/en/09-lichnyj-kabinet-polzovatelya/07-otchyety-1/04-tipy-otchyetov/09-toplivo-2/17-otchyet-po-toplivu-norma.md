**Fuel Report (Norm)**

This report type contains information on fuel consumption calculated by the norm. Consumption is calculated as the norm multiplied by the mileage. The norm is set on the Fuel tab.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the corresponding information is displayed.
    
-   **Date** - the time period for which the corresponding information is displayed.
    
-   **Fuel type** \- the fuel type of the monitored object.
    
-   **Norm calculation type** - the monitored object's mileage on the specified date.
    
-   **Norm -** the fuel consumption norm of the specified monitored object.
    
-   **Fuel consumption** - the amount of fuel consumed by the monitored object on the specified date.
    
-   **Mileage** - the mileage of the specified monitored objects on the specified date.
    
-   **Engine hours**  - the number of engine hours on the specified date.
