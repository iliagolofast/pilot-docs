**Fuel Report (by Trips)**

The fuel report (by trips) displays fuel indicators for the duration of a trip.

**The report contains:**

-   **Date** - the date of the trip.
    
-   **Vehicle number** - the number of the monitored object.
    
-   **Distance, km** - the monitored object's mileage during the specified trip.
    
-   **Total time** - the duration of the specified trip.
    
-   **Start** - the place where the specified trip started.
    
-   **Stop** - the place where the specified trip ended.
    
-   **Fuel consumption** - the amount of fuel consumed by the monitored object during the specified trip.
    
-   **Refueled** - the amount of fuel refueled during the trip.
    
-   **Drained** - the amount of fuel removed during the trip.
    
-   **Average km/L** - the distance the monitored object covered on one liter of fuel.
    
-   **Average L/100 km** - the average fuel consumption per 100 km during the trip.
    
-   **Average L/h** - the average fuel consumption per hour of the trip.
