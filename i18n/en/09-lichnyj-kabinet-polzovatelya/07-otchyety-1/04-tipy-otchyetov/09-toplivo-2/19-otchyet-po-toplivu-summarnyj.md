**Fuel Summary Report**

This report type displays summary information on fuel consumption for the specified period.

Fuel data is generated from the fuel sensor readings.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the corresponding data is displayed.
    
-   **Date** - the date for which the corresponding data is displayed.
    
-   **Distance, km** - the monitored object's mileage on the specified date.
    
-   **Balance at start, L** - the amount of fuel in the tank at the beginning of the specified date.
    
-   **Balance at end, L** - the amount of fuel in the tank at the end of the specified date.
    
-   **Refueled, L** - the amount of fuel refueled on the specified date.
    
-   **Drained, L** - the amount of fuel drained on the specified date.
    
-   **Consumption, L** - the amount of fuel consumed on the specified date.
    
-   **Average consumption, L/100 km** - the amount of fuel consumed per 100 km of mileage, calculated as (Consumption \* 100 km)/Mileage.
    
-   **Engine hours** - the number of engine hours on the specified date.
    
-   **Average consumption, L/h** - the amount of fuel consumed per engine hour, calculated as Consumption/Engine hours.
