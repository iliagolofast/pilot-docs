---
title: "Fuel Report (Sensor)"
---

This report shows how speed and fuel level changed over the selected period. Use it to control fuel consumption and track refuels and fuel drains.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_toplivu__datchik__custom.png)

The report shows:

-   Object — the vehicle for which the chart is built
    
-   Date — the selected time period
    
-   Distance, km — how many kilometers the Object traveled during this period
    
-   Balance at start, L — how much fuel was in the tank at the start of the period
    
-   Refueled, L — how much fuel was refueled during this period
    
-   Balance at end, L — how much fuel remained in the tank at the end of the period
    
-   Drained, L — how much fuel was drained during this period
    
-   Total consumption, L — how much fuel was consumed in total during the period
    
-   Consumption, L/100 km (sensor/norm) — fuel consumption per 100 km according to the sensor data and the set norm (you can set the norm on the “Fuel” tab in the Object settings.)
    
-   Idle consumption — how much fuel was used while the vehicle was stationary with the engine running
    
-   Consumption per engine hour, L/h — how much fuel is consumed per hour of engine operation
    

The chart shows changes in speed and fuel level over the selected period:

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_toplivu__datchik__custom_2.png)

-   The blue line shows the driving speed (km/h)
    
-   The purple line shows the fuel level according to the sensor data (L)
    
-   The blue vertical bars are the periods when the ignition was on
    
-   Green jerrycan icons mark refueling moments. Hover over or click an icon to see the volume of fuel refueled
    
-   Red icons with a cross mark fuel drain moments. Hover over or click an icon to see the volume of fuel drained
    

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_toplivu__datchik__custom_3.png)

Below the chart there are tables of refuels and fuel drains that help you see when and where they took place, and check the fuel volumes and event addresses.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_toplivu__datchik__custom_4.png)

The refuels table shows all refuels for the period. For each refuel it lists:

-   Object — the vehicle
    
-   Date — the time of refueling
    
-   Fuel level at start, L — how much fuel was in the tank before refueling
    
-   Refuel, L — how much fuel was refueled
    
-   Fuel level at end, L — how much fuel there was after refueling
    
-   Address — the place where the refueling took place (a link to the map)
    

At the bottom of the table is the total amount of fuel refueled for the period

The fuel drains table shows all fuel drain cases. For each fuel drain it lists:

-   Object — the vehicle
    
-   Date — the time of the fuel drain
    
-   Fuel level at start, L — how much fuel there was before the drain
    
-   Drained, L — how much fuel was drained
    
-   Fuel level at end, L — how much remained after the drain
    
-   Address — the place where the fuel drain took place (a link to the map)
    
    At the bottom of the table is the total amount of fuel drained for the period.
