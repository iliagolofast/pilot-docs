---
title: "Fuel Cards Norm"
---

The fuel cards report displays data as a table for analyzing fuel consumption against the specified factory recommendations and the actual fuel consumption during operation of the Object.

**The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle number -** the number of the monitored object.
    
-   **Average factory consumption -** the fuel consumption declared by the vehicle manufacturer.
    
-   **Mileage -** the distance over the specified period of time.
    
-   **Consumption -** the fuel consumption.
    
-   **Actual fuel consumption -** the actual fuel consumption per 100 km.
    
-   **Fuel consumption difference -** the difference in fuel consumption, as a percentage.
