**Refuels**

This report type displays information about refuels of a monitored object.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object that was refueled.
    
-   **Date** - the date and time of refueling.
    
-   **Fuel level at start (L)** - the fuel level at the start of refueling.
    
-   **Refuel (L)** - the amount of fuel added.
    
-   **Fuel level at end (L)** - the fuel level after refueling.
    
-   **Address** - the address where the refueling took place.
