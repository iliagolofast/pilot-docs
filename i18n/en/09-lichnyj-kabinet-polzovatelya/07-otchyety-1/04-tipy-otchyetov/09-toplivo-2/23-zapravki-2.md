**Refuels No. 2**

  This report displays refuels together with geofences.

  **The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Date** - the time and date of refueling;
    
-   **Refuel** - how much was refueled (in liters);
    
-   **Mileage** - the vehicle's current mileage;
    
-   **Geofence** - the geofence in which the refueling took place.
    

![](images/drex_zapravki__2_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_zapravki__2_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
