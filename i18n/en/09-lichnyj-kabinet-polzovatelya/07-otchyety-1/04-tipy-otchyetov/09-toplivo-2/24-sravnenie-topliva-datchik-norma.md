The fuel report

# **sensor / norm**

displays fuel indicators for the duration of a trip, based on the norm set in the GPS monitoring system.

This report contains data on the Object, date, fuel type, average consumption and the difference.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the corresponding data is displayed.
    
-   **Folder -** the location of the Object in the system;
    
-   **Date** - the date for which the corresponding data is displayed.
    
-   **Fuel type** -  the fuel type;
    
-   **Fuel consumption** - the amount of fuel consumed on the specified date according to the sensor.
    
-   **Norm calculation type** - the type value;
    
-   **Average consumption, L/100 km** - the amount of fuel consumed per 100 km of mileage, calculated as (Consumption \* 100 km)/Mileage.
    
-   **Difference** -  the difference indicators in liters;
