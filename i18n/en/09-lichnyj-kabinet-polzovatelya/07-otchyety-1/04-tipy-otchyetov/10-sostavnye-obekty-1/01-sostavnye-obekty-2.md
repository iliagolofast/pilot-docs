title: "Composite Objects"
---

The “Composite Objects” report shows the daily fuel balance across the entire fuel scheme configured in the “Composite Objects” module. It helps you see how much fuel there was at the start of the day, how much was received, how much was consumed and how much remained overall for the composite object.

![](images/drex_sostavnye_obekty_2_custom.png)

### What the report shows

Each row of the table corresponds to one day of the scheme's operation and contains:

-   Date — the day for which fuel movement was calculated
    
-   Fuel at start — the estimated balance at the start of the day
    
-   Delivery — the volume of fuel received by the composite object during the day
    
-   Fuel consumption — the actual consumption based on the fuel level in the tanks
    
-   Consumption by flow meters — the total consumption recorded by the dispensing flow meters
    
-   Consumption difference — the difference between the consumption by level and the consumption by flow meters
    
-   Fuel at end — the estimated balance at the end of the day
    

At the bottom of the report there is a total row for the selected period with the total values of delivery, consumption and balances.
