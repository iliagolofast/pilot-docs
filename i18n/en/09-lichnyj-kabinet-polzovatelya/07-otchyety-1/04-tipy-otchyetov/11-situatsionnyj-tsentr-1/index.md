---
title: "Situation Center"
---

Reports of the [“Situation Center](../../../moduli-1/situatsionnyj-tsentr-2/index.html)” module help you view the events that occurred in the system during the selected period. They let you understand what happened to Objects, when it happened and whether the event was processed.

The reports show who processed an event, when it was done and what comments the operator added.
