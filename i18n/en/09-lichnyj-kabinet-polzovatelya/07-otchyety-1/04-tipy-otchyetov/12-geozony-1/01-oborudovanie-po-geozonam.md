---
title: "Hardware by Geofences"
---

This report displays data on hardware operation within a zone.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Hardware -**  the sensor name;
    
-   **Start -** the time and date the sensor started operating;
    
-   **End -** the time and date the sensor stopped operating;
    
-   **Duration  -**  the operating time;
    
-   **Distance  -** the monitored object's mileage in km on the specified date;
    
-   **Start location  -** where operation in the zone started;
    
-   **Stop location -**  where operation in the zone ended;
    
-   **Driver -** the driver's name in the system.
