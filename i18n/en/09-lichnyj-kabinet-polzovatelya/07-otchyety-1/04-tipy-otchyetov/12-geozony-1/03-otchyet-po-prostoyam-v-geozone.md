**Geofence Idling Report**

The geofence idling report is used to monitor idling of monitored objects in geographic zones. It is available when the additional Geofences module is enabled.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Date** - the time period for which the idling table is generated for the specified Object.
    
-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the idling table is generated.
    
-   **Geofence** - the geographic zone in which idling was recorded.
    
-   **Start** - the date and time idling recording started.
    
-   **End** - the date and time idling recording ended.
    
-   **Time in geofence** - the time the monitored object spent in the specified geofence.
    
-   **Idling setting** - the permitted idling time in the geofence (set in the Object settings on the “Geofences” tab, in the idling control field).
    
-   **Idling** - the idling time of the monitored object in the geographic zone. Equal to the difference between the start and end of idling minus the permitted idling time.
