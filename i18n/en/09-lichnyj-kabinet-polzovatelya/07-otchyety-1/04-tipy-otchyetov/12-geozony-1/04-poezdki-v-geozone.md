---
title: "Trips in Geofence"
---

The trips report is used for monitoring in geographic zones. The report contains data on the trip start and end date, duration, mileage, the zone recorded at the start and at the end, as well as coordinates.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the idling table is generated.
    
-   **Zone** - the geographic zone in which idling was recorded.
    
-   **Start** - the date and time the trip started.
    
-   **End** - the date and time the trip ended.
    
-   **Duration** - the trip duration during the reporting period;
    
-   **Mileage -** the distance in km over the specified period of time.
    
-   **Start location  -** where the trip started in the zone;
    
-   **Stop location -**  where the trip ended in the zone;
    
-   **Coordinates** - coordinates are used to determine a location by latitude and longitude.
