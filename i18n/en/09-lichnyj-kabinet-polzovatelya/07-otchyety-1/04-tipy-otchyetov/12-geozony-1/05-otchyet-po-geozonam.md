**Geofences Report**

The geographic zones report (1) displays data as a table showing the Object name, mileage, time, the message about arrival in or departure from a particular zone, and the duration of stay in the zone at a given moment in time.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle** - the monitored object for which the idling table is generated.
    
-   **Mileage -** the distance traveled at the given moment in time.
    
-   **Start** - the start time of the event in the geofence.
    
-   **Stop**\- the end time of the event in the geofence.
    
-   **Geofence** - the geofence name.
    
-   **Duration** \- the length of time spent in the geofence.
    
-   **Driver -** the name of the driver linked to the Object in the system.

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchyet_po_geozonam__custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
