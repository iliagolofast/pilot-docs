**Geofences Report No. 3**

Geofences report #3 is used to account for the Object's starting and ending geofences, with additional parameters on mileage and time.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Date** - the date of the event.
    
-   **Vehicle type** - the type of vehicle.
    
-   **License plate** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Start time** - the trip start time;
    
-   **Start zone** - the starting geofence;
    
-   **Trip duration** - the duration of the trip.
    
-   **Trip distance** \- the distance traveled during the selected period of time.
    
-   **Arrival time** - the time of arrival in the zone;
    
-   **End zone**  - the ending geofence.
    
-   **Departure time -** the time of departure from the zone;
    
-   **Duration in zone -** the length of time spent in the zone**;**
    
-   **Drum sensor work -** sensor operation**;**
    
-   **Drum sensor duration -** the sensor operating duration**;**
    
-   **Drivers** - the driver assigned to this monitored object.
