**Geofences Report No. 4**

Geofences report #4 is used to account for time spent in a geofence.

 It is available when the additional Geofences module is enabled.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Zone** - the zone the Object arrived in;
    
-   **Entry** - the time of entering the geofence;
    
-   **Exit** - the time of exiting the geofence;
    
-   **Duration** - the length of time spent in the geofence.
    
-   **Mileage (km)** \- the distance traveled.
    
-   **Total** \- the total values for duration and mileage.
    
    ![](images/drex_otchet_po_geozonam__4_custom.png)
    

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_po_geozonam__4_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
