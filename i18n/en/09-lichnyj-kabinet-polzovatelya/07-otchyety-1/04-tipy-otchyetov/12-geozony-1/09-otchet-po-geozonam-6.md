---
title: "Geofences Report No. 6"
---

A brief report on a trip through geofences showing the zone name, start, end and mileage for the specified period.

 **The report contains:**

-   **License plate** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Zone** - the zone the Object arrived in;
    
-   **Start** - the date and time movement started;
    
-   **Stop** - the date and time movement ended;
    
-   **Mileage (km)** \- the distance traveled.
    

![](images/drex_otchet_po_geozonam__6_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_po_geozonam__6_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
