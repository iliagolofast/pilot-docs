**Geofences Summary Report**

 This report is used to account for geofence visits by Objects.

**The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Geofence** - the geofence in which visits were recorded;
    
-   **First entry** - the Object's first entry into the geofence;
    
-   **Last exit** - the Object's last exit from the geofence;
    
-   **Number of visits** - the number of visits during the specified period of time;
    
-   **Total duration** - the total length of time spent in the geofence.
    

![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchet_po_geozonam_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchet_po_geozonam_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
