---
title: "Geofences Summary Report 2"
---

This report is used to account for geofence visits by Objects, with trip details.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** -  the vehicle of the monitored object;
    
-   **Zone** - the geofence in which visits were recorded;
    
-   **Start** \- the Object's first entry into the geofence;
    
-   **End** - the Object's last exit from the geofence;
    
-   **Duration** - the length of time spent in the geofence.
    
-   **Start location**  \- the zone at the start;
    
-   **Stop location** - the zone at the end;
    
-   **Coordinates** - coordinates are used to determine a location by latitude and longitude.
