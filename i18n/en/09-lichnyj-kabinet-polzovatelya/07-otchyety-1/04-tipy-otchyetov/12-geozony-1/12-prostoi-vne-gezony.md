---
title: "Idling Outside Geofences"
---

This report displays data on Objects that are outside geofences during the specified period.

![](images/drex_prostoi_vne_gezony_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Vehicle — the vehicle or monitored object
    
-   Driver — the driver assigned to the vehicle at the time of idling
    
-   Department — the group the Object belongs to
    
-   Object type — the type of vehicle (passenger car, truck, trailer, etc.)
    
-   Start — the date and time idling started
    
-   Stop — the date and time idling ended
    
-   Duration — how long the idling lasted (hh:mm:ss)
    
-   Address — the address of the idling location (according to the geocoder). The value is shown as a hyperlink: clicking it opens a map with the idling point
    
-   Coordinates — the exact latitude and longitude of the point
