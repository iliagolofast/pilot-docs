---
title: "Mileage Outside Geofences"
---

The report displays data on an Object's mileage outside geofences.

If an Object did not leave the geofences and has no mileage outside geofences, such mileage values are cut off and are not displayed in the report.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** -  the monitored object;
    
-   **Duration -**  the duration of the mileage;
    
-   **Mileage** \- the mileage in km for the reporting period.
    

![](images/drex_probeg_vne_geozon_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_probeg_vne_geozon_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
