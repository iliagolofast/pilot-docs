**Intersections in Geofences**

  This report displays information on Objects intersecting in geofences.

  **The report contains:**

-   **Geofence** \- the geofence name;
    
-   **Quantity** \- the number of Objects that intersected in the geofence;
    
-   **List of vehicles** \- the list of Objects that intersected in the geofence;
    
-   **Start** \- the start time of the intersection in the geofence;
    
-   **Stop** \- the end time of the intersection in the geofence.
    

![](images/drex_peresecheniya_v_geozonakh_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_peresecheniya_v_geozonakh_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
