---
title: "Parking in Geofences"
---

The report contains data on parking in geofences.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** \-  the vehicle of the monitored object;
    
-   **Zone** -   the zone where parking was recorded;
    
-   **Start** \- the time and date parking started;
    
-   **Stop** - the time and date parking ended;
    
-   **Duration** - the parking duration;
    
-   **Address  -**  the idling address;
    

![](images/drex_parkovki_v_geozonakh_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_parkovki_v_geozonakh_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
