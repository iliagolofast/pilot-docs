---
title: "Trips with Geofences"
---

This report displays information on trips between geofences.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Direction -** the direction of travel between geofences.
    
-   **Number of loads (number of trips) -** the number of trips between zones.
    
-   **Trip duration** - the duration of a trip from one zone to another.
