---
title: "Zone List Report"
---

A list of the account's geofences with addresses.

**The report contains:**

-   **Zone name -** the zone name in the system;
    
-   **Zone shape -** the shape of the zone;
    
-   **Zone address -** the geographic location of the zone;
    
-   **Zone group** - the name of the zone group.
    

![](images/drex_otchet_o_spiske_zon_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_o_spiske_zon_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.

![](images/drex_otchet_o_spiske_zon_custom_3.png)
