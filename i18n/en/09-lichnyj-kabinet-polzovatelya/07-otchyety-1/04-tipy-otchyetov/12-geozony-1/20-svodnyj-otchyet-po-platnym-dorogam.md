---
title: "Toll Roads Summary Report"
---

This report works provided that toll roads are configured in the Geofences module.

**The report contains:**

-   **Toll Road Name -** the name of the toll road.
    
-   **Entrance Time -** the entry time.
    
-   **Entrance Location -** the entry location.
    
-   **Exit Time -** the exit date and time.
    
-   **Exit Location -** the exit location.
    
-   **Price -**  the price per kilometer. The cost may vary depending on several factors, including the vehicle type, time of day, day of the week and the specific toll road.
    

![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchyet_po_platnym_dorogam_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_svodnyj_otchyet_po_platnym_dorogam_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
