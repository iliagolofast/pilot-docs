---
title: "Delivery - Plan vs. Actual"
---

This report compares planned delivery parameters with actual results, letting you identify deviations and improve processes

# .

**The report contains:**

-   **Number -** the order number from the Logistics module;
    
-   **Customer -** the customer name;
    
-   **Receipt number-** the receipt number from the list in the Logistics module;
    
-   **Status -** the request status;
    
-   **Group -** the customer group;
    
-   **Start address -** the starting address of dispatch;
    
-   **End address -** the end address.
    

![](images/drex_dostavka___plan_i_fakt_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_dostavka___plan_i_fakt_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
