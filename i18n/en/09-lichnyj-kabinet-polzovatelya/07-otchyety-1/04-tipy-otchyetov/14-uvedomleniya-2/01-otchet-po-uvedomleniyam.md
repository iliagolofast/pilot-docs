---
title: "Notifications Report"
---

This report informs you of any changes to a monitored object, according to the settings in the Notifications module.

The Notifications module provides conditions such as speed notification, ignition event, sensor malfunction and others.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** -  the monitored vehicle;
    
-   **Driver -** the driver assigned to the vehicle;
    
-   **Time -** the date and time the notification was received;
    
-   **Notification type -**    the notification name;
    
-   **Notification name -** the name of the notification;
    
-   **Message-** the notification message;
    
-   **Latitude -** the geographic coordinate that defines the position of a point on the map.
    
-   **Longitude -** the geographic coordinate that indicates the position of a point on the map.
    
-   **Location -** the location of the notification;
    
-   **Speed  -** the recorded speed**.**
