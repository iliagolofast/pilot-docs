---
title: "Detailed Notifications Report"
---

The detailed report contains data on drivers, type, date, time and speed at the moment the notification was triggered.

**The report contains:**

-   **Driver -** the driver assigned to the vehicle;
    
-   **Object** -  the monitored vehicle;
    
-   **Notification name -** the name of the notification;
    
-   **Notification type -**    the notification name;
    
-   **Message-** the notification message;
    
-   **Date -** the date of the event;
    
-   **Start time -** the date and time of the notification status;
    
-   **End time -** the date and time of the notification status;
    
-   **Number of occurrences** - the number of notifications;
    
-   **Event value** - the value;
    
-   **Speed control** \-  the speed value for the notification;
    
-   **Max speed  -** the maximum speed recorded in the notification;
    
-   **Duration** -  the duration.

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_podrobnyj_otchet_ob_uvedomleniyakh_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
