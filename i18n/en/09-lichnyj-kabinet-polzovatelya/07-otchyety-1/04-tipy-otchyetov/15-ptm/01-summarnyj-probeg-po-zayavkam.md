# **The summary report displays data on total mileage by requests.**

# **Driver requests are located in the PTM module.**

**The report contains:**

-   **Date -** the date and time the request was created;
    
-   **Vehicle** -  the monitored object;
    
-   **Folder -**  the location of the Object in the system;
    
-   **Distance -**  the distance value for requests and outside requests.
