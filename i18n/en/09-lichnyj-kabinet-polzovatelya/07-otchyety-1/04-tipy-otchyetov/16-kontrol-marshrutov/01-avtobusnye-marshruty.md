**Bus Routes**

 This report displays information on visits to specified stops (geofences).

 **The report contains:**

-   **Between geofences -** trips between zones;
    
-   **First departure -** the time of departure onto the route;
    
-   **Last arrival -**   the time of arrival from the route;
    
-   **Quantity -**  the number of visits.
