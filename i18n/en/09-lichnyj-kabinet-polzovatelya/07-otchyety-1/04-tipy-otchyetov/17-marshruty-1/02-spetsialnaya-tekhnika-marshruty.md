---
title: "Special Equipment Routes"
---

This report belongs to the Routes module. It displays data on the operation of special equipment.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle -**  the Object in the system;
    
-   **Route-** the route set for the equipment;
    
-   **Start -** the date and time work started;
    
-   **Start address -** the address where the equipment started work;
    
-   **End -** the date and time work ended;
    
-   **End address -** the address where the equipment ended work;
    
-   **Actual duration -** the duration value;
    
-   **Reference duration -** a standard or normative time value used as a benchmark for comparison and calibration**.**
    
-   **Route length -** the length of the route traveled;
    
-   **Distance outside the route -**  the distance traveled outside the route;
    
-   **Number of violations** - the number of violations on the route.
