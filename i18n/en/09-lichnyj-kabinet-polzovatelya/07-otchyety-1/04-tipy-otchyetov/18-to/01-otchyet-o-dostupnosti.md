---
title: "Availability Report"
---

The “Availability Report” shows the availability of vehicles during the selected period, taking registered breakdowns into account. Use it to determine the fleet size, the number of unavailable vehicles, the total downtime and the availability percentage.

To build the report, you need to configure the [“Service”](../../../moduli-1/servis/prostoi.html) module. If breakdowns and downtime periods were registered for vehicles, the fleet availability percentage decreases.

![](images/drex_otchyet_o_dostupnosti_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Object or user group — the name of the Object or group for which the indicators are calculated
    
-   Fleet size report — the total number of vehicles in the group
    
-   Vehicles Down — the number of vehicles that were unavailable during the selected period
    
-   Total number of downtime days — the total duration of vehicle unavailability
    
-   % availability based on breakdowns — the share of time during which vehicles were available for operation, taking registered breakdowns into account
