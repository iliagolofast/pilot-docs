# **The Fleet Status report displays general information about an Object.**

 **The report contains:**

-   **Make -** the make of the Object in the system;
    
-   **Model-** the model of the Object in the system**;**
    
-   **Year of manufacture-** the year of manufacture of the Object in the system**;**
    
-   **Mileage-** the total mileage of the Object;
    
-   **Driver name -**  the driver's name in the system;
    
-   **Driver change history -**  the history of drivers during operation;
    
-   **Next maintenance -** data on maintenance completion;
    
-   **Comment**\- additional information.
