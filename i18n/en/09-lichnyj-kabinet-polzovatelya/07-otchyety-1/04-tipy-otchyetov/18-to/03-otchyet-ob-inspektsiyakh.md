---
title: "Inspections Report"
---

The inspections report helps you monitor all completed service operations for each vehicle during the selected period.

It is convenient for tracking both completed work and scheduled services.

![](images/drex_otchyet_ob_inspektsiyakh_custom.png)

When building this report, you can choose which services are displayed:

![](images/drex_otchyet_ob_inspektsiyakh_custom_2.png)

-   All — all service events: completed and scheduled
    
-   Completed — the report displays only work that has already been done
    
-   Scheduled —  services that have been assigned but not yet performed
    

![](images/drex_otchyet_ob_inspektsiyakh_custom_3.png)

The report shows:

-   Which vehicle 
    
-   The service name, for example: oil change, brake check, cleaning
    
-   When the service was performed 
    
-   Difference in days — how much time has passed since the service
    
-   The mileage or distance at the time of the service
    
-   Status — done, completed or pending
    
-   Condition — one-time or recurring service
    
-   Next service
    
-   Costs — separately for parts and labor
    
-   Comments — additional notes
