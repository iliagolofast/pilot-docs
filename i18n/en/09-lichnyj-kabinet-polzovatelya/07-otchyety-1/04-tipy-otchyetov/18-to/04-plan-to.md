---
title: "Maintenance Plan"
---

A maintenance plan is a technical maintenance plan that defines the frequency and scope of inspection, adjustment, lubrication, replacement and repair work on equipment and vehicles.

A maintenance plan is necessary to ensure reliable and safe operation of Objects, extend their service life and improve performance, and reduce repair and operating costs.

A maintenance plan is drawn up on the basis of technical documentation, norms, standards and recommendations

from manufacturers.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Make -** the make of the Object in the system;
    
-   **Model-** the model of the Object in the system**;**
    
-   **Year of manufacture-** the year of manufacture of the Object in the system**;**
    
-   **Mileage-** the total mileage of the Object;
    
-   **Driver name -**  the driver's name in the system;
    
-   **Average monthly mileage -**  mileage data for the reporting period;
    
-   **Interval between maintenance, km -** the set interval after which scheduled maintenance of the vehicle must be performed**;**
    
-   **Date of last maintenance -**  the maintenance date;
    
-   **Last maintenance No. -** the maintenance number;
    
-   **Date of next maintenance** - the date of the next maintenance.
