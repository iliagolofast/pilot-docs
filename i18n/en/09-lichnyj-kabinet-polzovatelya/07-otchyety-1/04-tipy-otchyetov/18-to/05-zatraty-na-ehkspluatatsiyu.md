# **Operating costs are the total expenses associated with keeping equipment and vehicles operational and reliable.**

Technical characteristics, operating mode, operating conditions and other factors should be taken into account.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Make -** the make of the Object in the system;
    
-   **Model-** the model of the Object in the system**;**
    
-   **Year of manufacture-** the year of manufacture of the Object in the system**;**
    
-   **Driver name -**  the driver's name in the system;
    
-   **Mileage-** the total mileage of the Object in km;
    
-   **Current mileage-** the vehicle's mileage for the reporting period;
    
-   **Maintenance and repair -**  data on maintenance and work performed;
    
-   **Fuel costs -** fuel expenses;
    
-   **Total costs -**  the total of expenses.
