---
title: "Reminders Report"
---

The report contains summary data on service reminders.

Reminders are created in the Service module on the Reminders tab.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Name  -** the Object name in the system;
    
-   **Object** -  the Object in the system;
    
-   **Type-** the reminder type;
    
-   **Next due from now** -  the reminder due date;
    
-   **Repeat every -**  the set repeat value;
    
-   **Notify before** -  an advance reminder before the main reminder;
    
-   **Status -** the status: done, overdue;
    
-   **Current location** - the location.

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_o_napominaniyakh_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
