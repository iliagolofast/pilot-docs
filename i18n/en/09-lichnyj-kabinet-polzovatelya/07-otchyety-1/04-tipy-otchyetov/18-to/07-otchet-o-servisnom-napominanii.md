title: "Service Reminder Report"
---

A service reminder report is a report that informs a customer of the need to carry out maintenance or repair of their equipment or vehicle

# .

A service reminder report can be sent to the customer by mail, e-mail, SMS, messenger or any other convenient method.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Registration  -** the Object name in the system;
    
-   **Description -** the reminder name;
    
-   **Service type** \-  the types of service;
    
-   **Once every** \- the number of days of the reminder;
    
-   **Warning period -** the number of days of the reminder;
    
-   **SMS notification -** the number to which the notification was sent;
    
-   **E-mail notification -** the e-mail to which the notification was sent.
