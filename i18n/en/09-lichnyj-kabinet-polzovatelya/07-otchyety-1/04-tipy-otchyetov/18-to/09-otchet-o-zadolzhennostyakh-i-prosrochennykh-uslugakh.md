---
title: "Debts and Overdue Services Report"
---

The report on overdue services requires a configured Service module (services).

 **The report contains:**

-   **Account name**
    
-   **Vehicle registration number.**
    
-   **Mileage before service**
    
-   **Service date**
    
-   **Next service, km**
    
-   **Days overdue**
    
-   **Mileage over the allowed limit**
    

![](images/drex_otchet_o_zadolzhennostyakh_i_prosrochennykh_uslugakh_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_o_zadolzhennostyakh_i_prosrochennykh_uslugakh_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
