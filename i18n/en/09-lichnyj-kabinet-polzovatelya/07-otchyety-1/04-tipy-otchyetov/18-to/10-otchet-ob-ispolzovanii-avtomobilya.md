---
title: "Vehicle Usage Report"
---

The vehicle usage report is a report that provides data on how and how much a vehicle is used over a certain period of time. The report includes mileage information.

 **The report contains:**

-   **Object or user group**
    
-   **From 2500 km to 4500 km.**
    
-   **More than 4500 km.**
    

![](images/drex_otchet_ob_ispolzovanii_avtomobilya_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_ob_ispolzovanii_avtomobilya_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
