**RFID Groups Report**

The RFID group report is used to view interactions with tags by group.

 **The report contains:**

-   **UID** - the tag identifier;
    
-   **Name** - the tag name;
    
-   **Group** - the group the tag is linked to;
    
-   **Quantity** - the number of interactions between the Object and the tag;
    
-   **Date** - the time when the interaction with the tag took place;
    
-   **Address** - the address where the interaction with the tag took place.
