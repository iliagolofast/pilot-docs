**RFID Tags Report**

 **The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the Object that interacted with the tag;
    
-   **Driver** - the Object's driver;
    
-   **Time** - the time of interaction with the tag;
    
-   **UID** - the tag identifier;
    
-   **Tag** **Name** - the tag name;
    
-   **Group** - the group the tag is linked to;
    
-   **Location** - the place where the interaction with the tag took place.
