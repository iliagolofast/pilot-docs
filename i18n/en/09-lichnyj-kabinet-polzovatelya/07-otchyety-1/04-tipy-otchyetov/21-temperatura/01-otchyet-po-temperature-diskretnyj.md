---
title: "Discrete Temperature Report"
---

The “Discrete Temperature Report” contains readings from the temperature and humidity sensors of monitored objects at a set time interval.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_temperature_diskretnyj_custom.png)

Before building the report, select the reporting period and Objects, then enter a value in the **Min. time (sec)** field.

![Discrete Temperature Report](images/drex_otchyet_po_temperature_diskretnyj_screen.png)

The **Min. time (sec)** field defines the interval between the temperature readings shown in the report. The value is specified in seconds.

For example:

-   900 — temperature readings are shown at 15-minute intervals
    
-   1800 — temperature readings are shown at 30-minute intervals
    

## What the report shows

-   Date — the date and time the reading was recorded
    
-   Temperature or humidity sensor — the value recorded by the sensor
