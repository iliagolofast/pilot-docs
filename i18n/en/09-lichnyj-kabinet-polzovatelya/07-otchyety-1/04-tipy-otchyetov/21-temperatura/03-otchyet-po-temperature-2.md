---
title: "Temperature Report No. 2"
---

The “Temperature Report No. 2” contains temperature sensor readings for the selected period. Use it to track temperature changes, check that the temperature stays within the permitted range, and determine the Object's location and speed at the moment the reading was recorded.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_temperature__2_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Object — the name of the monitored object for which the report was generated
    
-   Date — the date and time the temperature reading was recorded
    
-   Event — the event associated with the recorded reading
    
-   Temperature — the temperature value according to the sensor data
    
-   Temperature Name — the name of the temperature sensor
    
-   Temperature Status — the status of the recorded temperature value
    
-   Temperature High — the upper limit of the permitted temperature range
    
-   Temperature Low — the lower limit of the permitted temperature range
    
-   Location — the Object's coordinates at the moment the reading was recorded
    
-   Speed — the Object's driving speed at the moment the reading was recorded
    
-   Location — the Object's address at the moment the reading was recorded
