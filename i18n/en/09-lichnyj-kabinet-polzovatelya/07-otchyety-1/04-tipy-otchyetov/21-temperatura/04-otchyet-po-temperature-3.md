---
title: "Temperature Report No. 3"
---

The “Temperature Report No. 3” contains readings from the temperature and humidity sensors configured on Objects for the selected period.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_temperature___3_custom.png)

### What the report shows

-   Object — the name of the monitored object
    
-   Date — the date and time the reading was recorded
    
-   Temperature or humidity sensor — the value recorded by the sensor
