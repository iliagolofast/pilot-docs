---
title: "Temperature Interval Report"
---

The “Temperature Interval Report” contains temperature and humidity sensor readings for the selected period. Use it to track changes in readings and to determine the Object's speed and location at the moment they were recorded.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_temperaturnomu_intervalu_custom.png)

The report can be built only for one Object, for a period of no more than one day and with an interval of less than 900 seconds.

### What the report shows

-   Object — the name of the monitored object
    
-   Date and time — the date and time the readings were recorded
    
-   Speed — the Object's driving speed at the moment the readings were recorded
    
-   Location — the Object's address at the moment the readings were recorded
    
-   Temperature — the value received from the temperature sensor
    
-   Humidity — the value received from the humidity sensor
