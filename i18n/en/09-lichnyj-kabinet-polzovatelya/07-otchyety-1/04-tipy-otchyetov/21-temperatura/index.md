---
title: "Temperature"
---

This section contains reports for analyzing readings from temperature and humidity sensors installed on vehicles and other monitored objects. They help you track changes in readings, detect when the temperature goes outside the permitted limits and compare sensor data with the Object's location.

With these reports you can:

— view temperature and humidity readings for the selected period

— analyze changes in readings as tables and charts

— check that the temperature stays within the permitted range

— configure the data display interval

— compare readings from several sensors

The set of indicators and available parameters depends on the selected report, the installed sensors and the Object settings.
