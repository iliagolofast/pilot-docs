**Speeding Report**

The speeding report displays speeding data as a table showing the Object, date, time, distance, the limit value and the speed excess (max. speed), and the amount of fuel used.

**The report contains:**

-   **Date** - the time period for which the data is displayed.
    
-   **Object** - the monitored object for which the data is displayed.
    
-   **Date and Time** - the date and time when speeding was recorded.
    
-   **Address -** the address of the monitored object where speeding was recorded.
    
-   **Coordinates** \- the latitude and longitude of the GPS coordinates on Google Maps.
    
-   **Duration** - the time during which the monitored object exceeded the speed limit.
    
-   **Distance, km** - the distance over which the monitored object exceeded the speed limit.
    
-   **Speed control, km/h** - the maximum permitted speed (set in the Object settings).
    
-   **Excess** - by how much the speed limit was exceeded.
    
-   **Max. speed** - the maximum recorded speed.
    
-   **Fuel consumption** \- the average fuel consumption per kilometer.
