**Speeding Report No. 2**

 This report is used to account for speeding by Objects and the drivers linked to them.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Driver** - the driver linked to the Object;
    
-   **Date and time** - the date and time of the violation;
    
-   **Address** - the address of the violation;
    
-   **Duration (s)** - the duration of the violation in seconds;
    
-   **Distance** - the distance traveled while violating the speed limit;
    
-   **Speed control** \- the speed limit set for the Object;
    
-   **Excess** - by how much the limit was exceeded;
    
-   **Max. Speed** - the Object's maximum speed at the time of the violation.
    
-   **Precent** - the speed limit excess expressed as a percentage.
    
-   **Fuel consumption (by norm)** \- the average fuel consumption per kilometer.
