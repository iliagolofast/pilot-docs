**Speed Limit Violation Report**

The summary report displays data on violations.

**The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Driver** - the driver assigned to this Object during the trip.
    
-   **Time** - the time and date of the event;
    
-   **Duration** - the duration of the event in seconds;
    
-   **Distance** \- the distance traveled while speeding, in km;
    
-   **Speed control** \- the speed limit that was exceeded;
    
-   **Max. speed** - the Object's speed at the time of speeding;
    
-   **Speed excess** - by how much the speed limit was exceeded;
    
-   **Percentage**\- by how much the speed limit was exceeded, as a percentage;
    
-   **Location** - the place where speeding occurred.
    
-   **Road type** - the road type.

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_o_narushenii_skorostnogo_rezhima_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
