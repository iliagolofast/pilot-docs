**Speed Limit Violation Report No. 2**

This report displays speed limit violations together with the drivers who were linked to the Object.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Driver** - the driver assigned to the Object;
    
-   **Time** - the time and date of the violation;
    
-   **Duration** - the duration of the violation;
    
-   **Distance (km)** - the distance traveled while speeding;
    
-   **Speed** **control (km/h)** - the speed limit that was exceeded;
    
-   **Speed** **excess** - by how much the limit was exceeded;
    
-   **Max.** **speed** - the Object's maximum speed;
    
-   **Location** - the place of the violation.
