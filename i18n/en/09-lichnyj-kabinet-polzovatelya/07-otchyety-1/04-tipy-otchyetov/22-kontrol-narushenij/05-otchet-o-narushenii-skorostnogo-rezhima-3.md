---
title: "Speed Limit Violation Report No. 3"
---

This report displays data on violations.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the monitored object in the system;
    
-   **Time -** the time and date of the violation;
    
-   **Duration  -** the duration of the violation**;**
    
-   **Distance (km)** - the distance traveled by the Object in km;
    
-   **Speed** **control (km/h)** - the speed limit that was exceeded;
    
-   **Speed** **excess** - by how much the limit was exceeded;
    
-   **Max.** **speed** - the Object's maximum speed;
    
-   **Location** - the place of the violation.
    
-   **Road   type** - defines the characteristics and functions of the road surface that affect vehicle driving conditions. The types are highways, expressways and non-expressway roads.
