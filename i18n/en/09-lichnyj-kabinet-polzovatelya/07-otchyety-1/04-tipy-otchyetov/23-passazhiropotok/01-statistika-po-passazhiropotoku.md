**Passenger Flow Statistics**

This report contains statistics on the number of passengers boarding and alighting, and on revenue.

On the infrared map, you can use checkboxes to highlight only boarding or only alighting passengers.

**The report contains:**

-   **Trips** - the number of trips made;
    
-   **Boarded** - the number of passengers who boarded;
    
-   **Alighted** - the number of passengers who alighted;
    
-   **Revenue** - the revenue received from passengers.
