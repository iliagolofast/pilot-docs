**Bus Lines Report**

The report works together with the Trips module and was created for scheduled and unscheduled stops of a vehicle.

**The report contains:**

-   **Trip No.** - the number of the trip since the start of the day.
    
-   **Zone entry time** - the time at which the vehicle entered the stop zone.
    
-   **Zone exit time** - the time at which the vehicle left the stop zone.
    
-   **Zone** - the zone between two stops.
    
-   **Passengers carried in this zone** - the number of passengers who boarded the bus at the beginning of the zone.
    
-   **Passengers carried from other zones** - the number of passengers who boarded the bus at the beginning of previous zones.
    
-   **Plan** - the revenue plan for this stop.
    
-   **Amount** - the revenue at this stop.
