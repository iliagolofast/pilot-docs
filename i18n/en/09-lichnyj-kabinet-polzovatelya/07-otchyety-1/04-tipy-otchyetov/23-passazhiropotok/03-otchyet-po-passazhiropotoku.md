**Passenger Flow Report**

The passenger flow report lets you keep a record of passengers boarding and alighting and of their number inside the bus while it travels along the route.

**The report contains:**

-   **Time** - the time the door opened.
    
-   **Door** - the name of the door (front, rear).
    
-   **Boarded** - the number of passengers who boarded.
    
-   **Alighted** - the number of passengers who alighted.
    
-   **On board** - the number of passengers on board.
    
-   **Stop -** the name of the stop.
    
-   **Location** - the place where passengers boarded or alighted.
    
-   **Mileage** \- the distance between the start and end points of the route.
    
-   **Duration** - the time from the start to the end of the route.
    
-   **Distance** - the distance between the start and end points of the route.
