**Passenger Flow Report (City Bus)**

The passenger flow report (city bus) lets you keep a record of passenger flow and fare payments on a bus line with a fixed fare (urban transport).

**The report contains:**

-   **Time** - the time the door opened.
    
-   **Door** - the number of the door through which passengers boarded.
    
-   **Boarded** - the number of passengers who boarded.
    
-   **Location** - the place where passengers boarded.
    
-   **Price** \- the fare.
    
-   **Amount** - the amount of money in the cash box for passengers boarding at this location (equal to the price multiplied by the number of passengers who boarded).
    
-   **Debug** -ts: the event time as a timeStamp, id: the video index in the system.
    
-   **Total amount** \- the amount of money in the cash box for the day (the selected period of time).
