**Passenger Flow Report (City Bus No. 2)**

The passenger flow report (city bus) lets you keep a record of passenger flow and of scheduled and unscheduled stops.

**The report contains:**

-   **Lap** - the lap the Object makes along the route;
    
-   **Object** - the monitored object;
    
-   **Scheduled** - indicates whether the stop was scheduled or unscheduled;
    
-   **On** **route** - whether the bus is on the route or not;
    
-   **Date** **and** **time** - the date and time of the stop;
    
-   **Boarded** - the number of passengers who boarded at the stop;
    
-   **Alighted** - the number of passengers who alighted at the stop;
    
-   **Stop** **name** - the name of the stop;
    
-   **Coordinates** - the coordinates of the stop.
