---
title: "Passenger Transport Report"
---

**The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle -** the Object in the monitoring system.
    
-   **Boarding -** the number of passenger boardings.
    
-   **Alighting -** the number of passenger alightings.
    

![](images/drex_otchet_po_passazhirskim_perevozkam_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_otchet_po_passazhirskim_perevozkam_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.

![](images/drex_otchet_po_passazhirskim_perevozkam_custom_3.png)
