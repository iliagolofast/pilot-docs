**Round Trips Report No. 2**

Round Trips Report No. 2 displays information on complete and incomplete trips and on the difference between passengers boarding and alighting along the monitored object's route.

A trip is considered complete if all "anchor" stops were visited (set by selecting the checkbox on the Trips tab).

  **The report contains:**

-   **Lap** - the number of the lap made by the bus;
    
-   **Lap** **start** - the lap start time;
    
-   **Lap** **end** - the lap end time;
    
-   **Duration** - the lap duration;
    
-   **Boarded between finish and start** - the number of passengers who boarded before the start of a new lap;
    
-   **Alighted at start** - the number of passengers who alighted at the beginning of the lap;
    
-   **Boarded at start** - the number of passengers who boarded at the beginning of the lap;;
    
-   **Alighted between start and finish** - the number of passengers who alighted during the lap;
    
-   **Difference** - the difference between the number of passengers who alighted and boarded;
    
-   **Full lap** - indicates whether all anchor stops were visited.
