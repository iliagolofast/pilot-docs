**Trips Report**

A report for recording a vehicle's trip. It works together with the Trips module.

**The report contains:**

-   **Object** - the selected Object;
    
-   **Route** **number** - the route linked to the Object;
    
-   **Route** **name** - the name of the route linked to the Object;
    
-   **Stop** **coordinates** - the coordinates of the Object's stop;
    
-   **Stop** **name** - the name of the stop where the Object stopped;
    
-   **Date** - the date when the stop was made;
    
-   **Reference time** - reference information on the route time.
    
-   **Stop** **arrival** **time** - the time the Object arrived;
    
-   **Door** **opening** **sensor** - indicates whether the door was opened;
    
-   **Boarded** - the number of passengers who boarded;
    
-   **Alighted** - the number of passengers who alighted.
