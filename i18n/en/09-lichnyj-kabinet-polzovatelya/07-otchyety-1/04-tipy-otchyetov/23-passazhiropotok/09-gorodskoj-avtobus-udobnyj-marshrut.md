---
title: "City Bus + Convenient Route"
---

This report is active when the Convenient Route module is enabled.

**The report contains:**

-   **Time-** the time and date of the trip.
    
-   **Door -** the door number.
    
-   **Boarded -** the number of passengers.
    
-   **Stop -** the stop.
    
-   **Location -** the location.
    
-   **Price -** the fare.
    
-   **Amount -** the price.
    
-   **Convenient Route system data-** reading of various types of tickets.
    

![](images/drex_gorodskoj_avtobus___udobnyj_marshrut_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_gorodskoj_avtobus___udobnyj_marshrut_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
