---
title: "Convenient Route Summary"
---

This report is active when the Convenient Route module is enabled.

**The report contains:**

-   **Vehicle-** the monitored object in the system.
    
-   **Carried -** the number of passengers and the amount.
    
-   **Validator data -** the reader of various ticket types.
    
-   **Difference-** the number of passengers and the difference in amounts.
    
-   **Trips -** the number of trips per shift.
    

![](images/drex_summarnyj_po_udobnomu_marshrutu_custom.png)

If necessary, you can hide certain parts of the report by clicking the icon ![](images/drex_summarnyj_po_udobnomu_marshrutu_custom_2.png). A menu opens in which you can turn individual fields on or off.
