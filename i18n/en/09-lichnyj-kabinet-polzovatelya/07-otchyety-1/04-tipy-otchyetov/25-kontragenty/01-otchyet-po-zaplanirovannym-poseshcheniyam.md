---
title: "Planned Visits Report"
---

This report helps you see how sales representatives visit customers. It shows which points were visited, exactly when the visits took place, and how much time the representative spent with the customer.

With the report you can:

— check which customers were visited during the selected period

— view the agent's arrival and departure times

— assess the duration of visits

— compare the work of different sales representatives

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_zaplanirovannym_poseshcheniyam_custom.png)

### What the report shows

The top part of the report shows:

— the period for which the report was generated

— the date the report was generated

Below is a table. Each row is a separate customer visit.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_zaplanirovannym_poseshcheniyam_custom_2.png)

### Report fields

No. — the row number in the report

Customer ID — the customer's internal number in the system

Customer name — the name of the retail outlet or customer

Start time — when the sales representative arrived at the customer

Departure time — when the representative left the customer

Duration — how long the visit took

Agent — the sales representative who made the visit
