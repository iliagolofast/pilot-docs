---
title: "Counterparties Report"
---

The counterparties report shows how a sales representative visited customers during the selected period. It helps you understand which counterparties the agent visited, what time they arrived and left, and how much time they spent at each point.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_kontragentam_custom.png)

### What the report shows

The top part of the report shows:

— the period for which the report was generated

— the date and day of the week

— the Object — the sales representative for whom the report is built

Below is a table. Each row in the table is a separate counterparty visit.

![](images/drex_otchyet_po_kontragentam_custom_2.png)

### Report fields

Counterparty — the name of the customer or retail outlet in the system

Start — the time the sales representative arrived at the customer

End — the time the representative left the customer

Duration — how long the visit took

Address — the counterparty's actual address

Below the table, summary information is usually shown:

Total time — the total time of all visits during the selected period

Unique visits — the number of different counterparties visited by the sales representative
