---
title: "Counterparties"
---

This section contains reports for the [“Counterparties”](../../../moduli-1/torgovyj-predstavitel.html) module, also known as “Sales Representative”. They help you analyze the work of sales representatives and monitor customer visits.

The reports show which retail outlets were visited, when the visits took place, and how much time the representative spent with the customer.

With the reports you can:

— monitor customer visits

— see the actual time of visits

— analyze the length of work at points

— compare the work of different sales representatives
