---
title: "How to Configure Access to Reports"
---

Access to reports created in the builder is configured through user rights.

-   In the Contract configuration, go to the [“Staff and Groups”](../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/personal-i-gruppy/index.html) tab
    

![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_dostup_k_otchyetam_2_custom.png)

2\. Open the user card — double-click the user

3\. On the “Rights” tab, select the checkbox for the report builder rights

![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_dostup_k_otchyetam_2_custom_2.png)

4\. Click “Save”

You can also define which specific reports will be available to a user:

1\. On the [“Staff and Groups”](../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/personal-i-gruppy/index.html) tab, select the user in the list and click the icon ![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_dostup_k_otchyetam_2_custom_3.png)

![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_dostup_k_otchyetam_2_custom.png)

2\. A window with a list of reports opens

3\. Select the checkboxes next to the reports you are granting this user access to

![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_dostup_k_otchyetam_2_custom_4.png)
