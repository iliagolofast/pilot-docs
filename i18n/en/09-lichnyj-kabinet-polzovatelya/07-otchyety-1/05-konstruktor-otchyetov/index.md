---
title: "Report builder"
---

The report builder is a tool that lets you create your own reports in the monitoring system to suit your tasks.

It helps you configure which data to show, how to display it, and which blocks the report consists of.

You configure the report form once, save it, and then reuse it whenever you need the information in a convenient format.
