title: "How to configure tariffs"
---

On the “Tariffs” tab, you specify the cost of operating the connected hardware.

**![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_tarify_custom.png)**

A tariff defines how much it costs to connect and operate each device.

Select the tariff you need from the list for each manufacturer and save the settings.

To configure tariffs for devices:

-   In the admin panel, go to the “Contracts” tab
    
    2\. Select the contract you need
    
    3\. Right-click the contract and select “Edit”
    

![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_tarify_custom_2.png)

4\. Go to the “Tariffs” tab

**![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_tarify_custom.png)**

5\. Select a suitable tariff for the device

6\. Click “Save”
