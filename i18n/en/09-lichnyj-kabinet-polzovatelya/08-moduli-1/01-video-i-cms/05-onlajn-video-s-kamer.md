After you have [saved the cameras](podklyuchenie-videoustrojstva-v-kabinete-polzovatelya.html) in the object card, they become available in the system.

In the object list, a camera icon is displayed next to the vehicle name:

-   Green camera: the device is active, video is available
    
-   Gray camera: the device is inactive
    

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom.png)

## How to view online video

1\. In the object list, click the camera icon

![Online video from cameras](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_screen.png)

2\. A video player with a real-time stream opens

The player shows all available camera channels (for example: road, cabin, side or rear views, depending on the hardware).

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_2.png)

The video displays:

-    the duration of the current online stream
    

• recording date and time

• channel number

• GPS coordinates: latitude and longitude

• driving speed in km/h

### Streaming time limit

For online video, you can set the stream to stop automatically after a specified time. This lets you limit viewing duration and control how long live video runs.

To set the limit, click the settings icon ![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_3.png) in the upper-right corner of the video player.

![Online video from cameras](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_screen_2.png)

The “Streaming time limit” window opens. In it you can enable automatic stopping and specify the stream duration.

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_4.png)

To enable automatic stopping, select the “Automatically stop streaming” check box.

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_5.png)

Then specify:

• Duration: how long the stream runs before it stops

• Unit: the unit of measurement, for example minutes or hours

After setting it up, click “Save”.

The video player header will show the duration of the current online stream in the format:

Live duration: current time / set limit

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_6.png)

For example, Live duration: 00:03:15 / 00:30:00 means that the stream has been running for 3 minutes and the set limit is 30 minutes.

If automatic stopping is enabled, the system ends the online video after the specified time.

### Controlling the video player

In the player you can use the following functions:

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_7.png) Start / ![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_8.png) Stop: start or stop the stream

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_9.png) Sound: adjust the volume or mute it

![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_10.png) Screenshot: capture the current frame

🔲 Full screen: expand the player to full screen

### Two-way communication

In the upper-right corner of the player there is an intercom icon ![](images/drex_onlajn_video_s_kamer_custom_11.png). With this function you can communicate with the driver in both directions: hear the driver and speak to them.
