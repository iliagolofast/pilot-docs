title: "Viewing cameras on the Dashcam panel"
---

The **Dashcam** section in the main menu lets you watch the cameras of several objects at the same time. For example, you can display images from the cabins of different vehicles on one screen and quickly switch to the camera you need.

You can open up to 64 video streams on the panel. The cameras of each object are grouped into a separate block.

## Selecting cameras

The Camera management list shows all objects with cameras.

To see an object’s cameras, expand its row. The connected cameras appear under the object name with their names.

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_9.png)

## Building the panel

To open cameras:

1\. Go to the **Dashcam** tab

2\. In the **Camera management** area, expand the folder you need and find the object

3\. Expand its row and select the check boxes next to the cameras you want to open

You can select individual cameras or all available cameras of the object.

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_9.png)

4\. Check the number of selected cameras at the top of the panel

5\. Click Build dashboard

Separate blocks for the selected objects appear in the workspace. Each block shows the selected cameras.

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_10.png)

The total number of cameras open at the same time cannot exceed 64.

If the panel is already built, you do not need to create it again to add another object. Double-click the row of the object you need in the **Camera management** area. Its cameras are added to the workspace, and previously opened streams continue to run without restarting.

## Adjusting the zoom

With the **Zoom** slider you can change the size of all open video blocks at once:

— zoom out to fit more objects on the screen

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_11.png)

— zoom in to see the image in more detail

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_12.png)

## Viewing a camera in full screen

To take a closer look at the image, double-click the camera you need. It expands to full screen.

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_13.png)

To return to the general camera grid:

 — double-click the image again

— or press Esc

## Hiding a single camera

If you do not need the image from a particular camera for a while, you can hide it. To do this, click the **×** icon in the upper-right corner of the corresponding video stream.

![Viewing cameras on the Dashcam panel](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_screen.png)

After hiding:

— the camera is no longer displayed in the block

— its active stream stops

— the other cameras of the object continue to work

— the size and layout of the block do not change; a static area takes the camera’s place.

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_20.png)

Stopping unneeded video streams helps reduce internet traffic consumption and the load on your computer.

## Restoring hidden cameras

To bring back a hidden camera:

1\. Click the settings icon in the object block header

![Viewing cameras on the Dashcam panel](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_screen_2.png)

2\. Find the camera you need in the list of hidden cameras

3\. Select it to start displaying it again

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_21.png)

To restore all hidden cameras of the object at once, select **Show All**.

If the block has no hidden cameras, the menu displays the message **No hidden cameras**.

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_22.png)

## Closing an object’s video block

To close a video block completely, click the **×** icon in its header.

![Viewing cameras on the Dashcam panel](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_screen_3.png)

The block is removed from the workspace, and all its active video streams stop. The cameras of other objects continue to work.

## Streaming time limit

To keep streams from staying on longer than necessary, you can configure them to stop automatically.

1\. Click the settings icon on the right ![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_8.png)

![Viewing cameras on the Dashcam panel](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_screen_4.png)

2\. The “Streaming time limit” window opens

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_23.png)

3\. To enable automatic stopping, select the “Automatically stop streaming” check box.

![](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_custom_24.png)

Then specify:

• Duration: how long the stream runs before it stops

• Unit: the unit of measurement, for example minutes or hours

4\. Click “Save”

After saving, the current viewing time and the set limit are displayed next to **Live duration**:

![Viewing cameras on the Dashcam panel](images/drex_prosmotr_kamer_na_paneli_dashcam_screen_5.png)

When the set time runs out, PILOT automatically stops the streams.

If you do not need automatic stopping, clear the **Automatically stop streaming** option and save the settings.
