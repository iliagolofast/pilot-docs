The “Video” and “CMS” modules let you connect different types of video devices to the system: dash cams, vehicle cameras, and body cameras. With them you can view online streams and archived recordings, as well as receive telematics data. Mettax cameras can also send [driver face recognition results](../voditeli-3/opredelenie-voditelya-po-face-id.html) to PILOT.

These modules work together: to connect cameras and dash cams correctly, you need to activate both modules.
