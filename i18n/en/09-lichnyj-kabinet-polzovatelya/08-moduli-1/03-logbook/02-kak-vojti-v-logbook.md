title: "How to log in to LogBook"
---

To log in to LogBook:

-   Follow the link: [logbook.pilot-gps.com](http://logbook.pilot-gps.com/)
    

2\. Enter your login credentials: login and password

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_logbook_custom.png)

3\. Click **Continue**. You will then enter the system and can start using all its functions

## If you need to change your password

If you forgot your password or want to change it, follow these steps:

1\. Click **Reset password**

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_logbook_custom.png)

2\. Enter your email address and click **Reset password** again

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_logbook_custom_2.png)

3\. An email with a password reset link is sent to your address

4\. Follow the link, enter a new password, repeat it, and click **Continue**.

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_logbook_custom_3.png)
