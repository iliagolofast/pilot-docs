## Top panel

The main portal [sections](kak-rabotat-s-razdelami.html) are at the top of the screen:

-   Trips – a list of all trips for the selected period
    
-   Events – individual driving events (hard braking, speeding, and so on)
    
-   Fuel – data on refuels and fuel consumption
    
-   Tolls – detailed information on each toll section
    
-   Media – photos or videos associated with trips
    

The upper-right part of the screen contains:

-   Email – the current user
    
-   [Settings](kak-rabotat-s-nastrojkami-v-logbook.html) – access to account settings
    

![](images/drex_obzor_interfejsa_portala_custom_2.png)

-   Logout – log out of the system
