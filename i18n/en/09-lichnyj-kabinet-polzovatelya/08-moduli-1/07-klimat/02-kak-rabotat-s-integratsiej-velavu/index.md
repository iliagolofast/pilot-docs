---
title: "How to work with the Velavu integration"
---

Velavu is an indoor positioning system that lets you track the location of objects inside buildings. It uses fixed positioning points — anchors — and mobile tags to determine the location of equipment, vehicles, assets, or personnel on a building or floor plan.

The Velavu integration with PILOT lets you work with this data in the system interface.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_integratsiej_velavu_custom.png)

With the Velavu integration in PILOT, you can:

— load the structure of sites, buildings, and floors from Velavu

— see the location of objects inside buildings

— open detailed information about devices

— track movement history
