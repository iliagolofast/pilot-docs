---
title: "Climate"
---

The “Climate” module is designed to monitor temperature and humidity on monitoring objects where temperature and humidity control is important, for example, residential premises, warehouses, offices, and cold rooms.

In standard mode of the “Climate” module, you can:

— view the current sensor values

— see the date and time of the last update

— track changes in readings

— display sensors on the room plan

![](images/drex_klimat_custom.png)

When the Velavu integration is connected, the “Climate” module can also be used for indoor positioning — tracking objects inside buildings.

In this mode, the module displays Velavu objects, their location on the plan, status, and movement history.

![](images/drex_klimat_custom_2.png)
