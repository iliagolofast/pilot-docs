---
title: "Creating a geofence"
---

To create a geofence:

-   In the “Main” section, select the “Geofences” tab
    

![Creating a geofence](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_screen.png)

2\. Create a zone group

For convenient organization, you can group geofences by region or type:

1) Click the group icon ![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom.png)

2) Enter the group name and click “Save”

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_2.png)

3\. Select the zone type and add the zone:

![Creating a geofence](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_screen_2.png)

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_3.png)Polygon — mark the vertices of the polygon on the map

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_4.png)

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_5.png) Circle — hold down the left mouse button and drag to set the radius of the circle. You can change the radius by dragging the markers on the circumference. You can move the zone by dragging the center

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_6.png)

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_7.png) Line —  mark two or more points on the map to build the segment or route you need

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_8.png)

4\. Enter the zone name and choose a color for it

![](images/drex_sozdanie_geozony_custom_9.png)

5\. Click “Save” — the geofence appears in the list
