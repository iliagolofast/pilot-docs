---
title: "Deleting a geofence"
---

### Deleting a single zone

To delete an unneeded geofence, find it in the list and click the trash icon ![](images/drex_udalenie_geozony_1_custom.png) next to its name. Confirm the deletion, and the zone is removed from the list and no longer displayed on the map.

![](images/drex_udalenie_geozony_1_custom_2.png)

### Deleting several zones at once

If you want to delete several geofences at once:

-   Hold down the Ctrl key and select all the zones you need in the list.
    
    2\. Then right-click the selected zones and click “Delete”
    

![](images/drex_udalenie_geozony_1_custom_3.png)

3\. Confirm the deletion
