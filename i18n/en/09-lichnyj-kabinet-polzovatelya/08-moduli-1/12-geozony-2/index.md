title: "Geofences"
---

The “Geofences” module is designed for territorial control of monitoring objects.

With it, you can:

— track when an object enters or leaves a specific zone

— monitor idle time in a zone and receive notifications when the allowed time is exceeded

— get reports on the movements and actions of objects in specified areas

### What is a geofence?

A geofence is a designated area on the map that you create to monitor the movement of objects.

A geofence can have any shape:

-   Polygon — for example, the territory of a company or an entire city
    

![](images/drex_geozony_2_custom.png)

-   Circle — a zone with an arbitrary radius
    

![](images/drex_geozony_2_custom_2.png)

-   Line — for example, a specific street or road section
    

![](images/drex_geozony_2_custom_3.png)

You can configure automatic [notifications](../uvedomleniya/index.html) about entering or leaving a geofence, and view reports on all geofence-related events in the [“Reports”](../../otchyety-1/tipy-otchyetov/geozony-1/index.html) section.

To create and work with geofences, you must have the appropriate [access rights](../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/personal-i-gruppy/index.html#права_доступа).
