---
title: "Toll roads"
---

Toll roads is a function of the “Geofences” module that accounts for road sections with tolls. 

Use “Toll roads” to:

— control expenses — always know when and how much your objects traveled on toll roads

— plan routes — account for tolled sections in advance when building routes

— analyze trips — toll road data is saved and available in the [report](../../otchyety-1/tipy-otchyetov/geozony-1/svodnyj-otchyet-po-platnym-dorogam.html)
