---
title: "Tire history"
---

The tire history shows when and where a tire was installed on or removed from an object. To open the history, right-click the object on the “Tires” tab and select “History”.

![](images/drex_istoriya_shin_custom.png)

The history shows:

• Installation time — the date and time the tire was installed

• Uninstall Time — the date and time the tire was removed

• Axle Position — the axle position

• Tire Position — the tire position

• Mileage — the mileage at the time of installation or removal
