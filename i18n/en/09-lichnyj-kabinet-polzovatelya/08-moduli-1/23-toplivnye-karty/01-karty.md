On this tab, you can add and view fuel cards.

![](images/drex_karty_custom.png)

## How to add a card

1\. On the “Cards” tab, click “Add card” ![](images/drex_karty_custom_2.png)

2\. In the new window:

![](images/drex_karty_custom_3.png)

-   Enter the card number
    
-   Select the gas station type (fuel network)
    

![](images/drex_karty_custom_4.png)

-   In the “Objects” field, select one or more objects that use this card. For example, if one card pays for refuels of a tractor unit and a trailer, select both objects.
    

To save the card, you must select at least one object. After saving, the card is available in the details of each linked object.

-   Check the date added — it is filled in automatically, but you can change it if necessary
    
    3\. Click “Save”
    

![](images/drex_karty_custom_5.png)

After saving, you can open the fuel card information.

To do this, right-click the card in the list twice — a card with details then appears on the right side of the screen.

![](images/drex_karty_custom_6.png)

![](images/drex_karty_custom_7.png)

You can also save the card information in PDF format — click the “Save” button
