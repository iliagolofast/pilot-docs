---
title: "Displaying gas stations on the map"
---

On the main PILOT map, you can view the gas stations of connected fuel networks. You can see the location of fueling stations and check which of them are near objects.

The coordinates of added gas stations are also used [when checking operations](tranzaktsii.html#Проверка_совпадения_координат) with fuel cards. PILOT compares the object's location at the time of the operation with the location of the specified gas station.

![](images/drex_otobrazhenie_azs_na_karte_custom.png)

## How to display gas stations

1\. Open the “Refuel types” list on the map

2\. Select the check boxes next to the fuel networks you need

The gas stations of the selected networks appear on the map ![](images/drex_otobrazhenie_azs_na_karte_custom_2.png). To hide the stations of a particular network, clear the corresponding check box.

![](images/drex_otobrazhenie_azs_na_karte_custom_3.png)

The list shows only the fuel networks [connected for the account](podklyuchenie-spravochnika-azs.html). If the network or gas station you need is missing, contact technical support.

To add a gas station, prepare:

— the fuel network name

— the address

— latitude and longitude

Technical support will check the data and add the gas station to the system.

## Coordinate check during an operation

After a gas station is added, its coordinates are taken into account when checking transactions. To see the result:

1\. Go to the “Fuel cards” module

2\. Open the “Transactions” tab

3\. Select the operation you need in the list

4\. Look at the [“Coordinate match”](tranzaktsii.html#Проверка_совпадения_координат) field in the operation details:

-   green “Match” status — the object was no more than 100 meters from the saved gas station coordinates
    
-   red “No match” status — the object was more than 100 meters from the saved gas station coordinates
    

![](images/drex_otobrazhenie_azs_na_karte_custom_4.png)

If the gas station coordinates have not been added yet, PILOT cannot perform the check correctly. In this case, contact technical support and provide the station's address, latitude, and longitude.
