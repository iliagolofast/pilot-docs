---
title: "Fuel cards"
---

The “Fuel cards” module helps you account for fuel expenses and monitor fuel card operations.

With it, you can:

— add fuel cards and link them to objects

— view transactions and verify the correctness of operations

— configure notifications about charges

— load transaction data automatically or manually

![](images/drex_toplivnye_karty__custom.png)

The module consists of three tabs:

-   [“Cards”](karty.html) — adding and configuring fuel cards and linking them to objects
    
-   [“Transactions”](tranzaktsii.html) — viewing card operations: refuels, charges, and other received data
    
-   [“Services”](servisy.html) — connecting and configuring fuel network services for automatic transaction loading
