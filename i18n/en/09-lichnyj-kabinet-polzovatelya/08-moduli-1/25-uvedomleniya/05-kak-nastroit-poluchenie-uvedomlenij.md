---
title: "How to set up notification delivery"
---

To start receiving notifications:

1\. Go to “Contract settings” and open the [“Privacy”](../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/privatnost.html) tab

2\. Find the “Notification settings” section

![](images/drex_kak_nastroit_poluchenie_uvedomlenij_custom.png)

3\. Check the notification types you want to receive:

-   Speed
    
-   Refuels
    
-   Connection loss
    
-   Idle time
    
-   Violation control
    
-   Drivers
    

4\. For each selected notification type, choose how you want to receive it:

-   Email — notifications by email
    

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>To receive notifications by email, you need to <a href="../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/privatnost.html#Как_подтвердить_электронную_почту:_"><span>confirm</span></a> your email address.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>

-   Alert — a pop-up notification in the system
    
-   SMS — a message to your phone
    
-   Telegram — a message in Telegram
    
-   Push — a notification in the mobile app
    

5\. Click “Save”
