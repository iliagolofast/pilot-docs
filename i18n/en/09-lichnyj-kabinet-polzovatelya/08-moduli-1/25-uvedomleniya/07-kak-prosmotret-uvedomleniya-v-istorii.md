---
title: "How to view notifications in History"
---

In the “History” section you can see where and when notifications were triggered.

## How to enable notification display

To make notifications appear in “History”:

1\. Go to “Contract settings”, “[Map and geocoding](../../nastrojki-dogovora-2/karta-i-geokoding.html#отображение_событий)” tab

2\. Find the “Display events on the map” section

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_uvedomleniya_v_istorii_custom.png)

3\. Check the “Notifications” item

4\. Save the settings

## How to view notifications

1\. Open the “History” section

2\. Build a track for the period you need

3\. Go to the “Events” tab

The event list shows the notifications that were triggered during the selected time.

![How to view notifications in History](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_uvedomleniya_v_istorii_screen.png)

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_uvedomleniya_v_istorii_custom_2.png)

Click the notification you need in the list — a marker appears on the map showing the place of the event and information about it.

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_uvedomleniya_v_istorii_custom_3.png)
