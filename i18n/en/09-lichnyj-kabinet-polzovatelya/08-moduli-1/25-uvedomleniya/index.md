---
title: "Notifications"
---

A notification is an automatic message that the system sends when a certain event defined in the settings occurs.

Notifications let you receive information about events, for example:

— engine start or stop

— speeding

— fuel level change

— refuel or fuel drain

— entering or leaving a geofence

— loss of connection with the device

— the need for maintenance

With notifications you can respond to events quickly and keep track of the state of objects in the system.

The “Notifications” module lets you set up:

 — the conditions under which a notification is triggered

— the objects it applies to

— the delivery method, for example by email, SMS, Telegram, as a message in the system, or as a Push in the mobile app
