---
title: "How to create a trip"
---

A trip is the movement of a vehicle from the departure point to the destination along a set route and schedule.

1\. In the workspace, find the “Trips” block

2\. On the toolbar, click the icon![](images/drex_kak_sozdat_rejs_custom.png)

![](images/drex_kak_sozdat_rejs_custom_2.png)

3\. In the window that opens, specify:

![](images/drex_kak_sozdat_rejs_custom_3.png)

-   Trip name and number
    
-   Description
    
-   Check the box if the trip is bidirectional (the vehicle runs back and forth along the route)
    
-   Interval between trips (in minutes)
    
-   Ticket price (if required)
    
    4\. Add the stops you need to the trip — select them from the list of available stops and click the arrow
    
    5\. Click “Save” — the trip appears in the general list
    

![](images/drex_kak_sozdat_rejs_custom_4.png)

To edit a trip:

— right-click it and select “Edit” in the menu that appears

![](images/drex_kak_sozdat_rejs_custom_5.png)

— or double-click the trip in the list — a window opens where you can make changes and save them

![](images/drex_kak_sozdat_rejs_custom_6.png)

To delete a trip, select “Delete” in the same menu.
