The trip menu contains several tools. To open the menu:

— right-click the trip you need in the list

— or click the arrow next to the trip ![](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_custom.png)

![Trip menu](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_screen.png)

## Edit

Click this button in the menu to open the trip editing window.

![](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_custom_2.png)

In it you can:

-   change the trip name and number
    
-   update the description
    
-   set parameters: direction, interval, price
    
-   change the list of stops
    

After making changes, click “Save”.

## Schedule

A schedule is a timetable that shows what time a bus must be at each stop on a specific trip.

With this function you can add a new schedule or view existing timetables.

To learn how to add a schedule, see [this article](kak-rabotat-s-raspisaniem.html).

![](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_custom_3.png)

## Buses

This shows all buses linked to the selected trip.

![](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_custom_4.png)

To learn how to link a bus to a trip, read [here](kak-privyazat-avtobus-k-rejsu.html).

## Show all stops

Click this menu item and all stops of the route are displayed on the map. Click any stop on the map to see its name.

![](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_custom_5.png)

## Live view

If you select “Live view” in the menu, you will see the whole route with stops and the schedule in real time.

What the diagram shows:

-   Buses on the route — numbered icons show the actual buses currently running on the selected route
    
-   Arrival forecast — click a bus icon to find out when it will be at the next stop
    
-   Stops — marked with triangles
    
-   Arrows — show the direction of vehicle movement
    
-   Green and yellow lines — show the current position of buses on the route
    
-   Time scale — at the top and bottom of the diagram you can see when a bus is due to pass each stop
    

### ![](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_custom_6.png)

## Upload stops or a line for the route from KML

KML is a special file format for storing geographic data, such as points, lines, and areas on a map.

To upload stops or a route line from a KML file:

1\. Click the corresponding menu item

2\. Select the KML file you need on your device

3\. Click “Save”

The file is added to the route and displays the stops or the route line on the map, helping you set the geographic position of objects precisely.

## Calculate duration and route

After adding all stops to the route, click “Calculate route duration”. The system calculates the travel time between stops and shows them on the diagram in the “Live view” section.

![](images/drex_menyu_dlya_raboty_s_rejsami_custom_7.png)

Stops with arrival times appear in the live view only after the calculation — without it they are not displayed.
