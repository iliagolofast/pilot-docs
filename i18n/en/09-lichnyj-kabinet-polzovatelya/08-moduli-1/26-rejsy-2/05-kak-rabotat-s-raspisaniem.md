title: "How to work with schedules"
---

A schedule is a timetable that shows what time a bus must be at each stop on a specific trip.

### How to add a schedule:

-   Right-click the trip you need in the list and select “Schedule” in the menu that appears
    

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom.png)

2\. In the “Schedule” window, click ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_2.png) and enter the schedule name

![How to work with schedules](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_screen.png)

3\. Click ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_3.png) on the right to add a schedule table

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_4.png)

4\. In the window that opens, select the schedule type:

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_5.png)

-   Daily
    
-   By days of the week
    
-   For a specific date
    
    5\. Click “Create”
    

6\. The “Schedule” window shows the timetable for “Trip 1” with all stops. Set the bus arrival time at each stop

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_6.png)

Here you can also delete a trip or add a new one.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_7.png)

When adding a new trip, specify its start time.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_8.png)

If you check the “Continue” box, the system creates several consecutive trips with the specified interval (step) and departure time. If you leave the box unchecked, you can add and configure each trip manually, specifying its own departure time.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_9.png)

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_10.png)

7\. Fill in the schedule for each trip.

Use the “Import” and “Export” buttons to load schedules from files or export them.

### Schedule tools

Several functions are available in the “Schedule” window ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_11.png):

![How to work with schedules](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_screen_2.png)

 ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_12.png) — view the schedule history

 The schedule change history shows all periods when a given schedule was active for the trip: when and which schedule was assigned to the trip and until when it was used

![How to work with schedules](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_screen_3.png)

How to activate a schedule:

1\. Find the one you need in the schedule list

2\. Click the light gray check mark next to it  ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_raspisaniem_custom_13.png)— it turns bright gray

3\. This schedule becomes active and is now applied to the trip

4\. The system recalculates the route and timetable using the new schedule. This may take a few minutes

5\. After recalculation you can generate the [“Schedule adherence report”](../../otchyety-1/tipy-otchyetov/avtobusnye-linii/otchyet-po-soblyudeniyu-raspisanij.html)
