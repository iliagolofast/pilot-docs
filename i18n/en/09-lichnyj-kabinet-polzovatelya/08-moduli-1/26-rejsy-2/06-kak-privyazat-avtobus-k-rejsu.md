---
title: "How to link a bus to a trip"
---

For a bus to operate on the right route and appear in the schedule, it must be linked to a specific trip:

1\. Go to the “Online” tab

2\. Find the object you need and right-click it. In the menu that opens, select “Trips”.

![](images/drex_kak_privyazat_avtobus_k_rejsu_custom.png)

3\. In the window that opens:

![](images/drex_kak_privyazat_avtobus_k_rejsu_custom_2.png)

-   Check the trip you need
    
-   Specify the trip start date and time
    
    4\. Click “Save”
    

![](images/drex_kak_privyazat_avtobus_k_rejsu_custom_3.png)

Here you can also view the link history — click the “History” button to see active trips and link dates.

![How to link a bus to a trip](images/drex_kak_privyazat_avtobus_k_rejsu_screen.png)
