---
title: "Trips"
---

The “Trips” module helps you automate and simplify work with bus routes, schedules, and stops.

![](images/drex_rejsy_2_custom.png)

With it you can:

— create and edit trips

— add stops and manage them

— link buses to trips

— set up schedules

— monitor bus movement in real time

— get schedule adherence reports
