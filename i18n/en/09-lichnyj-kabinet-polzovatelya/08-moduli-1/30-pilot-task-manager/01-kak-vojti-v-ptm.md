title: "How to sign in to PTM"
---

To sign in to PILOT Task Manager:

-   Follow the link: [https://tasks.pilot-gps.com](https://tasks.pilot-gps.com/)
    

2\. Enter the login and password of your account

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_ptm_custom.png)

3\. If you want the system to remember your sign-in details, check the “Remember me” box — next time you will be signed in automatically

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_ptm_custom_2.png)

4\. If needed, change the interface language. To do this, click the language selection button ![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_ptm_custom_3.png) in the upper right corner and select the language you need ![How to sign in to PTM](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_ptm_screen.png)

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_ptm_custom_4.png)

5\. Click “Sign in” — you go to the electronic dispatch center

![](images/drex_kak_vojti_v_ptm_custom_5.png)
