In the “Acts” section you can view all acts created for completed tasks. An act is an official document confirming that the work on a task has been done.

![](images/drex_akty_custom.png)

### Filtering acts

To quickly find the act you need, use the “Search” bar — enter the task ID, act ID, name, address, or any other text related to the act.

![](images/drex_akty_custom_2.png)

You can also filter acts by period. To do this, select the date in the “From” and “To” fields. Then click “Set date filter” ![](images/drex_akty_custom_3.png). To reset the filter, click “Clear filter” ![](images/drex_akty_custom_4.png).

### Actions with acts

The main data is displayed for each act:

-   Unique number and name of the act
    
-   The address the act was issued for
    
-   Task name and type
    
-   The driver and vehicle that did the work
    
-   Start, acceptance, and completion times
    

On the right of each act row there are buttons for working with it:

![](images/drex_akty_custom_5.png)

 ![](images/drex_akty_custom_6.png)View act — opens the act for viewing

 ![](images/drex_akty_custom_7.png)Export act — save the act to your computer

 ![](images/drex_akty_custom_8.png)Print act — print the act for physical documentation

 ![](images/drex_akty_custom_9.png)Delete act record — delete the act if it is no longer needed
