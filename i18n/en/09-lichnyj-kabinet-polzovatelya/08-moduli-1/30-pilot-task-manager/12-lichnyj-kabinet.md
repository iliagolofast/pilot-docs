title: "User account"
---

When you select the “User account” section in the menu, you go to the user account.

# ![](images/drex_lichnyj_kabinet__custom.png)

In the user account you can view and edit your profile data:

-   Name
    
-   Email
    
-   Password
    
-   Date of birth
    
-   Phone numbers
    
-   Additional information: address, position, department, description
    

To change the data, click “Edit”, make the changes, and click “Save”. ![User account ](images/drex_lichnyj_kabinet__screen.png)

![User account ](images/drex_lichnyj_kabinet__screen_2.png)

You can also go to the user account by clicking the profile icon ![](images/drex_lichnyj_kabinet__custom_2.png) in the upper right corner of the top bar.

![User account ](images/drex_lichnyj_kabinet__screen_3.png)
