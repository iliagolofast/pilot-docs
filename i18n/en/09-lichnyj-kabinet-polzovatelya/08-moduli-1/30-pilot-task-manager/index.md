---
title: "PILOT Task Manager"
---

PILOT Task Manager (PTM) is a PILOT system module for working with tasks. It serves as an electronic dispatch center and task manager: here you create tasks, assign executors, and monitor their execution.

![](images/drex_pilot_task_manager_custom.png)

In PTM you can:

— create and assign tasks

— plan work by dates and routes

— monitor status and deadlines

— work with acts and the calendar

PTM is used by:

— dispatchers and logisticians — plan and distribute tasks

— drivers and executors — receive tasks and mark their completion

— managers — view reports and work results
