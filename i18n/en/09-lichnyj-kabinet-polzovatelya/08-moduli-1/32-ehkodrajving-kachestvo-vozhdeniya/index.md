---
title: "Eco-driving: driving quality"
---

The “Driving quality” module helps you assess drivers' driving style and identify dangerous or uneconomical maneuvers.

The system automatically analyzes data received from the device installed on the vehicle and records various events, for example:

— sharp acceleration

— harsh braking

— sharp turns

— speeding

— prolonged idling

— other events configured in the rules

For each recorded violation the system can assign penalty points. Based on this data, Eco-driving [reports](../../otchyety-1/tipy-otchyetov/kachestvo-vozhdeniya/index.html) are generated, which let you assess the driving quality of each driver or vehicle.

![](images/drex_ehkodrajving__kachestvo_vozhdeniya_custom.png)

Driving quality events are generated from data transmitted by the device installed on the vehicle. The available events depend on the device model and the parameters it supports.

The overall driving quality score is calculated taking into account the recorded events, the assigned penalty points, the selected penalty type, and the configured ECO weights.
