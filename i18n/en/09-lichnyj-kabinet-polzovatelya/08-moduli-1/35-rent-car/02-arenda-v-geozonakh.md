---
title: "Rental in Geofences"
---

-   Go to the Geofence module
    
-   Select a Geofence
    
-   Specify the type.
    

![](images/drex_arenda_v_geozonakh_custom.png)

-   **Name** - the displayed name of the type.
    
-   **Type** - (GREEN, BLUE, RED) - for display in the rental mobile app.
    
-   **Fine** - the fine for visiting this Geofence or staying in this Geofence longer than the idle limit.
    
-   **Idle limit** - the limit in minutes for this Geofence (for example, an airport drop-off zone)
    
-   **Office rental** - rental of office parking, where a vehicle may be located without a Contract
    
-   **Operating area** — a Geofence where a contract vehicle may operate (for example, a city, region or country).
