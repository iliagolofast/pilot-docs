---
title: "How to view fines"
---

After the module is connected, a purple fines indicator appears on the top panel of the User account. If the system finds new fines, the indicator shows their number.

![How to view fines](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_screen.png)

Click the purple indicator to open the “Fines” window.

![How to view fines](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_screen_2.png)

## List of fines

The window shows all fines found for the last 30 days.

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_custom.png)

The following information is available for each fine:

-   Vehicle — the Object the fine relates to
    
-   Driver — the Driver associated with the vehicle
    
-   Date — the date of the ruling
    
-   Status — the current state of the fine, for example Unpaid, Overdue or Discounted.
    
-   Ruling number — the number of the ruling
    
-   Fine — the fine amount
    
-   Pay by — the last day to pay
    
-   Discount until — the end date of the discounted payment period
    
-   Info — a description of the violation and additional information
    
-   Last update — the date and time the data was last received.
    

### How to work with the list

To view fines for a specific period, enter the dates in the “From date” and “To date” fields.

![How to view fines](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_screen_3.png)

To show only paid fines, check the “Show paid” box.

![How to view fines](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_screen_4.png)

By default, the “Fines” window opens in compact mode. If the violation description does not fit in the “Info” column, hover over the row content — the full information is displayed in a tooltip.

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_custom_2.png)

To open the window full screen, click the “Expand” button ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_custom_3.png) in the top right corner.

![How to view fines](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_shtrafy_screen_5.png)
