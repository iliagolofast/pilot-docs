title: "Fines"
---

The “Fines” module helps you track unpaid and overdue fines associated with vehicles and Drivers.

After the module is connected, fine data is received in PILOT automatically through the ELPAS service.

ELPAS is an external service that obtains fine data from government sources and passes it to PILOT. Once configured, the module automatically requests new data and displays the fines found in the system.

All fines are collected in one window, so you do not need to check each vehicle separately.
