title: "Modules"
---

Modules are separate components of the PILOT system, each responsible for specific functions.

Thanks to the modular organization, you can:

— configure the system for your company's specific tasks

— pay only for the features you really need

— add new modules depending on your needs

### How modules are connected

Modules are connected by the Contract administrator in the [Admin panel](../../panel-administratora/dogovor/moduli-2.html). Some modules have their own settings that must be activated correctly.

### Module Reports

When you enable a module, additional Reports appear to help you analyze operations.

You can find them in the [“Reports”](../otchyety-1/index.html) section.
