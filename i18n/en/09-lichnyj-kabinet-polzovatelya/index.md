---
title: "User account"
---

The User account in PILOT is the user interface for tracking and managing vehicles, machinery, people, premises and other assets.

### Who uses the User account:

-   Fleet managers
    
-   Heads of logistics departments
    
-   Business owners
    
-   Individuals tracking personal assets
    

The PILOT User account is a tool for monitoring Objects. You see where machinery, vehicles or employees are located, can track their movements and receive detailed Reports.
