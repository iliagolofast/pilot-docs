---
title: "Letter templates"
---

This section works for legal-entity Contracts that operate on prepayment.

By default, two types of letter templates about the end of funds are added: after 7 or 14 days.

In the template types you can edit and change the text as you wish, or keep the default Pilot templates.

How does it work?

All Contracts that are (Legal entity and Prepayment, Prepayment (lite)) are iterated, and if 7 or 14 days remain before the balance reaches 0, messages from the template are sent.
