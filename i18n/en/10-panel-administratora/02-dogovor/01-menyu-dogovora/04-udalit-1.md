title: "Delete"
---

A Contract can be deleted from the system only if it has no Objects.

<table><tbody><tr><td><span></span></td><td><div>If Objects remain on the Contract, first delete the Objects from the Contract through the&nbsp; user&nbsp; interface, and then&nbsp; delete the Contract through the Pilot system admin panel.</div><div>Objects are not deleted in the admin panel.</div></td></tr></tbody></table>
