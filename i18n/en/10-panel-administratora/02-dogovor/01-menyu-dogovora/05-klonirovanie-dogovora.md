---
title: "Cloning a Contract"
---

You can create a new Contract based on an existing one by copying the settings and data you need.

### How to clone a Contract

1\. Select the Contract you want to copy

2\. Right-click to open the Contract menu

3\. Click “Clone”

![](images/drex_klonirovanie_dogovora_custom.png)

4\. Enter the settings for the new Contract

![](images/drex_klonirovanie_dogovora_custom_2.png)

5\. Click “Save” — a window opens for choosing what to copy to the new Contract.

What can be copied:

-   Sensor templates — all Sensor settings and templates (fuel, temperature, doors, etc.)
    
-   Individual prices per Object — personal tariffs set for individual Objects of the Contract
    
-   Payment letter templates — texts and settings of e-mail Notifications (invoices, reminders, etc.)
    
-   User assignment — who is assigned to which Objects or clients
    
-   Additional fields — all custom fields
    
-   Users — rights and display settings of the Contract's Users
    
-   Reports from the Report builder — custom Reports created through the builder
    
-   Geofences — all zones associated with the Contract (base, warehouses, routes, etc.)
    

![](images/drex_klonirovanie_dogovora_custom_3.png)

6\. Select what to copy to the new Contract and click “Save”
