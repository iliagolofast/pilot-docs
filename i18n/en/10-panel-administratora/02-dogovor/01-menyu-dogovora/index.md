After a Contract is created, you can right-click it to open a context menu with the available actions.

![](images/drex_menyu_dogovora_custom.png)

### Main actions with a Contract

-   Users – opens a window with a list of all Users linked to the Contract
    
-   Edit – lets you change or add data in the Contract
    
-   Machines – opens the tab with the Objects assigned to the Contract
    
-   Video devices – shows the list of video devices connected to the Contract
    
-   Audit and audit history – lets you analyze in detail User actions on the Contract and Objects
    
-   Report list – a tab for activating Reports in the Contract; alternative names can be applied
    
-   Machines Excel report – download the list of machines in CSV format
    
-   Letter template – configure Notifications for clients (for example, 7 or 14 days before the balance runs out)
    
-   Email template (letter editor) – add and save letters by topic: blocking, password change, e-mail registration, etc.
    
-   Payment reminder – configure letter templates for monthly debt
    
-   Postpayment (lite) – configure messages about the blocking period under the Contract
    
-   Finance – view the history of payments and charges, add new transactions
    
-   Blockings – manage Object blockings
    
-   Delete – delete the Contract from the system
    
-   Clone – create a copy of the Contract without Objects and Notifications
    
-   Export templates to the Report builder — ready-made Reports can be bulk-uploaded through the admin panel. After uploading, the Reports become available to all Users of the Contract
