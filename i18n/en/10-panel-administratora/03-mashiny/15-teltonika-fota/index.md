# **Teltonika FOTA is a cloud service provided by Teltonika that lets you update the firmware and configuration of GPS trackers remotely. This tool simplifies the management of telematics devices, allowing you to perform the necessary updates from the office, without physical access to the devices.**

**Requests tab:**

The Requests tab lets you upload files.

The upload files for this service include firmware updates and configuration files, which can be uploaded and applied to devices remotely.
