title: "Finance"

The tab is used to monitor existing payments and add new ones.

![](images/drex_finansy_custom.png)

-   **ID -** the number of the unique payment.
    
-   **External ID -** the external identification of the payment.
    
-   **Contract -** the number of the Contract under which the payment was made.
    
-   **Amount -** the payment amount.
    
-   **Currency -** the currency in which the payment was made.
    
-   **Type -** the kind of payment.
    
-   **Description -** an additional description of the payment.
    
-   **Date -** the payment creation date.
    
-   **Receipt date -** the date the funds were received for payment.
    
-   **Organization -** the name of the organization that made the payment.
    
-   **Partner -** the person the client belongs to.
    
-   **Sub-partner –** the sub-partner the client belongs to (can be empty).
