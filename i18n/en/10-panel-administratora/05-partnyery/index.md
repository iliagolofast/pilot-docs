---
title: "Partners"
---

A Partner is an integrator or a company that uses the PILOT platform to work with its clients.

The Partner manages its client base within the system and is responsible for the connected Objects.

![](images/drex_partnyery_custom.png)

In this section of the admin panel you can:

— create and edit Partner agreements

— assign tariffs

— monitor the balance

— see the number of Contracts and connected Objects
