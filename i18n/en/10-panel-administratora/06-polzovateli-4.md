---
title: "Users"
---

The tab is intended for creating new administrators and editing existing ones.

 A Partner can create two types: admin user and mechanic (for access to the admin panel).

-   **ID -** the User's unique number in the system.
    
-   **Name -** the User's name.
    
-   **Full name -** the User's full name in the system.
    
-   **Last sign-in -** the time and date of the visit.
    
-   **Updated -** the update status in the system.
    
-   **Created -** the time and date of creation.
