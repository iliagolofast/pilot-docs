title: "QR codes"
---

The QR codes tab is used to register hardware kits in the monitoring system. 

-   **ID-** the unique number in the system.
    
-   **Agent id-** the unique hardware number.
    
-   **IMEI-** the imei of the hardware kit.
    
-   **Created-** the time and date it was entered into the database.
    
-   **Contract name-** the name of the Contract in the system.
    
-   **Contract id-** the unique Contract number.
    
-   **Activated-** the time and date of activation.
    
-   **Active-** the hardware operating status.
