**Etisalat SIM** — these are SIM cards and related services provided by Etisalat, one of the largest telecommunications operators in the Middle East, Africa and Asia.

**The Jasper platform** is a platform for controlling and managing M2M SIM cards. It lets you use various settings and get the full history of connections between hardware in real time. Data is provided in both numerical and graphical form. It is a single control center, available remotely at any convenient time.

As a reminder, M2M (machine-to-machine) is a solution designed to provide wireless data exchange between various devices (ATMs, terminals, security systems, sensors, etc.) and a single control center.

**Set the limit type.**

DEFAULT. The device cannot exceed the data limit.

TEMPORARY\_OVERRIDE. The device can use any amount of data until the end of the current billing cycle, after which the Control Center starts applying the data limit set in the tariff plan.

PERMANENT\_OVERRIDE. The device can use any amount of data, regardless of the data limit defined in the tariff plan.
