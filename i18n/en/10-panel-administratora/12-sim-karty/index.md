---
title: "SIM cards"
---

SIM cards in monitoring systems are used to provide communication between monitoring devices and the server. They play a key role in systems that require remote tracking and data transmission.

SIM cards are used in GPS trackers for vehicles to transmit location data in real time. This makes it possible to control routes, monitor schedule compliance and respond to deviations from the route.

SIM cards allow monitoring devices (for example, GPS trackers and Sensors) to connect to mobile networks to transmit location, status and other parameter data to the monitoring server.

This is especially important for devices that are in motion or in remote locations without access to Wi-Fi or wired networks.
