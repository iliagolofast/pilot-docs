---
title: "Points"
---

Original, unprocessed data collected directly from various sensors or devices.

Raw data can come, for example, from a temperature, humidity, pressure or speed sensor, and others.

Raw points are data in their original form, without any changes or processing.

Each raw point usually comes with a timestamp, which lets you track and analyze how parameters change over time.

-   **Time -** time the packet was created on the device.
    
-   **Unixtimestamp -** packet time in encoded format.
    
-   **Server time -** time the packet was received by the server.
    
-   **Latitude/Longitude/Altitude -** coordinates of the object.
    
-   **Speed -** speed of the object in km/h.
    
-   **Direction -** GPS compass heading relative to the object.
    
-   **Satellites -** number of satellites the object sees.
    
-   **TriggerData -** data from sensors (temperature, fuel, battery charge level, etc.).
