This tab lets you view existing news items and add new ones.

-   **ID -** unique number of the news item.
    
-   **Title -** name of the news item.
    
-   **Description -** name of the news item.
    
-   **Updated** - date the news item was last updated.
    
-   **Created -** date and time the news item was created.
    
    ![](images/drex_novosti___custom_2.png)
    
    To view news in the user interface, use the news view button.
