---
title: "Modules"
---

This tab is used to view and change access rights in PILOT.

-   **id -** unique number of the module in the system.
    
-   **Name -** name of the module.
    
-   **Description -** description of the module.
    
-   **Paid -** column showing the payment status of the module.
    
-   **Administrators only -** column showing the status of module usage rights.
