---
title: "Templates Editor"
---

The Templates Editor tab is used to create templates and display them in the user interface.

You can select the template type from the drop-down list in the Type field and add your own text.

For example, with the **Password change** type, when the customer clicks “Forgot password”, they see a message window configured through the admin panel.

Templates of these types do not support instant sending.

However, payment e-mails are sent to the address specified in the contract settings.
