Rebranding is a marketing strategy that includes a set of activities aimed at changing a brand. It may involve changing the name, logo, slogan or visual design. The goal of rebranding is to strengthen the brand, make it more unique, expand the target audience and attract new customers.

Enter the partner name and find it in the search; if it is not there, add it with +.

![](images/drex_dobavit_rebrending_custom.png)

Fill in the fields, select the color (theme) and upload the logos.
