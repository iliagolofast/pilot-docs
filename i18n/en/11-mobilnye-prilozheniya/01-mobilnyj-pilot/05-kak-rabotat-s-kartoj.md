After you log in to the app, you land in the “Map” section. The map shows all objects linked to your account.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom.png)

## Zooming

To zoom the map in or out, use the zoom buttons — ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_2.png) and ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_3.png) . When you tap them, the map zooms while the center point stays in place.

## Your location

Tap the ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_4.png) icon to make the map show your current position.

## Map settings

In the lower right corner you will find the map settings button ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_5.png). Tap it to show additional options.

## ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_6.png)

Here you can:

### Turn on traffic display

Move the switch to the on position.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_7.png)

The map shows road congestion using colors:

-   Green — free-flowing
    
-   Yellow — moderate traffic
    
-   Red — traffic jams and heavy congestion
    

### Select the map type

You can choose from several map types to display the area. The map type is the base layer of the map image that the monitoring system uses to display spatial data.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_8.png)

### Select the objects to display

Select the checkboxes for the objects you want to see on the map.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_9.png)

## Working with objects on the map

The map shows all your objects and their current locations.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom.png)

To open an object’s menu, tap its icon.

The available actions appear:

-   [Information and sensors](kak-rabotat-s-kartoj.html#инфо_и_датчики_в_мобпилот)
    
-   [Following an object](kak-rabotat-s-kartoj.html#следить_за_объектом)
    

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_10.png)

### Information and sensors

The information section has several tabs.

**“Info” tab**

The “Info” tab shows all the main information about the object.

The “Statistics” block shows indicators for the selected day: how long the vehicle was in motion, how many kilometers it traveled, its maximum and average speed, fuel consumption for the day in liters and average consumption per 100 km.

The “Sensors” block shows the current sensor values and the time of their last update.

“Additional information” shows the object’s main parameters: model, current status, time of last communication, speed, mileage.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_11.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_12.png)

The “Show on map” button opens a map with the object’s current location.

Below is the “Get directions” block – you can build a route right away:

-   In PILOT — open the route on the app’s map
    
-   On another map – open an external map service
    

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_13.png)

**“History” tab**

The “History” tab shows exactly how the object moved during the selected period.

![How to work with the map](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_screen.png)

The top of the screen shows a map with the object’s position and its track for the day.

Below the map is a short summary for the selected day: total mileage, maximum and average speed.

Just below is a date switcher — select the day you need, and the map, statistics and event list update for that date.

Next comes a detailed list of events for the selected day, in chronological order.

An event includes:

— start time

— event type (movement or parking)

— duration

— for movement events, the distance covered and the average or maximum speed

— the address where the movement started or ended, or the parking location

If you tap an event, the corresponding route appears on the map, and you can view it in the player.

Tap the three dots to open additional functions:

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_17.png)

-   Show on map — return to displaying the object
    

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_18.png)

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_19.png)

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_kartoj_custom_23.png)
