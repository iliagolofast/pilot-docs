---
title: "How to work with AI support"
---

AI support helps you get to grips with PILOT: it suggests where to find the function you need and explains the sequence of steps. For example, you can ask the assistant how to build a report, set up an object, create a geofence or connect a device. Answers are generated automatically and may contain links to the documentation.

### How to create a chat

1\. Go to the “Menu” section

2\. Open the “AI support” tab  
![How to work with AI support](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_ii_podderzhkoj_screen.png)

3\. Tap the plus icon in the upper right corner

![How to work with AI support](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_ii_podderzhkoj_screen_2.png)

4\. In the “Ask a question” field, enter your question  
![How to work with AI support](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_ii_podderzhkoj_screen_3.png) ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_ii_podderzhkoj_custom.png)

5\. Tap the send button

6\. Wait for the assistant’s answer

If the answer is long, scroll the chat to read it in full. After the answer, you can ask a follow-up question in the same chat.

## Chat history

All the chats you created are shown on the “AI support” tab. The chat name is based on the topic of the question or the creation date.

![How to work with AI support](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_ii_podderzhkoj_screen.png) ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_ii_podderzhkoj_custom_2.png)

Tap a chat to return to the conversation, view a previous answer or continue the discussion. To start a conversation on a different topic, tap the plus icon and create a new chat.
