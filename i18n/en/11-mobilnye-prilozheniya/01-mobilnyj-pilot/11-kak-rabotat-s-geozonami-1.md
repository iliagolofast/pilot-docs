title: "How to work with geofences"
---

When the [“Geofences”](../../lichnyj-kabinet-polzovatelya/moduli-1/geozony-2/index.html) module is connected to your account, you can work with geofences in the mobile app.

A geofence is an area on the map that you create to track object movement inside or outside it.

A “Geofences” tab appears in the “Menu” section. Here you can choose which zones to display on the map. Just select the checkboxes next to the geofences you need.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_2.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_3.png)

 Go to the “Map” section, and you will see all the selected geofences on the map. You can track object movement within these zones.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_4.png)

The [“Map settings”](kak-rabotat-s-kartoj.html#Настройки_карты) also let you turn geofence display on or off.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_5.png)

## How to create a geofence

1\. Tap “Add” ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_6.png)

2\. Specify the parameters:

-   Geofence name
    
-   The group it will belong to (or create a new group)
    

     ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_7.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_8.png)

3\. Set the geofence shape:

 ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_9.png)Circle — tap the map and drag to set the radius (you can change the radius by dragging the edge)

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_10.png)Polygon — mark points on the map one by one to set the boundaries

    ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_8.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_11.png)

4\. Select the geofence color

   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_12.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_13.png)

If needed, tap “Clear” ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_14.png) to undo your changes.

5\. Tap “Save” — the geofence appears in the list

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_geozonami_1_custom_15.png)
