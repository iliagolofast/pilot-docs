---
title: "How to work with drivers"
---

When the [“Drivers”](../../lichnyj-kabinet-polzovatelya/moduli-1/voditeli-3/index.html) module is connected to your account, you can work with driver information in the mobile app.

A “Drivers” tab appears in the “Menu” section. Tap it to see the list of all drivers in your account.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_voditelyami_2_custom.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_voditelyami_2_custom_2.png)  

In the driver list, select the driver you need to open their card. The card shows the driver’s main information:

-   Driver’s name and photo (if uploaded)
    
-   The group the driver belongs to
    
-   Status or license number
    
-   E-mail address
    
-   Additional information
    
-   Driver’s contact number.
    
-   Vehicles the driver is assigned to
    

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_voditelyami_2_custom_2.png)     ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_voditelyami_2_custom_3.png)

To find out which drivers are assigned to a specific object, go to the objects section. Tap the object menu ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_voditelyami_2_custom_4.png) to open additional actions for the object, and select the “Drivers” tab.

    **![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_voditelyami_2_custom_5.png)**![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_voditelyami_2_custom_6.png)
