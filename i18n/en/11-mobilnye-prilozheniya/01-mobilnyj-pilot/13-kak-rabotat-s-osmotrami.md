---
title: "How to work with inspections"
---

When the [“Service”](../../lichnyj-kabinet-polzovatelya/moduli-1/servis/index.html) module is connected to your account, you can work with inspections in the mobile app — a tool for checking the condition of vehicles.

With inspections you can:

— carry out inspections on vehicles

— record what was checked and the condition of the vehicle

— track the history of all inspections

An “Inspections” tab appears in the “Menu” section. Go to it, and you will see all completed inspections by object.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_2.png)

Use the “Search” field to find a specific inspection. You can also use the filters ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_3.png):

— by period — select the inspection dates

— by objects — select the objects that were inspected

— by inspections — select a specific inspection name

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_4.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_5.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_6.png)

Tap the inspection you need in the list to open the completed inspection form with questions and answers. To view all questions and results, tap ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_7.png) . To exit the inspection, tap “Exit inspection”.

 ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_2.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_8.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_9.png)

### How to carry out an inspection

-   Tap the ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_10.png) button in the upper right corner of the screen
    

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_2.png)

2\. Select the vehicle you are going to inspect

To find a vehicle, use the “Search” field or the filters ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_3.png) by vehicle model or year of manufacture.

 ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_11.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_12.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_13.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_14.png)

3\. Select the inspection type

Inspection forms with questions are created in the web version and can be filled in in the mobile app. To learn how to create an inspection form, read [here](../../lichnyj-kabinet-polzovatelya/moduli-1/servis/osmotry.html).

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_15.png)

4\. Answer the questions

Answer all the questions in the inspection form. If necessary, attach photos to confirm the condition of the vehicle.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_16.png)

5\. Add a signature

If a signature is required, sign in the designated field. If you didn’t manage to sign the first time, use the eraser ![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_17.png) to clear it and try again.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_18.png)

5\. Complete the inspection

When you have answered all the questions and signed, tap “Next”. The inspection is completed and saved.

![](images/drex_kak_rabotat_s_osmotrami_custom_19.png)
