---
title: "How to view video"
---

If the [“Video”](../../lichnyj-kabinet-polzovatelya/moduli-1/video-i-cms/index.html) module is connected to your account and video devices are installed on your objects, you can view the video recorded by these devices.

### Viewing the video stream

1\. In the “Objects” section, select an object with a connected camera

2\. Open the object menu and go to the “Video” tab

![How to view video](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_screen.png)![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom.png)

3\. On this tab you will see previews of all connected cameras

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_2.png)

4\. Tap the camera you need to go to the video player

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_3.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_4.png)

### Playback controls

In the player you can:

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_5.png) Switch between cameras using the arrows

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_6.png) Turn camera sound on and off

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_7.png) Take screenshots

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_8.png) Expand the video to full screen

### Viewing video history

-   To view video history, tap the icon in the upper right corner of the player ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_9.png)
    

![How to view video](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_screen_2.png)

2\. In “History”, tap ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_10.png) and select the date and time to filter recordings. Then tap “Apply”

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_11.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_12.png)

The history player shows the date and current time of the frame. By moving the slider, you can quickly jump to the moment of the recording you need.

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_11.png)

When viewing video history, you can:

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_7.png) Take screenshots

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_13.png) Change the playback speed

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_6.png) Turn sound on or off

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_1_custom_8.png) Expand the video to full screen
