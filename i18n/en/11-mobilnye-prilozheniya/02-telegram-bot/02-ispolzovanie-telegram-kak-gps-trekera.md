---
title: "Using Telegram as a GPS tracker"
---

The Telegram bot can work as a virtual tracker. The bot creates a special ID that is used as the tracker number.

When you turn on location sharing in Telegram, the monitoring system receives coordinates from this ID — just as it does from a regular GPS tracker.

## How to set up the Telegram tracker

1.

### Get the ID

1) Log in to the chatbot

2) Open Advanced Menu to open the extended menu

  ![Using Telegram as a GPS tracker](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_screen.png)

3) Tap “Get ID”

   ![Using Telegram as a GPS tracker](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_screen_2.png)

### 2\. Create an object in PILOT

1) In your user account, create a new object

2) Open the object card → “Main” tab

![Using Telegram as a GPS tracker](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_screen_3.png)

3) Enter the vehicle name or number

4) In the “Device ID” field, paste the ID you received in the bot

### 3\. Turn on location sharing in Telegram

1) Open the chat with the bot

2) Tap the paperclip ![](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_custom_2.png)

                ![](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_custom_3.png)

3) Select “Location”

![Using Telegram as a GPS tracker](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_screen_4.png)

4) Allow access on your phone

                 ![](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_custom_4.png)

5) Tap “Share live location”

                ![](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_custom_5.png)

6) Select how long to share the location

![](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_custom_6.png)               

7) Tap “Share”

Your phone now works as a tracker.

A message with your current location appears in the chat. It has a location icon — tap it, and Telegram opens a map where you can see the point showing your position.

    ![](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_custom_7.png)    ![](images/drex_ispolzovanie_telegram_kak_gps_trekera_custom_8.png)

In PILOT, the object moves along with you — its coordinates are updated from your phone’s location.
