### How to install the PILOT. Driver app

1\. Open Google Play or the App Store on your smartphone

2\. In the search bar, enter PILOT Driver or go to the app store using the link:

[Go to Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pilot.dispatcher) 

[Go to App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pilot-driver/id1582462032)

3\. Tap “Install” and wait for the download and installation to finish

4\. After installation, tap “Open” or find the PILOT. Driver icon on your smartphone screen and launch the app

### How to log in to the app

After you launch the app, on the login screen:

1\. Enter your account login

If needed, leave the “Remember me” option on so the app remembers your details

![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie__custom.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie__custom_2.png)

2\. Enter your password

![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie__custom_3.png)

3\. Tap “Next”

After logging in, you land on the main screen with new tasks.
