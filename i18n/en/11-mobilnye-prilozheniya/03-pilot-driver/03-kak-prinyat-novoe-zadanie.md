---
title: "How to accept a new task"
---

When a new task is assigned to you, it appears on the “New” tab.

To accept a task:

1\. In the bottom menu, open the “New” tab

![](images/drex_kak_prinyat_novoe_zadanie_custom.png)

2\. Find the task you need in the list and tap its card

![](images/drex_kak_prinyat_novoe_zadanie_custom_2.png)

3\. Review the task information: start and end times, route, point addresses and other details.

4\. If you are ready to start the task, tap the “Accept task” button.

![How to accept a new task](images/drex_kak_prinyat_novoe_zadanie_screen.png)

After you accept it, the task automatically moves to the [“Active”](aktivnye-zadaniya.html) tab, where you can track its progress and work with route points.
