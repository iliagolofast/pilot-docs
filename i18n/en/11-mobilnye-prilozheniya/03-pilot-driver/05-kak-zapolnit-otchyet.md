---
title: "How to fill in a report"
---

If a report is set up for a task or route point, a “Fill in report” button appears in the app.

A report can be linked to:

— an individual route point

— the whole task

This depends on the settings specified by the operator in the [web version of the system](../../lichnyj-kabinet-polzovatelya/moduli-1/pilot-task-manager/spravochniki-2.html#Как_создать_акт_выполненных_работ_для_шаблона:_).

The composition and number of fields in a report may vary. What data must be filled in is determined by the report form settings.

## How to open a report

1\. Open an active task

![](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__custom.png)     ![](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__custom_2.png)  

2\. If the report is linked to a route point, go to the point you need and tap “Arrived”

![How to fill in a report ](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__screen.png)

3\. Tap the “Fill in report” button

![How to fill in a report ](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__screen_2.png)

The report form then opens.

![](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__custom_3.png)

Fields marked with the (\*) symbol are required. Until the required fields are filled in, you cannot complete the report.

## Filling in a report

Depending on the form settings, a report may contain various types of fields, for example:

-   text fields
    
-   numeric values
    
-   selection lists
    
-   switches
    
-   comment and signature fields
    
-   photos and files
    

Fill in all the required data according to your organization’s requirements.

### Adding photos

If the form provides for photo capture, tap “Add photo”.

    ![](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__custom_3.png)    ![](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__custom_4.png)

In the window that opens, select one of the options:

-   Go to camera — take a photo with the device camera;
    
-   Choose photo from gallery — attach an existing image.
    

You can add several photos if needed.

## How to complete a report

1\. Make sure all required fields are filled in

  ![](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__custom_5.png)

2\. Check that the data you entered and the attachments you added are correct

3\. Tap the “Complete” button at the bottom of the screen

![How to fill in a report ](images/drex_kak_zapolnit_otchyet__screen_3.png)

After successful completion, the report is saved and sent to the system.
