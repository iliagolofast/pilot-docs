---
title: "Route map"
---

In the upper right corner of a new, active or completed task card there is a map icon.

Tap it to open the route map.

![Route map](images/drex_karta_marshruta_screen.png)

The map shows:

-   purple line — the travel route
    
-   numbered markers — route points
    

![](images/drex_karta_marshruta_custom.png)

The bottom of the screen also shows the waybill, so you can view the route on the map and the list of points at the same time.

### Map settings

To open the settings, tap the gear icon in the upper right corner of the map screen.

![Route map](images/drex_karta_marshruta_screen_2.png)![](images/drex_karta_marshruta_custom_2.png)

### Map layer

In this section, you can select the source of map data.

![](images/drex_karta_marshruta_custom_3.png)

For example:

-   OpenStreetMap
    
-   2GIS
    
-   Google Map
    
-   Google Satellite
    
-   Yandex Map
    
-   satellite maps and other available options.
    
    Choose the map view that is most convenient for you.
    

### Show points

The “Show points” switch controls the display of route points on the map.

-   On — route points are displayed on the map
    

![](images/drex_karta_marshruta_custom_4.png)

-   Off — points are hidden, and only the route line is displayed.
    

![](images/drex_karta_marshruta_custom_5.png)

### Map controls

Additional control buttons are available on the map

![](images/drex_karta_marshruta_custom_6.png) — zoom the map in or out

![](images/drex_karta_marshruta_custom_7.png) — show your current location on the map
