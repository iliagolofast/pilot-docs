---
title: "Account settings"
---

On the “Settings” tab, you can view information about your account, change the interface language and app theme, log out or delete your account.

## How to open settings

1\. Open the app

2\. In the bottom menu, tap the “Settings” icon

![](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_custom.png)

The screen shows:

— user name

— e-mail address

— “Language” section

— “Theme” section

— “Delete account” button

— logout button in the upper right corner of the screen

### Logging out

To log out of your account:

1\. Open the “Settings” section

2\. Tap the logout button in the upper right corner of the screen

![Account settings](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_screen.png)

3\. Confirm the action

After you log out, the login screen opens. To continue working, you need to log in to the account again.

### Changing the app language

To change the interface language:

1\. Open the “Settings” section

2\. Tap “Language”

![Account settings](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_screen_2.png)

3\. Select the language you need from the list

![](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_custom_2.png)

4\. Tap “Change language”

After you confirm, the interface language is changed.

### Changing the theme

To change the app theme:

1\. Open the “Settings” section

2\. Tap “Theme”

![Account settings](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_screen_3.png)

3\. Select one of the available options:

![](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_custom_3.png)

-   Light
    
-   Dark
    
-   Same as system — the app uses the theme set on the device
    

The selected theme is applied immediately after you select it.

### Deleting an account

To delete your account:

1\. Open the “Settings” section

2\. Tap “Delete account”

![Account settings](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_screen_4.png)

3\. In the confirmation window, select:

-   Cancel — to cancel the deletion
    
-   Delete — to confirm deletion of the account
    

![](images/drex_nastrojki_akkaunta_custom_4.png)
