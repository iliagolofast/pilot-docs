title: "PILOT. Driver"
---

PILOT Driver is a mobile app for drivers, couriers and delivery staff. It works together with [PILOT Task Manager (PTM)](../../lichnyj-kabinet-polzovatelya/moduli-1/pilot-task-manager/index.html) and helps manage tasks and routes.

Tasks for drivers are created in the PTM web version and then passed to the PILOT. Driver mobile version, where drivers can view and carry them out.

![](images/drex_pilot__driver_custom.png)

With PILOT. Driver, drivers can carry out various types of tasks, for example:

-   Cargo delivery — tasks for delivering goods, materials or construction materials, for example, delivering goods, collecting and removing waste or delivering concrete
    
-   Taxi and tourism — for taxi drivers and drivers who work with tourists, PTM lets you manage transport orders
    
-   Waste collection — drivers can work on waste collection routes, marking each route point as they complete the task
    

### How PTM works:

1\. The driver receives a task with the route points to visit

2\. While carrying out the task, the driver marks each route point as completed, confirming delivery or another job

3\. After all route points have been visited, the driver marks the task as completed and, if necessary, sends a report on its completion
