## How to install the app

1\. Open Google Play or the App Store on your smartphone

2\. In the search bar, enter PILOT Guarda or go to the app store using the link:

[Go to Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.octys.pilotguardo&hl=ru&pli=1) 

[Go to App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pilot-guarda/id6755179014)

3\. Install the app

4\. After installation, tap “Open” or find the Pilot Guarda icon on your smartphone screen and launch the app

## How to log in to the app

After you launch the app, on the login screen:

1\. Enter your account login and password

![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom.png)  

2\. If needed, leave the “Remember me” option on so the app remembers your details

![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_2.png)

3\. Tap “Log in”

After you log in, the “My cars” tab opens, where you can select the car you need and go to the screen with summary information about its condition.

   ![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_3.png)![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_4.png)

## Login settings

On the login screen, in the upper right corner, there is an icon ![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_5.png) . Tap it to open “Login settings”.

Here you can change the server. Enter the address of the server you need and save the changes with the “Accept” button.

![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_2.png)  ![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_6.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_7.png)

## Registration by QR code

The app lets you register using the device QR code, which already contains the object data.

1\. On the login screen, tap “Scan QR code”.

2\. Point your smartphone camera at the QR code (on the device or in PILOT). Make sure the code fits in the frame on the screen — the app reads it automatically.

![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_2.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_ustanovit_i_vojti_v_prilozhenie_pilot_guarda_custom_8.png)

2\. Select an action:

-   Log in — if you already have an account in PILOT. In this case, the device is linked to your existing account automatically.
    
-   Register — if you do not have an account. The app offers to create a new account, and the device is added to the new account.
    

Registering a new user:

1\. Fill in the details:

— Email

— phone number

— password

— password confirmation

 Tap “Continue”.

2\. Confirm your e-mail

A confirmation code is sent to the e-mail address you specified. Enter the code on the confirmation screen. If the message does not arrive, you can request it again after the timer ends. Tap “Confirm”.

After successful confirmation, you are logged in to the app automatically, and the device scanned via the QR code is linked to your account.
