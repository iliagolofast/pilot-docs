---
title: "How to view history"
---

The “History” tab shows information about the car’s movements and events for the selected period.

Here you can see where the car was, how it moved and what events took place.

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom.png)

## Viewing a trip route

To open a trip route:

1\. Select the movement track you need in the list

2\. Tap the route display icon

The route with detailed information then appears on the map.

  ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom.png)  ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_2.png)

To view the movement, use the route player — it shows how the car moved along the route.

   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_2.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_3.png)

## Events in history

In addition to routes, history shows events.

Tap an event in the list to see details — a point with information appears on the map.

Examples of events:

-   Parking — the car was stationary
    

      ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_4.png)

-   Alarm — a sensor or alert was triggered
    

     ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_5.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_6.png)

-   Arming / disarming
    

## History filters

Filters help you quickly find the events you need.

To set up a filter:

1\. Open the filter settings ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_7.png)

2\. Select the parameters you need:

-   Event type — for example, route, parking, alarm
    
-   Time period
    

3\. After setting up the filter, tap “Apply”.

The list will show only the events that match the selected parameters.

      ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_8.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_istoriyu_custom_9.png)
