---
title: "How to view video"
---

If video devices are installed in the car, you can view the video recorded by these devices.

## Viewing the video stream

Go to the “Video” tab in the app menu — the video players of the connected cameras are displayed.

   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom.png)![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_2.png)

In the video player you can:

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_3.png) Stop viewing

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_4.png) Turn camera sound on and off

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_5.png) Take screenshots

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_6.png) Expand the video to full screen

## Viewing video history

1\. In the player, tap the icon in the upper right corner ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_7.png)

2\. A list of recordings opens in chronological order

3\. To open a recording, tap the row you need or the play button next to it ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_8.png)

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_9.png)   ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_10.png)

While viewing a recording, the following functions are available:

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_11.png)  Play / ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_12.png) Pause — start or pause playback

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_13.png) Stop — end viewing

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_14.png) Seek — move through the recording

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_15.png) Screenshot — capture a frame from the video

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_16.png) Sound — adjust the volume (if the recording contains audio)

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_17.png) Playback speed — you can slow down or speed up the recording

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_18.png) Full-screen mode — expand the player to full screen

## How to download video

Tap the download button

### ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_19.png)

next to the recording you need in the history list.

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_9.png)

To filter recordings:

1\. Tap the filter ![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_20.png)

2\. Select the date and time

3\. Tap “Apply”

![](images/drex_kak_prosmotret_video_2_custom_21.png)
