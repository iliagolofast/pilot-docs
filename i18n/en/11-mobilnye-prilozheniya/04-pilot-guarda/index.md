---
title: "PILOT Guarda"
---

PILOT Guarda is an app for vehicle security and monitoring. It helps you monitor the condition of a car, manage access to it and track its movements. The app is suitable for both personal use and fleet management.

![](images/drex_pilot_guarda_custom.png)

With the app you can:

— Monitor the car’s condition

View the current status, charge, events and other parameters

— Manage access

Open and close the car without using keys

— Track movements

See the current location and trip history

### Ways to control

The car can be accessed:

-   Via GSM (mobile network) — from any distance
    
-   Via Bluetooth — when you are near the car
