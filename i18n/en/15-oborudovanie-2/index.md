---
title: "Hardware"
---

This section contains information about the hardware that you can connect and use with the PILOT platform.

It includes a list of supported hardware with the make, model, device type and connection method — from GPS trackers and IoT devices to mobile video recorders, security panels and tachographs.

The section also provides instructions for connecting and configuring hardware: how to take the first steps, what to pay attention to during installation and which features to consider in operation. These materials will help you understand a device and prepare it properly for use.
